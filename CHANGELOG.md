# Changelog

What changed in each version of THE ROOTS, for players and testers. Every pull request that merges into `develop` adds its line under the `Unreleased` heading below; a release turns that section into the version's heading and publishes it with the tag. The process is in [docs/process/GITFLOW.md](docs/process/GITFLOW.md#changelog).

## Unreleased

### Added
- Breaking a crate has a 30% chance to drop a key, a heart or a small bomb (#6).
- A laboratory with an alembic on floors 2, 4, 6 and 8: feed it up to three things from your hand, a positive intention brews (three brews per run), a negative one boils the bulb off (#20).
- The alembic leaves a flask on its top after a brew. Drink it with G, X or the flask button: a Plain Tonic restores half a heart, a Restoring Draught one heart. Two flesh brew a Restoring Draught; anything else brews a Plain Tonic (#20).
- Six more potions brew at the alembic: Footing Tincture, Spring Tonic, Clear Infusion, Questioning Tincture, Ember Tonic and Patient Draught, each from its own parts and blocks, lasting a few sealed rooms, one lock or the floor; ice in the bulb adds a room (#20).

### Fixed
- Open chest lids no longer clip through the chest body or its padlock (#4).
- Locked doors open when you touch them, not from across the room (#5).

### Changed
- The blade practice is named **Cut Ties with Toxic Relationships**; its description covers substances, food and technology too (#7).
- The Listener stays silent until you bring it a heart of a released pattern; the Gardener's key is a flesh. The part is taken when you touch the presence with it on top of your hand.
