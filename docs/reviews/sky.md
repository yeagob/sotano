# Review: the sky realm, Totality and the endgame (2026-11-03)

Proposal: [../architecture/SKY.md](../architecture/SKY.md) (engineer, at `20b1d54`). Engineer: the main session. Architect and mediator: review agents ([sotano-docs](../../.claude/skills/sotano-docs/SKILL.md) §4). Line numbers are those of `src/game/game.ts` at `20b1d54`; every reference below was checked against the code by the mediator.

## Owner decisions taken as binding

Made in chat before the ruling; they override the proposal where they differ.

1. The sky is a separate realm above the crown, generated by formula, storing only its exceptions. The seven built stories of the giant tree stay as the climb to it.
2. Build up to Totality, ending at a closed Sun threshold. Unity is issue #95 (a different game) and is not built.
3. The endgame is the Spiked Entity encounter, its revelation, a staircase up through every floor and a permanent unlock. No rematch.
4. Open design questions take the proposals written in the docs, marked provisional.
5. The permanent shortcut staircase is on **floor 0, the roots** (the start room's area), not floor 1.
6. The Spiked Entity waits in **the silence chamber**. Silence still carries the player to heaven.
7. The way up between sky floors opens **only when that floor's deity is won**.
8. **No damage in the sky.** The player never dies there; deity attacks, hindrances and minions only move, slow or hold the player; the cost of a mistake is lost height. This replaces the proposal's 2 points of harm.
9. The Spanish revelation is a faithful translation of the English, marked for the owner to confirm.

For the other open points of the proposal's §19 the owner takes the engineer's proposal, marked provisional: the koala and the horse teleport; the Triple Jump has no forward boost and full air steering; the Sun's warm light is a stated exception to art pillar 2 (a provisional ledger entry); a knocked-down tower falls as loose cubes; a positive shot removes a player block with no cube back; the unlock is forgotten through a console command only; rising past the top of the dungeon's sky from outside the tree also opens the realm.

## Verdict of the architect

The core is right: a formula world with exceptions, a field swap behind a narrow `VoxelSpace`, a dedicated creature system, fresh `fork('sky', …)` paths. Not ready to build because of three blockers (the sky still reaches dungeon-only systems, weapons cannot touch sky creatures, the package graph has cycles) and fourteen major points.

## Resolution
