# Procrastination

Status: built.

## What it is

Putting things off. It is slow and sleepy, a blob of three crooked crates. Hit it and the work splits: the tasks pile up.

## Where it appears

Floors 2 to 5. Code: [procrastination.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/procrastination.ts). This file also holds its two splits, [Procrastination B](PROCRASTINATION-B.md) and [Procrastination C](PROCRASTINATION-C.md).

## How it moves and attacks

- It walks slowly and pauses for a second every 2.5 seconds.
- Its touch does 3 damage.
- Every hit splits it: it shares its remaining health between two smaller Laters, each of which splits the same way once more. It is two splits deep, and its smallest forms are fast.

## How the player answers it

Each hit makes more of it, so a fight with Procrastination is a fight with its splits. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage. Its splits are the real danger when a room is crowded.

None. The card's fire behaviour is an open question ([OPEN-QUESTIONS](../../not-built/projections/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md)).
