# Second-Guessing

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The second form of the doubt pattern: "Wait, are you really sure?" It asks again, and holds on longer.

## Where it appears

Floors 3 to 7. Code: [second-guessing.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/second-guessing.ts). It is the second form of [Doubt](DOUBT.md).

## How it moves and attacks

- It walks at you, quickly, and pauses for 0.8 seconds every 1.2 seconds.
- Within 1.5 blocks it reaches for you for half a second, then presses. Its touch does 4 damage.
- Within 2.2 blocks it lunges.

## How the player answers it

Its pauses are the windows. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

- **Water:** it keeps out of water when it can, and walks at 35 per cent speed in it.
