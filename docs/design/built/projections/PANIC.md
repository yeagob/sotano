# Panic

Status: built.

## What it is

Anxiety spiralling into panic: an alarm that rings when nothing is on fire. It is small and dark, all head, with its captive light showing through hairline cracks.

## Where it appears

All floors. Code: [panic.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/panic.ts). The roster adds a second entry from floor 3, so it is more common from floor 3 on. Its card gives floors 1 to 4 ([OPEN-QUESTIONS](../../not-built/projections/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md)).

## How it moves and attacks

- It rushes at you, fast.
- Within 2.2 blocks it swells over 0.9 seconds, in three steps (the last one white), and then bursts. The burst reaches 2.2 blocks.
- Its touch does no damage. Its burst is its attack, and the burst is announced by the swell.

## How the player answers it

Stay outside its burst radius while it swells. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage, and a kill releases it. A burst that breaks blocks often opens a way forward.

## Element affinities

- **Water:** calms it for 2.5 seconds, even mid-swell: it drifts and its light dims. It crosses water.
- **Fire and lava:** it swells at once (0.3 seconds) and keeps out of them.
