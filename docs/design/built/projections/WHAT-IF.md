# What If

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of regret: "What if I had…" It drifts toward you through the blocks and keeps the past close.

## Where it appears

Floor 9. Code: [what-if.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/what-if.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It hovers a little above the floor and drifts toward you slowly, passing through solid blocks.
- Every four seconds it fires a bolt at you. A bolt stops at the first solid block.
- Its touch does 4 damage. It has 34 health.

## How the player answers it

Cover does not stop it, only its bolts. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

None.
