# Scarcity

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of hoarding: "There will not be enough." It runs for what is on the floor and keeps it.

## Where it appears

Floors 3 and 4. Code: [scarcity.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/scarcity.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It drifts, and runs from you when you come within about seven blocks.
- It goes for the nearest pickup in its room, and when it is within 1.2 blocks of one it takes it. While it carries something it keeps running.
- It does not touch.

## How the player answers it

Kill it to get back what it took. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage. When it dies it drops everything it carried, plus one more of those pickups chosen at random.

## Element affinities

None.
