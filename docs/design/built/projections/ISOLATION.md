# Isolation

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of withdrawing: "Leave me alone." It builds walls so that no one can reach it.

## Where it appears

Floor 9. Code: [isolation.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/isolation.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It stands still. Its touch does 4 damage. It has 72 health.
- Its first shot, after about three seconds, raises one wall of ice across the middle of the room, two blocks high. It does this once per fight.
- The ice is ordinary breakable ice.

## How the player answers it

Break the wall or go around it. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

None.
