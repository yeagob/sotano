# Resentment

Status: built.

## What it is

The grudge: "I will not forget." It holds on to every hurt, and it grows the longer it is left unhurt.

## Where it appears

Floors 3 and 4. Code: [resentment.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/resentment.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It walks at you, slowly, and its touch does 4 damage.
- Every second it leaves a pool on the floor under it: a slowing field of about 0.8 blocks that lasts five seconds. Standing in a pool slows you.
- Every six seconds that it is not hit, it grows: its health and its maximum rise by 20 per cent. It stops growing at twice its base health. Any hit resets the six-second count.

## How the player answers it

Hit it early, while it is small. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage. Keep out of the pools it leaves behind you.

## Element affinities

None.
