# Backlog

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The later form of the putting-off pattern: "I will get to all of it. Later." A stack that is always about to topple.

## Where it appears

Floors 5 to 9. Code: [backlog.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/backlog.ts). It is the later form of [Procrastination](PROCRASTINATION.md).

## How it moves and attacks

- It walks slowly, and pauses for a second every three seconds.
- Its touch does 4 damage.
- Every three seconds, while it has a target, it throws a crate at you. The crate flies in an arc and stops at the first solid block.

## How the player answers it

Keep out of the crates' arc, and use blocks as cover. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

None.
