# Not Yet

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of "Not yet. Not yet. Not yet." It keeps mending itself toward a finish it never reaches.

## Where it appears

Floors 6 to 9. Code: [not-yet.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/not-yet.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It walks at you, slowly. Its touch does 4 damage.
- It is armoured: it takes 30 per cent of the damage of any hit.
- When it has not been hit for four seconds, it heals two health every four seconds.

## How the player answers it

Keep the pressure on, so that it does not get four seconds without a hit. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage. Its armour applies after either.

## Element affinities

None.
