# Burnout

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of running past empty: "I cannot stop." It burns faster the longer it runs, and then it has to stop.

## Where it appears

Floors 7 and 8. Code: [burnout.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/burnout.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It walks at you. Its speed rises with time, to about 2.2 times its base speed after eight seconds of running.
- After those eight seconds it is spent for three seconds. While spent it stands still and leaves a slowing field of about 0.9 blocks where it stands.
- It then walks again, at 80 per cent of its base speed.
- Its touch does 4 damage. It has 26 health.

## How the player answers it

Its speed grows with time, so do not let the fight run long. The spent period is the moment to strike. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

- **Fire:** it crosses fire without harm.
- **Lava:** it keeps out of lava.
