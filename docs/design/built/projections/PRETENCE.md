# Pretence

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of the mask: "I am fine." It smiles so that no one asks.

## Where it appears

Floors 7 and 8. Code: [pretence.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/pretence.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It walks at you, quickly, and lunges when you come close.
- Its touch does 6 damage, the highest contact damage of the built and partial projections. It has 16 health.
- It is always visible: its disguise is in the code, but nothing starts it, so it never hides.

## How the player answers it

It comes straight at you, so intentions and blocks are the answer. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

None.
