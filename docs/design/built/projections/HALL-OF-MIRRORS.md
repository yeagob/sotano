# The Hall of Mirrors

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of being behind: "Everyone is ahead of you." Its shots come from more than one place at once.

## Where it appears

Floors 7 to 9. Code: [hall-of-mirrors.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/hall-of-mirrors.ts). It is the third form of [Comparison](COMPARISON.md).

## How it moves and attacks

- It stands still and does not touch.
- Every 2.6 seconds it fires three bolts toward you, from three points beside its body (4 damage each). A bolt stops at the first solid block.
- It has 44 health. Its budget cost is five, the highest on the roster.

## How the player answers it

It stands still, so it is a fixed target. Keep out of the lines of its bolts, and use blocks as cover. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

None.
