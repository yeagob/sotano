# The Tribunal

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of consensus: "We have all agreed." It is one body with three posts, and the posts speak in turn.

## Where it appears

Floors 6 to 9. Code: [tribunal.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/tribunal.ts). It is the third form of [Inner Critic](INNER-CRITIC.md).

## How it moves and attacks

- It stands still and does not touch.
- Every 1.2 seconds it fires a bolt (4 damage) from one of three posts, two and a half blocks apart, in turn: left, centre, right. A bolt stops at the first solid block.
- It has 72 health.

## How the player answers it

Its volleys sweep across the room, so use blocks as cover and step between the posts' lines. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

None.
