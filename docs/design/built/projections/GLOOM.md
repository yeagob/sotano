# Gloom

Status: built.

## What it is

A heavy mood: "Everything is grey." It hangs high and does not come down.

## Where it appears

Floors 3 and 4. Code: [gloom.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/gloom.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It hovers about three blocks above the place where it appeared. It does not come down and does not move on its own.
- Every 0.7 seconds it throws a cold drop at a random point one to six blocks around your head. The drop does 4 damage when it lands on you and stops at the first solid block.
- It never touches you.

## How the player answers it

Keep moving: its drops follow you around the room. Reach it with intentions from a height, from a gallery, or by building up toward it. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

None.
