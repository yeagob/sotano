# Procrastination B

Status: built.

## What it is

The middle split of [Procrastination](PROCRASTINATION.md): a smaller Later with nine health. It is the work you put off in two pieces.

## Where it appears

Only as a split of Procrastination. The roster never rolls it. Code: [procrastination.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/procrastination.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It walks at a faster pace than Procrastination, and pauses for a second every 2.5 seconds.
- Its touch does 3 damage.
- Every hit splits it into two of the smallest form, [Procrastination C](PROCRASTINATION-C.md).

## How the player answers it

Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage. Its health is small enough to finish in a few hits, but each hit adds one more piece.

## Element affinities

None.
