# Dread

Status: built.

## What it is

The third form of the alarm: "Something will go wrong." It stands still and sends rings out across the room.

## Where it appears

Floors 6 to 9. Code: [dread.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/dread.ts). It is the third form of [Panic](PANIC.md).

## How it moves and attacks

- It stands still and does not touch.
- Every three seconds, after a 0.8-second lead, it sends a ring out from its body. The ring does 8 damage, is 1.4 blocks high, and grows across the room, to about 4.6 blocks, in 0.8 seconds.
- It has 40 health.

## How the player answers it

The ring is announced by its lead. Stay out of its path or let it pass over you; its height is 1.4 blocks. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

None.
