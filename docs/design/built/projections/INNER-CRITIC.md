# Inner Critic

Status: built. Its health differs from the card (open question).

## What it is

The judging voice. It wants you to be accepted, so it judges you first. It is a tall, pale stack of ruled pages on a post, with a mask of two slits, a quill pointing at you, and an ✗ on its back. It has no legs.

## Where it appears

All floors: the roster gives it no range. Code: [critic.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/critic.ts). Its card gives floors 1 to 4; see [OPEN-QUESTIONS](../../not-built/projections/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md).

## How it moves and attacks

- It keeps about six blocks from you. It backs off when you come within five, approaches when you are beyond eight, and strafes in between.
- Every 2.4 seconds, when you are within twelve blocks, it winds up for 0.6 seconds and lobs a judgement at your head. The judgement does 4 damage and stops at the first solid block.
- Its touch does 4 damage.

## How the player answers it

Good intentions do half damage and warm it. Bad intentions do full damage. A block placed in the arc of a judgement stops it.

## Element affinities

- **Fire:** takes double harm and keeps out of fire.
- **Lava:** takes double harm and keeps out of lava.
- **Water:** walks at 45 per cent speed in water and crosses it.
