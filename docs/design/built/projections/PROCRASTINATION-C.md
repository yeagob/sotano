# Procrastination C

Status: built.

## What it is

The smallest split of [Procrastination](PROCRASTINATION.md): a fast Later with four health.

## Where it appears

Only as a split of [Procrastination B](PROCRASTINATION-B.md). The roster never rolls it. Code: [procrastination.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/procrastination.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It is the fastest form of Procrastination, and it pauses for a second every 2.5 seconds.
- Its touch does 3 damage.
- It does not split.

## How the player answers it

It dies to a few hits. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

None.
