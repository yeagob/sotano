# The Margin

Status: built.

## What it is

The second form of the judging pattern: "And another thing…" It stays where it is and marks the ground under you.

## Where it appears

Floors 3 to 6. Code: [margin.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/margin.ts). It is the second form of [Inner Critic](INNER-CRITIC.md).

## How it moves and attacks

- It stands still and does not touch.
- Every three seconds, after a half-second lead, it marks the spot under you: a slowing field of about 1.2 blocks that stays for eight seconds. It also fires a shot at you.
- Its shot stops at the first solid block.

## How the player answers it

Step off a mark: the mark slows you while you stand in it. It stands still, so it is an easy target from a distance. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

- **Water:** keeps out of water when it can.
