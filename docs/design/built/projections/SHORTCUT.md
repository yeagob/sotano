# Shortcut

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of cutting corners: "There is an easier way." It runs from you, and it does not touch you.

## Where it appears

Floors 7 and 8. Code: [shortcut.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/shortcut.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It flees when you come within about five blocks, and otherwise drifts.
- When you come within four blocks, every four seconds it swings a short ring: 6 damage, announced for 0.6 seconds, growing to about two blocks across.
- It does not touch. It has 18 health.

## How the player answers it

It is fragile: a few hits end it. Keep out of the ring's reach when you close in. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

None.
