# Doubt

Status: built.

## What it is

The pattern of self-doubt. It wants you not to fail, so it keeps you still, because still is safe. It is a medium, dark figure hunched over a sack of stones it drags, its head hooked forward, its forearms crossed over its chest, and two cold eyes that cannot hold your gaze.

## Where it appears

All floors: the roster gives it no range. Code: [doubt.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/doubt.ts). Its card gives floors 1 to 4; the difference is an open question ([OPEN-QUESTIONS](../../not-built/projections/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md)).

## How it moves and attacks

- It walks at you. Every 1.4 seconds it stops for half a second to reconsider. That pause is the window to move or act.
- Within 1.5 blocks it reaches for you for half a second (a visible pulse), then it presses. Its touch does 4 damage.
- Within 2.2 blocks it lunges, moving faster.
- It breaks the softest blocks in its way.

## How the player answers it

Good intentions do half damage and warm it; at full warmth it turns to your side. Bad intentions do full damage, and a kill releases it. Its pauses are the moments to throw.

## Element affinities

- **Water:** it keeps out of water when it can, and walks at 35 per cent speed in it.
