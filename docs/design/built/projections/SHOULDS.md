# The Shoulds

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of rigid rules: "I should… I should…" It fires along the lines it has drawn.

## Where it appears

Floors 5 and 6. Code: [shoulds.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/shoulds.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It stands still and does not touch. It has 16 health.
- Every 2.2 seconds it fires a bolt (4 damage) along the axis on which you are farther from it, so its shots run straight across the room.
- A bolt stops at the first solid block.

## How the player answers it

Stay off its axis, or put a block between you. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

None.
