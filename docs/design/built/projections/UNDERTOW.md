# Undertow

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of being overwhelmed: "It is too much." It walks in water and sends its water across the room.

## Where it appears

Floors 3 and 4. Code: [undertow.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/undertow.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It walks at you, slowly. In water it keeps to the water cells and moves faster there.
- Its touch does 4 damage.
- Every four seconds it sends a wave: a wall of 8 damage that crosses the room from one side to the other, announced by a lead of 1.2 seconds. A wave hurts whoever it reaches. It does not push you.

## How the player answers it

Move out of the wave's path. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

- **Water:** it keeps to water cells, and moves at 1.5 times its speed in water.
