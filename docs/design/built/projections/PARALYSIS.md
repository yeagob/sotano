# Paralysis

Status: built.

## What it is

The third form of the doubt pattern: "Do not move." It roots itself where it stands and holds you with a tether.

## Where it appears

Floors 6 to 9. Code: [paralysis.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/paralysis.ts). It is the third form of [Doubt](DOUBT.md).

## How it moves and attacks

- It does not walk and does not touch.
- Every three seconds, after a 0.6-second lead, it tethers: a pull of seven blocks drags you toward it for 2.5 seconds, and a slowing field of about 2.2 blocks on the same spot slows you.
- It has 50 health.

## How the player answers it

It stands still, so you can choose the moment. While you are in its pull, you are dragged back, so get out of its radius before you throw. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

None.
