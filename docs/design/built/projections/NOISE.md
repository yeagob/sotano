# Noise

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of too many small things: "Ooh! And that! And that!" It is one fast, tiny flitter here.

## Where it appears

Floors 4 to 9. Code: [noise.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/noise.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It flits around its target at about three blocks, changing direction now and then, at a fast pace.
- Its touch does 2 damage.
- It has four health.

## How the player answers it

It is quick and small: time your intentions to its passes. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

None.
