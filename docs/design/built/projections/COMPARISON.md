# Comparison

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of measuring yourself against others: "They are ahead of you." It keeps a step ahead and shoots.

## Where it appears

Floors 3 to 6. Code: [comparison.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/comparison.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It keeps between four and six blocks from you, strafing in between.
- Every two seconds it fires a bolt at you (4 damage). A bolt stops at the first solid block.
- It does not touch. It has 30 health.

## How the player answers it

Use blocks as cover from its bolts. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

- **Water:** keeps out of water when it can, and walks at 40 per cent speed in it.
