# Rumination

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of the thought that loops: "Again. Again. Again." It goes round and round.

## Where it appears

Floors 3 and 4. Code: [rumination.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/rumination.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It moves through solid blocks, as a ghost, toward you, at a slow pace.
- Every four seconds it fires a line twelve blocks long toward you, after a lead of 0.9 seconds. The line is live for 0.4 seconds and does 6 damage.
- Its touch does 4 damage.

## How the player answers it

Step off the line's path before it fires. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

None.
