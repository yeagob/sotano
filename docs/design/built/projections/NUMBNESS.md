# Numbness

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of not feeling anything: "I do not feel anything." It is slow, and it holds things still.

## Where it appears

Floor 9. Code: [numbness.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/numbness.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It walks slowly at you. Its touch does 4 damage.
- Every three seconds it leaves a slowing field of about 2.5 blocks, where it stands, for three seconds.
- In water it crosses at 70 per cent speed. It has 60 health.

## How the player answers it

Keep out of its field, and keep moving: its pace is slow. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

- **Water:** crosses water at 70 per cent speed.
