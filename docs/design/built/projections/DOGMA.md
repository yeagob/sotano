# Dogma

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of a belief set in stone: "It has always been this way." It is a monolith that sets its rules around itself.

## Where it appears

Floors 5 and 6, rare (weight one). Code: [dogma.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/dogma.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It stands still and does not touch. It has 60 health and armour 0.2: it takes 80 per cent of the damage of a hit.
- Every five seconds it sets a field of three blocks around itself for four seconds. The field's effect depends on its health: above half, it mutes your casting; between a quarter and a half, it slows you; below a quarter, it stings you for 2 damage each second.

## How the player answers it

Keep out of its field, and watch the effect change as its health falls. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

None.
