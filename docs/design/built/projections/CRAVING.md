# Craving

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of wanting that never fills: "Just one more." It circles at a distance and pulls.

## Where it appears

Floors 1 and 2. Code: [craving.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/craving.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It keeps between about 2.2 and 4.5 blocks from you, circling.
- Every four seconds it opens a wind of radius five blocks for 2.5 seconds. The wind drags players and other actors toward it, and it swells in rather than clicking on. Loose cubes, bombs and pickups are not pulled.
- Its touch does 3 damage.

## How the player answers it

Leave the wind's radius before it opens. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

None.
