# Perfectionism

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of "It has to be perfect." It is armoured in plates, and a crack in one plate is not the end of it.

## Where it appears

Floors 3 to 6. Code: [perfectionism.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/perfectionism.ts). It is the earlier form of [Not Yet](NOT-YET.md).

## How it moves and attacks

- It walks at you, slowly, and its touch does 4 damage.
- Its shell has six plates. While a plate stands, damage breaks the plate and does not reach its body; the next plate takes the rest. Only when all six are broken does the body take damage. Its body has 28 health.

## How the player answers it

Break the plates first, then the body. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage, and a kill releases it.

## Element affinities

None.
