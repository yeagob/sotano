# Sulk

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of sullenness: "Fine." It is hurt and does not say so, and it never chases.

## Where it appears

Floors 5 and 6. Code: [sulk.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/sulk.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It stays where it is, in water, and does not move.
- Every four seconds, when you are within three blocks of it, it sends a slowing field of about 2.5 blocks that lasts three seconds. Standing in the field slows you.
- It does not touch. It has 36 health.

## How the player answers it

Stay beyond three blocks and it will not reach you. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

- **Water:** it keeps to water cells (it dwells there), and walks at half speed in them.
