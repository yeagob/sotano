# Reactivity

Status: built.

## What it is

Reacting in anger: "How dare you!" It charges when you line up with it on an axis.

## Where it appears

Floors 5 and 6. Code: [reactivity.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/reactivity.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It walks toward you.
- When you are within nine blocks and lined up with it on an axis, it aims along that axis for 0.7 seconds, drawn as a dashed line on the floor, then charges at high speed for up to 1.8 seconds.
- Its touch does 4 damage. It breaks the softer blocks in its path.
- When it hits something hard, such as a placed block, stone or a wall, it stands still and is stunned for 1.5 seconds. That is the window.
- After a stun it waits about 1.2 seconds before it can charge again.

## How the player answers it

Stay off its axes, and stand where a placed block is between you. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage. The stun is the moment to strike.

## Element affinities

None.
