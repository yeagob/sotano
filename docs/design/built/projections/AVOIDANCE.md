# Avoidance

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of looking away from what hurts: "Not now." It stays out of sight and peeks out only now and then.

## Where it appears

Floors 1 and 2. Code: [avoidance.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/avoidance.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It does not move and does not touch.
- It stays shut for three seconds: out of sight, and not hittable.
- When you come within seven blocks, it peeks out for 1.2 seconds and throws a shot at you, then shuts again.

## How the player answers it

It can be hit only while it is peeking. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage. Wait for the peek.

## Element affinities

None.
