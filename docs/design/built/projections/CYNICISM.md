# Cynicism

Status: partial. This document holds the built part only. The rest of the card is in [GAPS](../../not-built/projections/GAPS.md).

## What it is

The pattern of giving up before trying: "What is the point?" It holds still and backs away from anyone who comes close.

## Where it appears

Floor 9. Code: [cynicism.ts](../../../../src/content/enemies/cynicism.ts).

## How it moves and attacks

- It stands still. When you come within five blocks, it backs away from you.
- Every four seconds it opens a ring of three blocks around itself for four seconds. While you stand in the ring you cannot cast. The mute lasts 0.4 seconds after you leave it.
- It does not touch. It has 26 health.

## How the player answers it

Stay out of the ring and cast from outside it. Good intentions do half damage and warm it; bad intentions do full damage.

## Element affinities

None.
