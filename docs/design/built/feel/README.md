# Feel (built)

Up: [built design](../README.md)

What the player sees, hears and feels, as built: the HUD and its screens, the sounds, the feedback that keeps the game gentle, the art pass, and the languages. The art rules are not restated here. The authority is the art guide and its ledger, and this folder says what the build does against them.

| Document | Contents |
|---|---|
| [HUD.md](HUD.md) | What the HUD shows: hearts, breath, counters, hands, the polarity mark, the minimap, the boss bar, banners, toasts, the toll, the screen, the death screen |
| [AUDIO.md](AUDIO.md) | The synthesised sounds: about sixty cues, the spatial layer for omens and breaches, and no music track |
| [FEEDBACK.md](FEEDBACK.md) | The gentleness rules: flash budget, the beat of the last heart, steady invulnerable hearts, the hurt vignette, shake, rumble, and the lesson tablets |
| [ART.md](ART.md) | The built art pass: colour tokens, materials, cracks of light, projections, hands, the slate HUD, and the gallery view |
| [LANGUAGES.md](LANGUAGES.md) | English and Spanish, how the language is chosen, and the message catalogues |

The art rules themselves are in [docs/art/ART-GUIDE.md](../../../art/ART-GUIDE.md) and the binding decisions in [docs/art/DECISIONS.md](../../../art/DECISIONS.md). What is not built yet is in [not-built/feel](../../not-built/feel/README.md).
