# Mechanics and metaphor

Up: [built story](README.md)

Each mechanic the game has now, and what it stands for. The metaphor is the reason for the rule, and the rule is what the player feels. Built means the mechanic works in the game today; a mechanic that is only designed is not in this table.

The mechanics live in the world code under `src/`, and the floors and rooms are described in the levels documents. This document says what they mean.

| Mechanic | What it stands for | How the player meets it |
|---|---|---|
| **Intentions** (the shot) | Focused will: attention plus purpose | A bead of light flies straight while the focus holds, then falls. Intentions are good or bad (see [VOCABULARY](VOCABULARY.md#polarity)) |
| **Breaking blocks** | Dismantling a limiting structure: a belief, a habit, an avoidance | Some blocks break with one hit; some take sustained effort. With enough intention, everything breaks |
| **Block tiers** | How deep a limitation runs | Dirt is a passing excuse; obsidian is a core belief from childhood |
| **Loose cubes** you can carry and place | What you dismantle becomes material to build with | Broken blocks become cubes you can pick up, place and throw. "The impediment to action advances action. What stands in the way becomes the way." (Marcus Aurelius, public domain) |
| **Carry limit** | Working memory, emotional bandwidth | You carry one block in your hand. Second Brain and Letters from a Stoic carry more |
| **Kicking and throwing** | Letting go; using momentum | Not everything needs to be carried. A kicked block hurts projections, and a heavy one hurts more |
| **Going down** | Going inward: introspection, shadow work | The floors get deeper and harder. The circles' names show on the HUD |
| **The silence and the tree** | Integration: what the depths taught reaches up into daily life | Standing still in the silence under the ninth circle carries you up to heaven; the top of the sky brings you back down. The giant tree rises above the roots |
| **Keys** | Insights | Found by breaking through something. They open locked doors, which were locked to you until then |
| **Bombs** | Breakthroughs | Rare and powerful. They clear a lot at once and can hurt you if you are careless |
| **Hearts** | Resilience, self-worth | Lost when projections hit you. Practices and care restore them, and some practices add a heart container |
| **Doors** | Opportunities respond to movement and to what you have learned | A locked door needs a key. A room with projections seals its doors until the projections are gone |
| **Treasure rooms** | Moments of learning | A practice waits on a pedestal. Its door is a treasure door, and the vault room holds the richer pedestals |
| **Bosses** | Core blocks: the big, old patterns behind the small ones | Each boss is built from the same blocks as everything else, so it can be dismantled piece by piece. Their arenas are in [built bosses](../bosses/README.md) |
| **The roots** | The earth, where you begin | Floor 0 has every door open, and the first lessons are carved into the stone walls as tablets, never as an overlay |
| **The way down asks a price** | Leaving something behind is how the path opens | To go down you give something: a heart of damage, a key, a bomb, a block or a monster part from your hand, or, from the sixth level on, a practice. The toll window lists what you carry and you choose exactly what is asked. The price grows with the floor, and it never traps a player who has played fair, because a heart can always be given |

## Notes

- The toll is the metaphor of the way down, and its numbers are in the levels documents.
- Projections rise from the matter of their room, each one after an omen. The rules of a fight's opening are in the [bestiary](README.md) (design).
- Not built, and not in this table: hidden pockets and side biomes (the Noita layer). They are in [META-PROGRESSION](../../not-built/story/META-PROGRESSION.md).
