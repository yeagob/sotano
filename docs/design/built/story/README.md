# Story (built)

Up: [built design](../README.md)

The story of The Roots as the game does it now: the premise, what each mechanic stands for, the words the player reads, the naming rules, and the four paths. Every claim here is true in the code, and it is what the other documents in this branch build on. What is not built, and the open questions of the story, live in [not-built/story](../../not-built/story/README.md).

The naming rules and the story text win on names and tone; the mechanics documents win on numbers and behaviour ([sotano-design](../../../../.claude/skills/sotano-design/SKILL.md), section 5).
