# Premise

Up: [built story](README.md)

A person goes down into the basement of their own mind. Down there, reality bends to intention: what you direct your will at changes. The whole game is a metaphor for unlocking inner potential through intention, and the metaphor is fixed here so that mechanics, names and art all point the same way.

## The person

Every item is a practice: something people have used to grow, such as a book, a course, a technique or a therapy. Picking one up changes not only what you can do but who you are. The protagonist is not a fixed character who collects gear. The person moves along four paths as they collect practices, and at three of one path they reach a state of being ([PATHS](PATHS.md), [states of being](../practices/states-of-being/README.md)). The protagonist is faceless and gender-neutral by default; the open question of showing them is in [PROTAGONIST](../../not-built/story/PROTAGONIST.md).

## Every wall, every creature

Every wall is something you once built to stay safe and that now keeps you small. Every creature that stands in the way is a pattern that blocks you from going after what you want. Those patterns are called projections. They are never people and never diagnoses: a projection is the voice of doubt, not a person with anxiety ([NAMING](NAMING.md)).

## The way down

The way down is the work. The roots are the first layer, where every door is open and the first lessons are carved into the walls. Each circle below is older, darker and harder than the one above, until the frozen bottom and the silence. Going down is going inward: the deeper you go, the older and harder the material.

## The tree

Above the roots, a giant tree rises into the sky. The depths and the tree are one journey seen from its two ends: what the depths taught reaches up into daily life, and the deepest point and the highest one meet. The tree's meaning is open in [THE-SKY-MEANING](../../not-built/story/THE-SKY-MEANING.md), and the cosmology behind both ends is in [COSMOLOGY](../../../lore/COSMOLOGY.md).

## The skeleton

The descent and the return have the shape of the classic hero's journey, and the image of going down into the basement echoes the descent into the unconscious. Neither is named in the game. They are the skeleton of the story, and the game does not quote them.
