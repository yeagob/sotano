# Totality (built)

Up: [built levels](../README.md) · the sky above the crown of the giant tree, where the climb of the sky ends.

Totality is the last stretch of the sky: three strata above the crown, small platforms over a long climb, the Earth below and the Sun above. What is built is the sealed threshold, where the climb ends in this build. The rest of Totality's design (the climb's pace, the strata's look, the fall and the updraft) is still in [not-built levels](../../../not-built/levels/TOTALITY.md).
