# The canopy (the leaves)

Up: [giant tree](README.md) · Status: built.

The canopy is the crown of the giant tree: a wide shell of leaves, held up by limbs of bough, over a floor of old bark giving way to bough that you can stand on, with patches of young leaves through the old (see [BARK](BARK.md)). The HUD names this zone "the leaves". The owner's line for it is a star map: the star map belongs to the crown (see [STAR-MAP](STAR-MAP.md)), and the crown holds the tree's one room, the Leaves (see [LEAVES](LEAVES.md)).

## What the player meets

- **A crown.** The leaves form a wide, flat shell, with gaps through it. The crown spreads far beyond the trunk.
- **Limbs.** Five limbs of bough run out from the crown's centre, twenty-six to thirty-six blocks long, rising a little as they go.
- **A floor.** Beneath the crown, a disc of old bark and bough spreads out to about thirty blocks from the centre, its gaps set by a random pattern. You can walk on it, and jump from one part to another.
- **The Leaves.** High in the crown, beside the stair that climbs to the stars, a hall of leaves with a ring of starstone and an open eye in its dome (see [LEAVES](LEAVES.md)).
- **Leaves burn.** Like all leaves, the crown burns when fire touches it (see [FIRE](../../elements/FIRE.md)).

Code: `src/world/tree.ts` (the canopy, its limbs, its disc and the Leaves), `src/content/materials.ts` (leaves and living wood).
