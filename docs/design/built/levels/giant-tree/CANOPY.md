# The canopy (the leaves)

Up: [giant tree](README.md) · Status: built.

The canopy is the crown of the giant tree: a wide shell of leaves, held up by limbs of living wood, over a floor of living wood that you can stand on. The HUD names this zone "the leaves". The owner's line for it is a star map, but the build keeps the star map for the stars (see [STARS](STARS.md)); the canopy is a place you walk under and over.

## What the player meets

- **A crown.** The leaves form a wide, flat shell, with gaps through it. The crown spreads far beyond the trunk.
- **Limbs.** Five limbs of living wood run out from the crown's centre, twenty-six to thirty-six blocks long, rising a little as they go.
- **A floor.** Beneath the crown, a disc of living wood spreads out to about thirty blocks from the centre, its gaps set by a random pattern. You can walk on it, and jump from one part to another.
- **Leaves burn.** Like all leaves, the crown burns when fire touches it (see [FIRE](../../elements/FIRE.md)).

Code: `src/world/tree.ts` (the canopy, its limbs and its disc), `src/content/materials.ts` (leaves and living wood).
