# The branches

Up: [giant tree](README.md) · Status: built.

The branches are the story that leaves the trunk. The owner's line for it is a three-dimensional map: platforms and hollows in every direction, joined by branches. The build grows seven to nine limbs of living wood from the trunk, each one rising as it leaves.

## What the player meets

- **Limbs.** Each limb leaves the trunk through a tunnel cut in the wall, at a height of two to five blocks above the story's floor, and runs outward, climbing a block for every four it travels.
- **Tunnels.** Where a limb leaves the trunk, the air is cut out above it, so you can walk into the limb from inside the trunk.
- **Tips.** Each limb ends in a platform of living wood, with a rounded ball of leaves above it, the place where you stand and see out.
- **Walking out.** A limb's own blocks are the path out to its tip, and the tip is the end of it.

The branches are the zone the HUD names "the branches", and the sky starts to turn to night here.

Code: `src/world/tree.ts` (the branches, their tunnels, the tips), `src/game/game.ts` (the zone name, the night sky from the branches up).
