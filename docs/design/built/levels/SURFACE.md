# The surface and its mountains (built)

Up: [levels](README.md) · the grass round the giant tree, and the ring of mountains that closes it (issue #10).

You come out of the roots onto a wide plain of grass. The giant tree stands in its middle. Round the plain, a ring of mountains rises as high as the tree: grassy foothills first, then cliffs of bare stone, and a peak on each ridge that stands exactly as tall as the tree's crown. The world no longer ends in a square of grass hanging in the sky: it ends in mountains.

## What you find

- **The plain.** Flat grass out to about 76 blocks from the trunk's axis, all round. The dungeon's own ground (the floors' footprint) lies inside it, so the grass simply goes on past where it used to stop.
- **The ridges.** Three ridges make the ring. Their crest stands at about 136 blocks from the axis and half the tree's height on average; each ridge has one peak as tall as the tree (146 blocks above the grass). The walls are rugged: they rise in steps of one, two and three blocks, never as a smooth face.
- **The trails.** Each ridge has one trail: a strip of worn earth, three blocks wide, that climbs one block at a time from the plain to a shoulder of the crest (48 blocks up). Jumping up it is enough. Anywhere else the walls are steeper: you build your way up, or find another way.
- **The passes.** Three flat passes, about eight blocks wide, cut through the ring between the ridges. They lead to a far plain behind the mountains, where other areas will open later. Past the far plain there is no ground yet.
- **What it is made of.** Grass on gentle ground, bare stone on steep or high ground, earth on the trails. The same blocks as the rest of the world.
- **What you cannot do yet.** The mountains cannot be broken or built on: blocks you aim at them are refused, and a blast leaves them whole. Inside the old ground everything works as before.

Every run has its own mountains: the passes, the peaks and the trails are drawn from the run's seed, so the same seed gives the same ring.

## Not verified, and open

- **Art gate: owner review needed.** The mountains use only existing blocks and palette tokens, but nobody has looked at them in the game yet (built and tested headless). Their read at distance and in the surface's fog, the grass and stone on the slopes, and what they mean to the story need the owner's eye before the art gate is passed.
- **What lies past the passes**, and whether the far edge should be hidden or grown into new areas.
- **Whether the mountains should become diggable** like the rest of the world.
