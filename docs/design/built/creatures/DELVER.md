# Delver

Built. Floors 1 to 3, as ambient life. Code: `src/content/creatures/delver.ts`.

A Delver is a small digger with a cap of dirt on its back. It is play: it wanders the cellar floors as if it had a hole to get to. In the build it only wanders. Its tunnels and the digging for the player are not built.

- **Pattern:** play.
- **Health:** 5.
- **Answers to:** both polarities.

## Movement and character

- Walks about the floor, changing direction every one to three seconds.
- Stays two to five blocks from the player, comes closer when far and steps back when near.
- Its dirt cap and two glowing eyes are the only details; its body is living.
- One or two appear per floor from 1 to 3.

## What the player can do

- Watch it. Its presence is a sign of soft ground, in the design; in the build it is only a sign of life.
- Harm it: it hides until the room clears.
- Heal it: a positive intention heals it.

## Open questions

- **Tunnels.** The design has it make small tunnels and pop out of them, and dig a block ahead of a befriended player. Not built: see [Burrowing](../../not-built/creatures/BURROWING.md).
- **Befriending.** Not built. See [Befriending](../../not-built/creatures/BEFRIENDING.md).
