# Cinderwalker

Built. Floors 7 and 8, the Furnace, as ambient life. Code: `src/content/creatures/cinderwalker.ts`.

A Cinderwalker is a small creature with a stone-grey top and dark sides. It walks through fire and lava without being hurt. It is the Furnace's answer to the fire: a creature that is not consumed.

- **Pattern:** walking through fire without being consumed.
- **Health:** 6.
- **Answers to:** both polarities.

## Movement and character

- Walks across the floor and across lava and fire. Both are harmless to it.
- Changes direction every one to three seconds, and stays two to five blocks from the player.
- A stone-grey top plate and two glowing eyes.
- One or two appear per floor on 7 and 8.

## What the player can do

- Watch it cross the lava. It is a sign that lava can be walked across, which is a fact of the Furnace.
- Harm it: it hides until the room clears.
- Heal it: a positive intention heals it.

## Open questions

- **Cooled path.** The design has the lava cool to stone behind it for a few seconds, making a path the player can follow. Not built: see [Crossing fire](../../not-built/creatures/CROSSING-FIRE.md).
- **Harm.** The Cinderwalker takes no harm from fire or lava. Is that the right rule, or should the Furnace's own fire hurt it when the room is burning?
