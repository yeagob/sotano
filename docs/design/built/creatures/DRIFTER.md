# Drifter

Built. Floors 5 and 6, the Ledger Halls, as ambient life. Code: `src/content/creatures/drifter.ts`.

A Drifter is a flat creature that floats in the air of tall rooms, a pale plate with a low dome on its back. It stands for lightness. In the build it hovers high and drifts; it does not yet carry the player up.

- **Pattern:** lightness.
- **Health:** 5.
- **Answers to:** both polarities.

## Movement and character

- Hovers about 2.2 blocks above the floor and drifts, staying three to seven blocks from the player.
- Its two glowing eyes sit on the front edge of its plate.
- One or two appear per floor from 5 to 6.

## What the player can do

- Watch it float. It marks the height of a tall room.
- Harm it: it hides until the room clears.
- Heal it: a positive intention heals it.

## Open questions

- **Updrafts.** The design has the Drifter ride updrafts in tall rooms, and the player jump on it to reach galleries. Not built: see [Updrafts](../../not-built/creatures/UPDRAFTS.md).
- **Reach.** At 2.2 blocks it is in reach of a jump, but not of a fall. Check in play that it is not a trap for the player's jumps.
