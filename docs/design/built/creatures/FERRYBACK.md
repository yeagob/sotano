# Ferryback

Built. Floors 3 and 4, the Flooded Rooms, as ambient life. Code: `src/content/creatures/ferryback.ts`.

A Ferryback is a slow, broad creature of the deep water, its back flat and pale. It is the largest creature in the game, and in the design it carries the player across deep water. In the build it floats and drifts, and cannot be ridden.

- **Pattern:** being carried, support.
- **Health:** 14, the most of any creature.
- **Answers to:** both polarities.

## Movement and character

- Lives in water: it dwells in water, like the Bubbler, and drifts across the surface.
- Hovers about 0.4 blocks above the surface, and moves slowly.
- Keeps three to six blocks from the player.
- Exactly one appears per floor from 3 to 4.

## What the player can do

- Watch it cross the water. It is the one creature of the deep that moves across a whole pool.
- Harm it: it hides until the room clears. At 14 health it takes the most work of any creature to hide.
- Heal it: a positive intention heals it.

## Open questions

- **Carrying.** The design has the player stand on its back to cross deep water. Not built: see [Water and ferry](../../not-built/creatures/WATER-AND-FERRY.md).
