# Bubbler

Built. Floors 3 and 4, the Flooded Rooms, as ambient life. Code: `src/content/creatures/bubbler.ts`.

A Bubbler is a small fish of the deep water, long and round, with a dark tail and a pale head. It breathes out bubbles, the body's rhythm. In the build it swims in the water and wanders; it does not yet make bubbles.

- **Pattern:** breath, the body's rhythm.
- **Health:** 4.
- **Answers to:** both polarities.

## Movement and character

- Lives in water: its element affinity is to dwell in water, so it stays in the pools and does not climb out.
- Hovers about 0.8 blocks above the water surface and drifts.
- Stays two to five blocks from the player.
- One or two appear per floor from 3 to 4.

## What the player can do

- Watch it swim. It is a sign of deep water in the rooms of Zone II.
- Harm it: it hides until the room clears.
- Heal it: a positive intention heals it.

## Open questions

- **Bubbles.** The design has bubbles that refill the player's breath when swum through. The build has no bubbles. Not built: see [Water and ferry](../../not-built/creatures/WATER-AND-FERRY.md).
- **Breath.** The design's breath is not a stat in the build. Decide whether the player has breath before the bubbles are built.
