# Rootling

Built. Floor 0, the roots, as ambient life. Code: `src/content/creatures/rootling.ts`.

A Rootling is a tiny root-shaped creature that potters along the roots of the first floor. The design says it burrows toward hidden pockets, to show the player secrets; in the build it only wanders.

- **Pattern:** curiosity.
- **Health:** 4.
- **Answers to:** both polarities.

## Movement and character

- Walks on two short legs over the floor, changing direction every one to three seconds.
- Keeps two to six blocks from the player: it comes closer when the player is far, and steps back when the player is near.
- Two glowing eyes, the living glow, on its front.
- Two to three appear on floor 0.

## What the player can do

- Follow it, to see where it goes. It does not lead to anything yet.
- Harm it: it hides until the room clears.
- Heal it: a positive intention heals it.

## Open questions

- **Pockets.** The design has it burrow toward hidden pockets. Not built. See [Burrowing](../../not-built/creatures/BURROWING.md).
- **Tutorial.** The design has the Rootling show the tutorial's next step on floor 0. Not built: floor 0 has no such cue from it.
