# Inkmite

Built. Floors 5 and 6, the Ledger Halls, as ambient life. Code: `src/content/creatures/inkmite.ts`.

An Inkmite is a tiny mite, black as ink, with two glowing eyes. It stands for letting things go. In the design it eats the judgements of the Lectern and the Margin off the floor; in the build it only wanders.

- **Pattern:** letting things go.
- **Health:** 2, the least of any creature.
- **Answers to:** both polarities.

## Movement and character

- Scurries about the floor, changing direction every one to three seconds.
- Stays one and a half to four blocks from the player.
- Its body is a dark ink tone; its eyes are the only light on it.
- Two or three appear per floor on 5 and 6.

## What the player can do

- Watch it run. It is the only creature whose body is ink, not living tones.
- Harm it: at zero it hides until the room clears, as all creatures do. With 2 health, one strong hit is enough.
- Heal it: a positive intention heals it.

## Open questions

- **Cleaning.** The design has the Inkmites clean the judgements (the marks of the Lectern and the Margin) off the floor during fights. Not built: see [Cleaning](../../not-built/creatures/CLEANING.md).
- **Colour.** Its body is the ink tone, not the living tone. The design's living side does not include it. Is that a deliberate exception?
