# Huddler

Built. Floor 9, the frozen bottom, as ambient life. Code: `src/content/creatures/huddler.ts`.

A Huddler is a small round creature with a rim of frost, and it gathers in groups on the ice. It stands for warmth shared. It answers only positive intentions: the frozen floor does not change it, and only warmth reaches it.

- **Pattern:** warmth shared.
- **Health:** 4.
- **Answers to:** positive intentions only. A negative intention passes through it.

## Movement and character

- Walks slowly on the ice and across water. Its frost rim is the only cold detail.
- Keeps within one and a half to three blocks of the player, and steps back.
- Two or three appear on floor 9.

## What the player can do

- Heal it: a positive intention heals it if it is hurt.
- Ignore it: a negative intention does nothing.
- Find it among the ice. It is the only warm life of the frozen floor.

## Open questions

- **Thawing.** The design has the Huddlers thaw the ice they stand on and the frost that slows the player, and follow a befriended player to thaw the Numbness's ice. Not built: see [Thawing](../../not-built/creatures/THAWING.md).
- **Living on ice.** The frozen floor's ice is a proposal awaiting an art ruling. The Huddler's rim is a cold tone. Confirm the look with the ruling.
