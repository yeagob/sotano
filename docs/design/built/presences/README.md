# Presences (built)

Pictures and a catalogue entry for each presence are in the [bestiary](../bestiary/README.md). The detail of each presence is in its document below.

Presences are the beings of the depths who speak: the parts of the player that already know. They are archetypes, never gods, saints or real people, and they never give orders. They are immortal: they have no health and take no damage, and a negative intention silences them rather than hurting them. Thirteen are in the game: the Listener, on floors 1 to 5; the Boundary, a calm guardian of the vaults; the Trickster, a messenger on one seeded floor from floor 3; the Echo, a messenger that waits where the last run faded; the Wanderer, a messenger on one or two seeded floors from floor 1, with a gift to take or leave; the Gardener, a dweller on one seeded floor from floor 1, which asks for a flesh part and stays; the Elder, a dweller that asks which path still holds and gives a lesson of that path; the Cartographer, a dweller that marks the nearest vault or boss in words; the Child, a dweller that asks you to place three blocks while it watches, then gives one gift; the Witness, a messenger on the ninth floor that asks for a yes to its gift; the Boatman, a dweller by the drowned nave of floor 3 or 4 that ferries you across the deep water for one block; the Mender, a dweller that comes to the first vault you open on floors 3 to 5, asks for two parts of different kinds, gives its Thread and brings back one projection you released as an ally; and the Star-reader, a dweller in a quiet room of one Tree floor that asks for a yes to its light. The Listener is the reference implementation for a part-for-reward Presence: it searches the carried hand and asks before taking the first matching part. The Trickster and the Echo ask the same way, on the same consent card, and decline costs nothing. The Wanderer asks for nothing but a yes to its gift, on the same card. The Gardener waits on one seeded floor from floor 1 and asks for a flesh part. Everything else in the design is in [not-built/presences](../../not-built/presences/README.md).

## What they share (built)

- **Immortal.** No health. The Boundary has armour that absorbs every hit; the Listener has one point of health that nothing removes.
- **Polarity.** Positive intentions pass through with a chime or a healing where it applies. Negative intentions silence a presence within two blocks: its card shows one line of refusal, and it says nothing more until a positive intention passes within eight blocks. See [Polarity](../encounters/POLARITY.md).
- **Talk by contact.** A presence speaks when the player comes within about two blocks of it, in a card with its lines, read in the player's language. The card stays until it is dismissed.
- **Replies and bodies.** Each presence has a body the player cannot walk through. Until its part is brought, it answers each approach with the name of what the player holds, and says it is not what it waits for; with empty hands it says so. It answers at most once every four seconds.
- **The key part.** A presence speaks only to the player who brings it its part. The Listener takes a heart; the Gardener, which is not placed, takes a flesh. The carried hand is searched from the interaction item down, and the first matching part is taken; other carried things stay. Until then the presence says nothing, not even its silence line. See [Parts](../projections/PARTS.md).
- **Their rooms never seal.** A presence's room is placed with no projections in it, so it is cleared from the start and its doors stay open.
- **Look.** The Listener is built from bone tones. The Boundary is limestone, like its vault. Neither uses the cold or the living tones.

## Open questions (shared)

- **Sanctuary.** The design gives each presence a sanctuary: projections never enter its room, and none spawn in its ring. The build does not enforce this beyond the empty room.
- **Kind.** The Boundary is a projection in the code and a creature in the design (see its document). The shared rules above assume a presence is neither.
- **Naming.** The design's roster names thirteen presences placed in the build (the Listener, the Boundary, the Trickster, the Echo, the Wanderer, the Gardener, the Elder, the Cartographer, the Child, the Witness, the Boatman, the Mender and the Star-reader). The rest are in [not-built/presences](../../not-built/presences/README.md).
- **Consent.** Every delivery shows the exact part, takes it only on confirmation and keeps everything on a refusal. The Trickster and the Echo's defaults (their costs, weights and places) are proposed, pending owner ruling; see each document.
