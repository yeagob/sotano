# Red heart

The normal heart: one heart back into the containers.

Part of [kinds of heart](README.md).

## What the player sees and feels

- A classic red heart on the floor. Walking into it heals 100 (one heart) into the containers.
- At full health it stays on the floor until you are hurt.
- Nothing happens when its health is spent: red health is the containers', and it is always recoverable.

Code: `src/game/game.ts` (`collectHeart`, `collect`).
