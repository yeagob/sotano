# The sky (built)

Up: [built](../README.md) · the parts of the sky realm that are in the code now.

The sky is the second world of a run, above the crown of the built giant tree. Reaching the top of the built tree carries the player, through a short fade, onto the sky's arrival deck, at the foot of the sky tree. Its five halls are the five guardians' floors (panda, koala, elephant, lion, horse), stacked inside the trunk, each closed above until its deity is won; above the crown rises [Totality](../levels/totality/README.md), which ends in the Sun. Nothing in the sky takes the player's health: the player cannot die there.

What is still to come (the open laws, the minions in play, the deities' sounds, the tower rule inside a floor, Unity) is in [not-built/sky](../../not-built/sky/README.md).
