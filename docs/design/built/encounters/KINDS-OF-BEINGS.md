# The kinds of beings

Built. Everything that moves in the depths is one of three kinds. The kind decides its faction, whether it can be hurt, whether it speaks and which side of the palette it is drawn from. All three are built from the same small blocks and the room's own materials.

| Kind | What it is | Faction | Can be hurt | Speaks | Look |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Projection | A pattern that blocks intention, given a body of the room's matter | Projection: hostile to the player | Yes; it is released, never killed | Never | The cold side: a dark husk with a cold eye |
| Creature | What was always alive down here: instinct, curiosity, play, rest, care | Neutral | Yes, by polarity; it hides rather than dies | No (chirps and hums only) | The living side: soft tones with a breathing glow |
| Presence | The parts of the player that already know; they ask, listen, teach | Neutral, immortal | Never | Yes, in short lines | Bone tones, with no eyes until the player is close |

Bosses are projections with a larger body (see [bosses](../bosses/README.md)).

## What holds for all three

- **Patterns, never people.** No being is a person, a group, a diagnosis, a tradition or a figure from one. Presences are archetypes.
- **Made of the world.** All three are built from the room's blocks and small boxes. Nothing is a model imported from elsewhere.
- **Readable without text.** A projection has a cold eye; a creature has a living glow; a presence has no eyes until the player comes close.

## How the kind shows in play

- Projections are the only beings that are hostile and that are counted to clear a room.
- Creatures are never a target and never counted. Explosions push them gently.
- Presences are never a target and never counted. They talk when the player comes near.

## Open questions

- **The Boundary.** The built Boundary is a projection with armour that makes it unhurt, and the design calls it a creature. See [the Boundary](../presences/BOUNDARY.md).
