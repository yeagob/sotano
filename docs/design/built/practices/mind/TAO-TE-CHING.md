## What it means

The softest thing overcomes the hardest: intentions flow through five blocks of any kind.

## What it does

Intentions pass through up to 5 blocks of ANY tier (even obsidian), damaging every block they cross, and keep flying. Mining x1.5.

## Stats and events

- mining: x1.5
- pass-through count: +5 blocks of any tier
- when an intention is fired
