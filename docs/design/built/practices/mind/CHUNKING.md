## What it means

A big thing becomes small pieces you can carry.

## What it does

An intention that hits a projection or a block splits into two: each has half the damage and mining, 60% of the speed, and flies on in its own direction.

## Stats and events

- each piece: half damage and mining, x0.6 speed
- when an intention hits: it splits in two (pieces do not split again)
