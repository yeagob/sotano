## What it means

Let something else hold what you carry: carry one more block.

## What it does

Carry 1 more block. It adds to whatever you carry, so with the two hands you carry 3. Reach +1 block.

## Stats and events

- carry: +1 block
- reach: +1 block (`reach` add 1; starting reach 4, so 5 while held)

## Why reach fits

Something else holds what you carry, and a tool in the other hand reaches what your own arm cannot. The external memory that holds the extra block also reaches a little further for the place it goes.
