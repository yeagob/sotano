## What it means

Two things that understand each other pass the current along.

## What it does

Intentions that hit a loose cube charge it. A charged cube that lands hard discharges: 4 points to projections within 2.5 blocks, shoved back.

## Stats and events

- when an intention hits a loose cube: the cube is charged
- when a loose cube lands: if it is charged, it discharges (2.5 blocks, 4 damage)
