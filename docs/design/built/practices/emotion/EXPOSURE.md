## What it means

Walk into your fears and they wear away: whatever you press against crumbles, little by little.

## What it does

Walking into any block around you (never the ones below) wears it down: 20 block damage per second, armor still counts. Soft blocks fall at once, obsidian takes seconds.

## Stats and events

- contact wear: +20 block damage per second (a core rule of contact, not a hook)
