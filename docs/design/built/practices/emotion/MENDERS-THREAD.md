## What it means

What broke was mended with gold, and the seam shows. It is stronger where it broke. It is the Covenant's Thread, held in the hand (owner, issue #228, D10).

## What it does

+1 heart container, and 0.25 s more invulnerability after each hit. Promised: no capture, toll or rule can take it from you.

## Stats and events

- heart containers: +1
- invulnerability after a hit: +0.25 s
- promised: its only pool is `covenant`, which nothing draws; the toll card neither offers nor accepts it; a sky capture never takes it; no presence table gives it; it has no recipe (one source only)
