## What it means

A line that says how close is close enough.

## What it does

Intentions and weapon strokes deal no damage to projections. Each one an intention or a beam hits is shoved 7 blocks/s back along the shot.

## Stats and events

- when a beam strikes: no damage
- when an intention is fired: no damage
- when an intention hits a projection: shoved 7 blocks per second back along the shot
