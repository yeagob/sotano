## What it means

Release what touches you: projections that hit you are hurt by it, and half the time you are not.

## What it does

Projections that touch you take 4 damage. Half of their contact hits on you are ignored. +1 heart container.

## Stats and events

- heart containers: +1
- when a projection touches you: it takes 4 damage; half the time its hit does nothing
