## What it means

A symbolic act reaches what words cannot: blocks you throw burst on impact.

## What it does

Blocks you throw explode when they hit something (radius 1.8, can hurt you). Throw strength x1.3.

## Stats and events

- throw strength: x1.3
- when a block is thrown: marked as volatile
- when a thrown block lands: if it is volatile, it explodes (radius 1.8, 14 damage)
