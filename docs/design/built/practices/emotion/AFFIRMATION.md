## What it means

What you affirm you make a little more real, one copy at a time.

## What it does

Intentions curve toward the nearest block within 6 blocks. A positive one that hits a block leaves a loose copy of it beside the block.

## Stats and events

- homing: +3
- when an intention is fired: it curves toward the nearest block
- when an intention hits a block: a positive one leaves a loose copy beside it
