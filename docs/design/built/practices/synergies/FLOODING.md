## Effect as built

Habit Stacking's neighbour rule is switched off while Flooding is held. In its place, every block break (except the chain of a break that came from this rule) shatters the same-material neighbours outright.

## Design text

Blocks you shatter by contact break their neighbours outright (STORY section 5).

## Open questions

- Trigger F1: the design text says the shattering follows contact (blocks you shatter by walking into them). The built rule follows every block break, from any source such as a bomb or an intention. The owner decides which rule stands.
