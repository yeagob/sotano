## What it means

Conscious breathing is the most important thing you can do. Gather your breath, hold it, and let it all out at once: nothing in its way stays standing.

## What it does

Replaces your intentions with a charged beam: hold to charge (2.7 divided by fire rate seconds, between 0.35 and 2), release for a 1-second beam of 10 ticks. Each tick deals 2x mining to the block in its way (it burns on through as blocks break) and 1x damage to every projection on it, and destroys their projectiles.

## Stats and events

- a weapon: the beam (it replaces the intention)
- when the beam strikes: its damage and mining can be changed by a synergy
