## What it means

One change triggers the next: breaking a block damages its same-material neighbours.

## What it does

When you break a block, its same-material neighbours take 60% of their HP as damage.

## Stats and events

- when a block breaks: same-material neighbours take 60% of their HP; damage from this rule does not chain again
