## What it means

Pain can light a warmth that answers it back.

## What it does

When a projection hurts you, fire bursts out of you: 2.5 blocks, 4 points to projections in it, and flammable blocks within 2 blocks catch fire.

## Stats and events

- when a projection damages you: a fire burst of 2.5 blocks, 4 damage to projections; flammable blocks within 2 blocks catch fire
