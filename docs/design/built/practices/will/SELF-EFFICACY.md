## What it means

Believing you can climb makes the climb possible.

## What it does

A projection hit by an intention runs away from you for 3 seconds instead of attacking. Reach +1 block.

## Stats and events

- when an intention hits a projection: it runs away for 3 seconds
- reach: +1 block (`reach` add 1; starting reach 4, so 5 while held)

## Why reach fits

Believing you can climb is believing you can get there. The practice's belief now also stretches what your hand can place, so the same confidence that moves the projection moves the block.
