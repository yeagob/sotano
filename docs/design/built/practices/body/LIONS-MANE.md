## What it means

A shaggy mushroom that grows on old wood, where nobody looks. Eat it and you feel bigger than the room: the ceiling was never the limit.

## What it does

Jump height x2. Jumping into dirt, crates, stone or brick (tier 1 or lower) breaks them with your head.

## Stats and events

- jump height: x2
- head-break reach: tier 1 or lower
- when your head breaks a block: now and then (1 in 5) a short wink message shows
