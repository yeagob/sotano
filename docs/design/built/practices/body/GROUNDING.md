## What it means

Rooted in the body, you push with your whole weight: kick twice as hard.

## What it does

Kicks are twice as strong. Reach +1 block.

## Stats and events

- kick strength: x2
- reach: +1 block (`reach` add 1; starting reach 4, so 5 while held)

## Why reach fits

Rooted, you do not move your feet to reach: the weight that pushes harder also places from where you stand, one block further.
