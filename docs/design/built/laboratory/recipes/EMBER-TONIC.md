The captive ember of a will pattern, made steady: the player casts faster for three rooms.

- **Path:** will.
- **Bulb:** one heart of a will pattern, for example Panic's captive ember.
- **Effect:** fire rate ×1.3.
- **Duration:** three sealed rooms.
- **Why it stays scarce:** Hearts are left about 5 % of the time.

Code: `src/content/potions.ts` (the effect) and `src/content/recipes.ts` (the bulb).
