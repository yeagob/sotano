# Spring Tonic

Up: [recipes](README.md) · built. Name and numbers are proposals the owner has not confirmed; the open questions are in [potions: open questions](../../../not-built/practices/potions/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md#built-recipes).

One floor of easier climbing. The name refers to the spring of the Impulse pattern.

- **Path:** body.
- **Bulb:** one heart of a body pattern, for example Impulse's spring.
- **Effect:** jump strength ×1.4.
- **Duration:** until the next floor entry. Climbing back up does not give it back.
- **Why it stays scarce:** Hearts are left about 5 % of the time. It gives one floor of easier climbing, not the climb back.

Code: `src/content/potions.ts` (the effect) and `src/content/recipes.ts` (the bulb).
