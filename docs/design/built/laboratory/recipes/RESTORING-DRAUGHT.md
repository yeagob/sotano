A heart for two flesh: the first real healing, dearer than a heart from clearing a room.

- **Path:** body.
- **Bulb:** two flesh, of any patterns, the same one or two different ones.
- **Effect:** restores one heart, never past the heart containers.
- **Duration:** instant.
- **Why it stays scarce:** Two parts and a brew for one heart. A heart from a room clear is cheaper.
