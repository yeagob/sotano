# Footing Tincture

Up: [recipes](README.md) · built. Name and numbers are proposals the owner has not confirmed; the open questions are in [potions: open questions](../../../not-built/practices/potions/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md#built-recipes).

A stance for three rooms: kicks land harder.

- **Path:** body.
- **Bulb:** one flesh of a body pattern (Craving, Impulse, Reactivity, Spark, Perfectionism, Not Yet) and one deepstone or obsidian cube.
- **Effect:** kick strength ×1.5.
- **Duration:** three sealed rooms.
- **Why it stays scarce:** It needs a hard block carried to the bulb, a second trip with one hand slot.
- **Knockback:** the design also says projection hits do not knock the player back. No projection hit knocks the player back in the game today, for anyone, so the tincture changes only the kick.

Code: `src/content/potions.ts` (the effect) and `src/content/recipes.ts` (the bulb).
