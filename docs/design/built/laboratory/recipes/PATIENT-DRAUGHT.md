# Patient Draught

Up: [recipes](README.md) · built. Name and numbers are proposals the owner has not confirmed; the open questions are in [potions: open questions](../../../not-built/practices/potions/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md#built-recipes).

A draught for digging. It uses two kinds from one pattern, and it helps the player dig, not fight.

- **Path:** will.
- **Bulb:** one heart and one mind of the same will pattern.
- **Effect:** mining ×1.6. Damage to projections does not change.
- **Duration:** until the next floor entry.
- **Why it stays scarce:** Two kinds from one pattern, and the heart is rare (about 5 % of releases). It helps the player dig, not fight.

Code: `src/content/potions.ts` (the effect) and `src/content/recipes.ts` (the bulb).
