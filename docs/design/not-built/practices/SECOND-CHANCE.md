# Second chance

Up: [not-built practices](README.md)

Status: partly built. Waymark builds the second chance: [built/practices/will/WAYMARK.md](../../built/practices/will/WAYMARK.md) is the practice (one copy is spent per death, the player returns at full hearts to the checkpoint they last knew, with 2.5 s of invulnerability). The questions below are the parts that are still open. The player's side of the idea ([player/SECOND-CHANCE.md](../player/SECOND-CHANCE.md)) points here.

## What is built (moved to Waymark)

- A practice of its own kind, in the will path, from the vault and boss pools.
- The cost: one copy per save; several copies save several deaths.
- What it restores: full hearts; everything carried is kept.
- The name: Waymark, a generic concept with its credit in `inspiredBy`.
- The death screen is unchanged: a save is a death that does not happen, so the death screen does not show.

## What is open

- Its interaction with the toll. The way down asks a price, and a second chance must not make the price meaningless or trap a player who has played fair. Waymark keeps the toll as it is; a toll can still take the copy.
- Its interaction with the promised gifts (the gold seam that cannot be lost) if those are built ([BESTIARY, section 8](README.md)).
- Whether a boss room that is overcome gives a copy back. Not decided.
- Whether the death screen should name a save (for example "the waymark brought you back" in the run's log). Not decided; it would change the story's words for the end of a run ([the death screen](../../built/player/DEATH.md)).
