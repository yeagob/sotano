# Second chance

Up: [not-built practices](README.md)

Status: idea (from the architecture of entities, not decided by the owner).

## The idea

A practice that lets a run go on when the last heart is lost: a second chance. The death of the player is an event that can be cancelled, and the architecture notes that a "second chance" item is a future use of that cancel ([ENTITIES](../../../architecture/ENTITIES.md)).

## What exists today

Losing the last heart ends the run. The death screen says that the intention faded, names the cause and the floor reached, and shows the seed and the practices held. R begins again. Nothing carries over.

## What is open

- Whether it is a practice, and if so its quality, its pool and its path. The story says practices are the way the person grows, so a second chance would have to be a practice of its own kind.
- The cost: whether it is used up on the first save, or whether it returns on each floor.
- Its name. No name is chosen. The naming rules apply: a generic technique or a public-domain title, and never a modern book ([NAMING](../../built/story/NAMING.md)).
- Its meaning against the death screen's words. "The intention faded" is the story's words for the end of a run, and a second chance must not contradict them.
- Its interaction with the toll. The way down asks a price, and a second chance must not make the price meaningless or trap a player who has played fair.
- Its interaction with the promised gifts (the gold seam that cannot be lost) if those are built ([BESTIARY, section 8](README.md)).
