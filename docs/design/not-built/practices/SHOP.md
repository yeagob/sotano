# A shop for what you carry

Up: [not-built practices](README.md)

Status: idea (the owner's pending task of 28 October 2026; the owner has not decided the shape).

## The idea

Items and parts need a purpose, and many have none yet. A shop, where what you carry can be traded for other things, would give them one. The question is in the design text as the eighth open question of the design section ([DESIGN, section 13](../player/SHOP.md)).

## Why it is open

- The design says "no coins, no shop" (DESIGN, section 4): the consumables are bombs and keys, as in Isaac, and there is no currency.
- The owner's pending task asks for a way for parts and practices to have a purpose. A shop is one answer, and the design text leaves it open.
- A part is built as a collectible that takes a hand slot and has no effect (see [PARTS-PURPOSE](PARTS-PURPOSE.md)). Without a use, it is only weight in the hand.

## The other answer on the table

The potions proposal ([potions](potions/README.md)) gives parts a use without a shop: an alembic in a laboratory brews captive parts into potions. The proposal records the owner's decision that the alembic closes this task with no shop, and it lists the one question still open: whether that keeps "no coins, no shop". The owner decides.

## Open questions

1. Where is the shop: a room type, a vendor who appears on some floors, or a fixed place on the way down?
2. What trades for what: a part for a practice, a practice for a practice, or only parts for keys and bombs?
3. Do keys and bombs count as currency, or do they stay consumables?
4. Does the shop replace the alembic answer, or sit beside it?
5. Does "no coins, no shop" stand, or is it changed?
