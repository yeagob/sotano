# Riding the pendulum

Up: [not-built practices](README.md)

Status: idea (from the lore; the owner has not chosen a form for it).

## The idea

The master rises above the swing of rhythm instead of being carried by it. The Kybalion teaches this as neutralising the pendulum (Three Initiates, 1908, public domain). The lore suggests it for rooms or projections with a rhythm that rewards timing over reaction ([CONCEPTS](../../../lore/CONCEPTS.md), [COSMOLOGY, the Kybalion further](../../../lore/COSMOLOGY.md#the-kybalion-further)).

A station of the design already has the same idea: a clock whose pendulum swings, and whose platform moves only on the beat ([STATIONS](../../not-built/stations/README.md)).

## Where it could live

- A room whose floor or wall moves in a rhythm: the player crosses on the beat.
- A projection whose attacks come in a rhythm: the player who reacts is carried, the player who waits for the beat is not.
- A practice that reads the rhythm and gives timing a reward.

## Open questions
