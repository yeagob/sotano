# Warm Cordial

Status: proposal. The name and numbers are proposals; the brewing caps are approved ([BREWING-RULES](BREWING-RULES.md)). Index: [potions](README.md).

A cordial that makes positive intentions count double for three rooms. It converts, and it never kills.

- **Path:** emotion.
- **Bulb contents:** one heart of an emotion pattern, and any heart.
- **Effect:** positive intentions give double warmth, so a projection converts in half the hits.
- **Duration:** three sealed rooms.
- **Why it stays scarce:** two hearts, each about 5 % of the time.
