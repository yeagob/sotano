# Restoring Draught

Status: proposal. The name and numbers are proposals; the brewing caps are approved ([BREWING-RULES](BREWING-RULES.md)). Index: [potions](README.md).

A heart for two flesh. It is the first real healing, and it is dearer than a heart from clearing a room.

- **Path:** body.
- **Bulb contents:** two flesh, of any patterns.
- **Effect:** heals one heart, never past the player's containers.
- **Duration:** instant.
- **Why it stays scarce:** two parts and a brew for one heart. A heart from a room clear is cheaper.
