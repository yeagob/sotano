# Open-Hand Elixir

Status: proposal. The name and numbers are proposals; the brewing caps are approved ([BREWING-RULES](BREWING-RULES.md)). Index: [potions](README.md).

An open hand for a whole floor: contact hurts less, and a projection that touches the player is warmed a little.

- **Path:** emotion.
- **Bulb contents:** one essence of an emotion pattern.
- **Effect:** contact hits on the player are halved. A projection that touches the player gains 0.2 warmth.
- **Duration:** one floor.
- **Why it stays scarce:** essences drop 2 % of the time.

## Open questions

1. The 0.2 warmth needs a warmth event that does not exist yet ([PARTS-AND-SUPPLY](PARTS-AND-SUPPLY.md) §6).
2. Does "contact" mean the contact event only, or also a hit that touches the player during a dash?
3. A projection that touches a player who is warmed is a conversion path, which the owner should confirm against the rule that positive intentions convert rather than kill ([BESTIARY](README.md)).
