# Ember Tonic

Status: proposal. The name and numbers are proposals; the brewing caps are approved ([BREWING-RULES](BREWING-RULES.md)). Index: [potions](README.md).

The captive ember of a will pattern, made steady: the player fires faster for three rooms.

- **Path:** will.
- **Bulb contents:** one heart of a will pattern, for example Panic's captive ember.
- **Effect:** fire rate times 1.3.
- **Duration:** three sealed rooms.
- **Why it stays scarce:** hearts drop 5 % of the time.
