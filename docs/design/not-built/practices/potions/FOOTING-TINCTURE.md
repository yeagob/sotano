# Footing Tincture

Status: proposal. The name and numbers are proposals; the brewing caps are approved ([BREWING-RULES](BREWING-RULES.md)). Index: [potions](README.md).

A stance for three rooms: the player does not get knocked back, and kicks hit harder.

- **Path:** body.
- **Bulb contents:** one flesh of a body pattern, and one deepstone or obsidian cube.
- **Effect:** projection hits do not knock the player back; kick strength times 1.5.
- **Duration:** three sealed rooms.
- **Why it stays scarce:** it needs a hard block carried to the bulb, which is a second trip with one hand slot.
