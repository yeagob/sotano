# Distillate of a pattern

Status: proposal. The name template and numbers are proposals; the brewing caps are approved ([BREWING-RULES](BREWING-RULES.md)). Index: [potions](README.md).

The long game of the potion system. One recipe with one name template: "Distillate of Doubt", "Distillate of the Lectern", filled with the pattern's name from the catalogues. The player has prepared the whole pattern, so the player knows it when it comes again.

- **Path:** the pattern's own path.
- **Bulb contents:** the heart, the mind and the essence of the same pattern.
- **Effect:** that pattern, and its lineage, materialise with half warmth. Three good hits convert them.
- **Duration:** the whole run.
- **Why it stays scarce:** one per pattern per run. It takes the whole bulb, an essence, and a full ferry: every part carried to the bulb one at a time through the single hand slot.

## Open questions

1. The bulb contents do not include flesh. The text says "the whole bulb", which may hold flesh too. The owner decides whether flesh belongs.
2. "Materialise with half warmth" is a new mechanic: the pattern and its lineage return as projections. Does a materialised projection count as the player's own work, and does it drop parts?
3. Does a distillate of a pattern the player has not met need the codex first?
4. The Distillate survives floor entries; see [RISKS-AND-TESTS](RISKS-AND-TESTS.md), risk 3.
