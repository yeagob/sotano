# Mental transmutation

Up: [not-built practices](README.md)

Status: idea (from the lore; the owner has not chosen a practice for it).

## The idea

A state is changed by moving it along its own pole, fear toward courage, rather than by fighting it. The teaching comes from the Kybalion (Three Initiates, 1908, public domain), and the lore's cosmology suggests it for practices that turn a state into its opposite ([CONCEPTS](../../../lore/CONCEPTS.md), [COSMOLOGY, the Kybalion further](../../../lore/COSMOLOGY.md#the-kybalion-further)).

The game already has poles: an intention is good or bad, and the player switches the loaded intention with F. A practice of mental transmutation would turn a bad thing into a good one along the same pole instead of removing it.

## Where it touches the game

- The polarity of the intention. A practice could change a bad intention into a good one, or a projection's state along its pole.
- The shard. The art ledger has a decided, not built, shard whose hue moves from its dull pole to its bright pole. It is cosmetic today ([DECISIONS](../../../art/DECISIONS.md)).
- A possible alchemy practice in the potions proposal, which keeps a fourth brew ([potions](potions/README.md)).

## Open questions

1. Which practice carries it, and at what quality? The lore suggests it is a practice that turns a state, not one that removes a projection.
2. Is the effect a conversion of an intention, a projection, or a block?
3. Does it need a new path tag? The tags are the four paths, and the practice would be a mind or an emotion practice.
4. Is it a stat, a hook, or a weapon? The answer decides which systems it touches.
5. Is "mental transmutation" a name? The Kybalion is public domain, and the phrase is its term. The owner confirms the name.
