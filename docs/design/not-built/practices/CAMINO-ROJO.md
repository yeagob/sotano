# Camino Rojo practices for the Body path

Up: [not-built practices](README.md) · Issue [#194](https://github.com/yeagob/sotano/issues/194)

Status: **idea** (pending the owner's definition). The owner asked to add the Camino Rojo tradition to the lore and to design three practices from it for the Body path. Nothing here is built, and nothing is decided beyond the lore entries.

## What is built

Nothing from this tradition. The Body path's built practices are in [built/practices/body](../../built/practices/body/README.md) (presence, energy, nervous-system regulation). The sources are in [SOURCES.md](../../../lore/SOURCES.md#indigenous-traditions): `camino-rojo`, `temazcal`, `vision-quest`.

## The tradition, as presented

The Camino Rojo (Red Road) is a modern path of self-knowledge that draws on indigenous traditions of Mesoamerica and the wider Americas. It is a tradition of several indigenous nations and their living practices, not one community's property, and the game presents it that way. Its origin and its founders are not verified; the lore says so.

Shared ideas the game may use:

- **The Earth as a living being**: the world is alive and relational.
- **All beings are kin** ("por todas mis relaciones", for all my relations): no being is above or below another.
- **Discipline and self-observation**: the path is walked through practices that slow the mind and open perception.
- **Ceremonies as channels**: ritual as release, purification and vision.

## Design targets

Three practices, each touching at least two systems (the rule in [practices README](README.md)).

### 1. "Por todas mis relaciones": interconnection

Proposed: an intention's effect reaches nearby allies and weakens nearby projections. A heal also mends creatures near the player; breaking blocks also weakens projections close by. Touches **combat** (projections weakened) and **creatures** (allies healed). Lore: `camino-rojo`; [CONCEPTS.md](../../../lore/CONCEPTS.md) *All beings are kin*.

### 2. Temazcal: purification and preparation

Proposed: a room-based ceremony or a consumable. In a short ceremony the player cannot move, regenerates health and gains a temporary shield; afterwards a temporary boost (for example break power or resistance). Clears negative status effects on entry. Touches **status effects**, **health** and **combat**. Lore: `temazcal`, a shared ceremony of many communities.

### 3. Búsqueda de visión: solitude and clarity

Proposed: a practice that trades short-term power for long-term gain. The player enters a fasting state with less movement or attack power and more perception (hidden blocks become visible). After a duration, or after clearing a room in this state, the player receives a vision: a unique practice, a map reveal or a buff. Touches **movement and combat** (reduced), **perception and exploration** (enhanced) and a **unique reward**. Lore: `vision-quest`.

## How it would connect to existing systems

- **Body path**: all three develop presence, regulation and inner work.
- **States of being**: the three could feed the Embodied state, or unlock a state tied to this tradition.
- **Encounters**: the temazcal could be a safe room with a timed ceremony.
- **Meta-progression**: a completed vision quest could mark the root floor (see path unlocks, #193).
- **Lore**: "all beings are kin" extends the systemic thinking of constellations and the Tree of Life with an indigenous framing ([COSMOLOGY.md §4](../../../lore/COSMOLOGY.md#4-other-maps-of-the-same-world)).

## Respect

- The practices are not magic cures. The tradition is not a joke and not an enemy: projections are patterns, never people, groups or traditions.
- Quotes are never presented as said by real practitioners.
- Sacred medicines are **out of scope** (open question 7).

## Open questions (the owner's, from #194; not decided)

1. **Scope.** Should all three be Body path, or should "Por todas mis relaciones" sit in Emotion or cross paths?
2. **Naming.** How should "por todas mis relaciones" appear in-game: the phrase, a translation ("For All My Relations"), or a paraphrased name?
3. **Temazcal as room or item.** A room the player finds, a consumable practice, or both?
4. **Vision quest penalty.** How severe is the fasting state? Does it block combat entirely, or only reduce power?
5. **Vision reward.** A unique practice, a map reveal, a codex entry or a buff?
6. **Path unlocks.** Should completing all three trigger a special unlock (see #193)?
7. **Sacred medicines.** Should the game mention peyote and ayahuasca at all, or explicitly exclude them?
8. **Cultural sensitivity.** Should the owner consider whether a roguelite should represent a living indigenous tradition, and whether to consult practitioners?
9. **Lore connections.** "All beings are kin" overlaps with systemic constellations and the Tree of Life. Make the connection explicit, or keep the sources distinct?
10. **Visual identity.** If these practices form a recognisable set, should they share a marker (a glyph, a colour accent, a room decoration)?

## Flagged in the book (for the owner, not decided)

The book's pages (page 10) mention San Pedro cacti, a plant used in ceremony, and page 11 mentions a ceremonial fire and the calling of the seven directions. These are the author's own accounts, not named works, and they are not sourced here. Whether the San Pedro cactus stays out of the game falls under open question 7.
