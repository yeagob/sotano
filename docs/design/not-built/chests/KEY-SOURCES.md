# Keys change only through chests

Up: [not-built chests](README.md) · Status: proposal, open question.

The chest design says keys change only through chests: a key is spent to open one, and keys come out of chests. The build also spends keys on the locked vault door and on the toll, and gives them from room clears and the roots. The two cannot both stand, and the owner has not chosen.

## The two readings

- **Design.** Keys live in chests. A locked door would be opened by a key found in a chest, not a key carried from the floor; the toll would not ask for keys; a cleared room would not drop a key. Every key the player holds came from opening a chest, so the economy is one loop.
- **Build.** A key has four doors: chests, the vault door (one key), the toll on floors 0 to 4, and three sources (the floor's own keys, chest contents, room clears and the roots' loose key). The build does not follow the design's one loop.

## What would change

- The floor's key on a K spot of a normal room, and its loose keys in rooms, would become chest contents.
- The toll on floors 0 to 4 would ask for other things (hearts, bombs, items, hand blocks) without keys.
- Room clears would drop hearts and bombs only.

## Open questions

1. Does a key come only from a chest? If yes, the vault key and the toll's keys move into chests.
2. Does the toll keep its keys (the build), or give up keys for another currency?
3. Do the roots' loose keys stay loose, or become a chest?
