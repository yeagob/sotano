# Room-clear rewards

Up: [not-built chests](README.md) · Status: idea, open question.

The build still rewards a cleared room: when the last projection dissolves, the room may drop a heart, a bomb or a key, the way Isaac does. The chest design asked whether every reward should come from chests instead. The owner has not answered.

## The two readings

- **Keep the drops.** A cleared room now and then gives a heart, a bomb or a key. Luck makes it likelier. This is the build, and it keeps the feel of the room that has just been cleared.
- **Chests only.** A cleared room gives nothing; every reward is in a chest, a pedestal or the roots. This keeps the economy in one place (see [KEY-SOURCES](KEY-SOURCES.md)).

## Open questions

1. Should clearing a room still drop a heart, a bomb or a key, or should only chests reward the player? The build says keep the drops; the design leaves it open.
2. If the drops stay, what share of clears give a key? The build's table gives a key in about one clear in seven.
3. Do the drops of a challenge room, which pays after each wave, stay the same?
