# Chest variants (ideas)

Up: [not-built chests](README.md) · Status: idea.

Three variants of the chest came up in the design and are not built. None is decided. Each changes what a chest is, so each needs the owner's word before a build.

## Mimic chest

A chest that asks for something other than keys: a heart or a bomb, as a cursed or mimic chest. The player would pay the cost in what they carry, and the chest would hold more.

- **Would do.** Ask for a heart or a bomb instead of a key; show the cost before the player pays.
- **Open.** Is a mimic a chest that hurts when opened, or a chest that asks for a cost the player can refuse?

## A chest that needs a state of being

A chest that opens only when the player carries a practice of a given path, or is in a given state of being. The design's question was whether a chest should need the practice of the path, not a key.

- **Would do.** Open for a state of being instead of keys.
- **Open.** Which states of being, and does the chest say what it needs?

## A chest that moves when you look away

A chest that moves when the player is not looking, as a presence would. This is a moving chest, a creature's trick rather than a block's.

- **Would do.** Move one block when the player turns away, and wait.
- **Open.** Is it a presence (the bestiary decides presences) or a block? A moving chest would need its own rule for how it moves through the room, which the design does not give.

## Chests the player can carry

Chests that the player can pick up and place are a station idea, not a chest variant. They are in [stations](../stations/README.md).
