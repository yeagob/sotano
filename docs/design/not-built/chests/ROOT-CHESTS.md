# Room chests on the roots

Up: [not-built chests](README.md) · Status: proposal, open question.

The chest design says that free-standing chests appear in normal rooms "every floor including the roots". The build places no room chest on floor 0. The roots are the tutorial: their rooms are fixed templates with lessons, and the tutorial gives its rewards as loose keys and bombs instead.

## The two readings

- **Design.** Floor 0 holds room chests like the other floors, so the first floor already teaches the chest, its locks (zero or one key on the roots) and its practices. The roots are friendly and every door is open, so a free chest would fit.
- **Build.** The roots carry no room chests, and their practices are on the pedestals only. The lessons of the roots are on the tablets, with no chest to explain.

## What would change

- A room chest on the roots would use the same placement rules as elsewhere (see [CHESTS](../../built/chests/CHESTS.md)), so the lessons' rooms would need free tiles that the tutorial plan keeps clear.
- The tutorial's loose key and bomb would be joined by the chest's contents, so the roots would give more.

## Open questions

1. Do the roots have room chests, or do they stay free of them as the build does?
2. If yes: free only, or can a root chest ask for one key (the roots' lock table allows zero or one)?
3. Are the roots' rooms with a chest the lesson rooms, or only the rooms the lessons do not use?
