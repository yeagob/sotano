# The run's hexagram

Up: [not-built feel](README.md)

Status: decided in the cosmology (the run's seed is read as a hexagram), not built. Where it shows, and the changing lines, are partly decided and partly open.

## The decision

The owner decided that the run's seed is shown and read as a hexagram: the same seed gives the same reading. The changing lines could hint at how the run turns ([COSMOLOGY, the Book of Changes](../../../lore/COSMOLOGY.md)). The I Ching reads any moment as one of sixty-four hexagrams of six lines. Nothing is built yet: the code has no hexagram.

## The six lines

The art ledger ties the six lines to the six stations of the run:

- Line 1 is floor 1, through line 4, floor 4. Line 5 is the source, and line 6 is the sky.
- A line is shown at 40% strength until its station is completed. The hexagram builds upward as you go down.
- Lines are drawn from the bottom up, in the paper colour, with the changing lines in gold.

The ledger says the hexagram is an image, not a fate, and that it never appears on the always-on HUD.

## Where it shows

The ledger and the cosmology do not agree on this.

- The ledger's section on the run's hexagram lists four places: the start screen (large, beside the seed code), the floor inlay in the start room (phase D), the pause screen (small, with the stations lit), and the end of the run, where the changing lines flip into the second hexagram ("where this moment was going").
- The death screen also shows the hexagram, small, with the completed stations lit.
- The ledger's answer to its own question 8 says the names of the hexagram show on the start and pause screens only, and that the six lines are the six stations.

## Open questions

1. Where does it show: the start and pause screens only (the answer to question 8), or also the start-room inlay, the death screen and the end of the run (the section on the hexagram)?
2. Do the names of the hexagram show at all, and in which language? The names are the ledger's own gentle wording.
3. Do the changing lines change the run, or only show where the moment was going? The ledger leaves the mechanics for later, to be designed with the tree map.
4. Does a practice read the hexagram? The lore lists "reading the moment" as a future practice, companion to Follow the Omens ([CONCEPTS](../../../lore/CONCEPTS.md)).
5. Does the bestiary's witness read the hexagram at the end of the run? The design says yes ([BESTIARY](README.md)), and the owner has not decided the witness.
