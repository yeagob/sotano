# Art, sound and safety notes for the new beings

Status: proposal. The rulings below are for the owner and the art gate ([`sotano-art`](../../../../.claude/skills/sotano-art/SKILL.md)); none of them is decided yet. The gate for each being is in its card ([projection cards](../projections/cards/README.md)) and in its built document.

## Every projection passes the art gate

Every projection passes the art gate ([`sotano-art`](../../../../.claude/skills/sotano-art/SKILL.md)): need, glyph (unique; see the glyph list in [the cards](../projections/cards/README.md)), four face frames, release clip, the touching moment, the meaning test, the red lines. The cards give the need, glyph, motion, release and touching moment; faces, palettes and sounds are written per creature when it is built.

## Art rulings needed

Art rulings for the owner, in the style of the art ledger §14:

1. **The living** as a fourth side for creatures: a small `living.*` ramp (moss and lichen tones, low chroma, a soft breathing glow) that must stay ΔE ≥ 15 from `cold.body`, the path hues and `life.heart` in every vision simulation.
2. **Presences on the light side**: warm only near your lantern and while listening.
3. **Floor 9's ice** and the Tree floors' ramps (the ledger has four ramps for ten floors).
4. **Omen particles and the edge marker** as new uses of the cold ramp.
5. **The art guide's rule "materialisation always at the room centre"** (§8.5) changes to "at its spawn point, announced by an omen" ([SEAL-AND-OMENS](../../built/encounters/SEAL-AND-OMENS.md)).
6. **Intention polarity** ([POLARITY](../../built/intentions/POLARITY.md)): a good intention stays round and bright (the light, yours). A negative one stays round, with a darker core and a cold rim: still yours, now dark. Never a cube, which is a hostile shot. Its exact colour is an open question (below). Converted projections keep their pattern and gain gold seams that grow with healing.

## Open questions

- **The negative intention's colour.** The owner decided that the negative intention keeps its darker colour (2026-10-27). The exact colour is still the owner's ruling.
- **The living palette.** The `living.*` ramp is a proposal; the art gate for the new creatures and projections is not passed yet ([BUILD-ORDER](../BUILD-ORDER.md), status).

## Creatures and presences: the living tell

- **Creatures** have a living tell: a slow, soft, 2 × 1 glow that breathes, never a 2 × 2 eye in a socket, which is the projections' family mark ([built/creatures](../../built/creatures/README.md)).
- **Presences** have no eyes at all until you come close, and then they look like light held in your lantern ([built/presences](../../built/presences/README.md)).

## Safety

- No projection is a body, a person, a diagnosis or a tradition; none speaks; defeat is release.
- The player is never given simulated panic. Dread's rings are mechanical and telegraphed, not a screen effect.
- Dante: images only, never his figures, never punishment of people. The circles give each zone an *image* (wind, rain, weights, marsh, tombs, burning sand, ditches, ice), not a sentence.
- Patterns that touch clinical words are named in everyday language (Numbness, not a diagnosis; Panic is the pattern, not a disorder).
- Requests from the depths are never urgent and never ask for the camera or the microphone ([REQUESTS](../presences/REQUESTS.md)).

## Words
