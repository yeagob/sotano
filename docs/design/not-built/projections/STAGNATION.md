# Stagnation

Stagnation is a projection made of mud, and it slows you on touch. It is named as one of the element projections that follow fire, water and electricity: Burnout (fire), Overwhelm (deep water), Stagnation (mud) and Spark (electricity).

## What it could be

The idea is a stillness that holds you in the marsh, the pattern of making no progress. It would be the element form of the marsh's pattern, as Burnout is the fire form of running out.

## Open questions

- **Its own pattern or a form of Sulk?** [Sulk](../../built/projections/SULK.md) is the built projection that lives in the marsh. Is Stagnation a separate projection, or Sulk's mud form?
- **Mud is not an element yet.** The element notes list mud as design. Until mud exists in the code, Stagnation has nothing to live in.
- **Its touch.** The element notes say it slows you on touch. Is that a field, or a status applied when you touch it?
