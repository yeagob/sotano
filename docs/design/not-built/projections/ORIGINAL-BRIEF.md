# The original brief: first enemies and first bosses

Status: idea (superseded). This is the first design brief for enemies and bosses, kept for history. Where it differs from the current design it is superseded: the projection cards ([cards](cards/README.md)), the bestiary's bosses ([not-built/bosses](../bosses/README.md) and [built/bosses](../../built/bosses/README.md)) and the built projection documents are the current design. Nothing here is decided.

## The first enemy table

The first prototype brief gave the enemies a Minecraft look and original names, with each enemy's behaviour and its interaction with blocks:

| Enemy | Behaviour | Interaction with blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Zombie | Walks towards you | Hits blocks in its way (slowly) |
| Skeleton | Keeps distance, shoots in arcs | Its arrows break tier 0 blocks |
| Slime | Jumps; splits on death | Bounces off blocks; sticks to walls |
| Exploder (own name) | Approaches and blows up | **Breaks blocks**: both a threat and a tool |
| Spider | Climbs walls and ceilings | Ignores gravity on blocks |
| Bat | Erratic flight | None |

The superseded parts: the Minecraft-style names and the Zombie, Skeleton, Slime, Spider and Bat types are not in the design. The projections are patterns of the mind ([STORY](../../built/story/README.md)), and their current designs are the cards. The idea that an enemy can break blocks is kept in the design of the attacks: see the cards and [built/encounters/ATTACKS](../../built/encounters/ATTACKS.md).

## The first boss idea

One boss per floor in the prototype, big and made of blocks that really break when damaged. Hitting a specific part (an arm) knocks it off as physics cubes. This is the most visually sellable part of the prototype.

The superseded part: the current bosses are the zone pools in [built/bosses](../../built/bosses/README.md). The idea that a boss is made of breakable blocks, and that its released blocks become architecture, is kept as [VOXEL-BODIES](../bosses/VOXEL-BODIES.md).
