# Zones and the growth of projections with depth

Status: proposal. The zones group the ten floors below the roots. The boss pools per zone are built ([built/bosses](../../built/bosses/README.md)); the rest of this document is design.

## The five great zones

The ten floors below group into **five great zones**. Each zone has its rock ramp (art ledger §3.2), its circle(s) of Dante (structure and images only, never his figures, no punishment of people: [CIRCLES](../../built/levels/floors/CIRCLES.md)), a **theme of patterns**, its own **unique projections**, its own **creatures**, and its own **boss pool**.

## Three ways projections grow with depth

1. **Lineages.** The first patterns you meet grow older and harder as you go down: each has **three forms** (I, II, III) that appear by zone. The form keeps its glyph's spine, its need and its family eye, and adds what the pattern becomes when left alone for years. You learn Doubt on floor 1 and meet Paralysis on floor 7: you will recognise it. The forms are on the [cards](cards/README.md); the built ones are in [built/projections](../../built/projections/README.md).
2. **Zone-unique projections.** Each zone has patterns that live only there, built around its element and theme. They never appear above their zone, and only rarely below it (as an echo, at 10 % weight on the next zone). The zone rosters are in [built/projections](../../built/projections/README.md).
3. **Rooted variants.** Any projection may spawn rooted, the same pattern more deeply held: see [ROOTED-VARIANTS](ROOTED-VARIANTS.md). Its chance by floor is in [SCALING](SCALING.md).

## Open questions

- **Lineage forms in the pool.** The table in [SCALING](SCALING.md) gives which forms each floor draws from. The code has the rosters by floor; whether the pool follows the table is not decided.
- **Echo weight.** The 10 % echo from the next zone is a proposal; no build has tested it.
