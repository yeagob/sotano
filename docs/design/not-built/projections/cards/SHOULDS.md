# The Shoulds: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/SHOULDS.md](../../../built/projections/SHOULDS.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Zone III · The Ledger Halls (floors 5–6).

**Line · pattern · need.** "I should… I should…" · rigid rules · to be good

**Path · size · husk · glyph.** Mind · mounted · pale · `⊣` (a bracket in a wall)

**Behaviour and signature.** **Mounted in walls** (spawn kind "wall"), usually in pairs facing each other: they fire **bolts straight across the room** (a Line) on a rhythm, making a crossfire grid. Break the wall block around one to free it: then it's slow and weak

**Release · touching moment.** Freed from its wall, it falls to the floor and lies there, out of its frame · It thought the rules held the walls up; out of the wall, it was the wall that held it

**Mechanic nod.** Isaac's wall-mounted shooters; Noita's turrets
