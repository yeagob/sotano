Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/PROCRASTINATION.md](../../../built/projections/PROCRASTINATION.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Lineage: Procrastination → Backlog (Will).

Form on this card: I · Procrastination.

**Line.** "Later."

**Floors.** 2–5

**Size · husk · glyph.** Medium · dark · `≡` (three crooked crates)

**Health · cost.** 18 · 2

**Behaviour.** Slow, sleepy. Hit it and it **splits** into two smaller "Laters" (two splits deep): tasks pile up. Its smallest forms are fast

**Signature.** Touch; splitting

**Elements.** Its crates burn in fire (it splits faster)

**Release.** The last crate settles into a loose crate you can carry

**Touching moment.** It wants rest, not escape; released, it gives you the box it was holding

**Mechanic nod.** Isaac's splitting blobs
