Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/NOT-YET.md](../../../built/projections/NOT-YET.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Lineage: Perfectionism → Not Yet (Body).

Form on this card: III · Not Yet.

**Line.** "Not yet. Not yet. Not yet."

**Floors.** 6–9

**Size · husk · glyph.** Tall · pale shell, dark core · `⬢`

**Health · cost.** Shell 8 plates × 6, core 24 · 4

**Behaviour.** Same, and it **repairs one plate every 4 s** while its core is hidden: you must keep the pressure on, or attack the core through a gap

**Signature.** Touch + repair

**Release.** Same, and it kneels, whole and visibly mended

**Touching moment.** It kept repairing because it thought broken meant finished; the seams say otherwise
