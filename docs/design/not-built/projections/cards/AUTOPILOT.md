# Autopilot: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/AUTOPILOT.md](../../../built/projections/AUTOPILOT.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Zone I · The Cellar of Habits (floors 1–2).

**Line · pattern · need.** "Same as always." · living by routine · to save effort

**Path · size · husk · glyph.** Will · medium · dark · `⟲`

**Behaviour and signature.** **Walks a fixed loop** through the room and ignores you, until you **cross its path**: then it charges along its loop (a Line, telegraphed by its loop lighting up as a dashed trail). Put a block on its loop and it stops, confused, and turns

**Release · touching moment.** It stops at the block, looks up, and walks somewhere new before it dissolves · It wasn't against you; it just never looked up

**Mechanic nod.** Isaac's patrolling enemies
