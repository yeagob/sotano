# The Tribunal: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/TRIBUNAL.md](../../../built/projections/TRIBUNAL.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Lineage: The Lectern → The Margin → The Tribunal (Mind).

Form on this card: III · The Tribunal.

**Line.** "We've all agreed."

**Floors.** 6–9

**Size · husk · glyph.** Tall ×3 · pale · `Π` (three posts under one bar)

**Health · cost.** 3 × 24 · 4

**Behaviour.** Three lecterns that rise together and **fire in sequence** (left, centre, right), so the volleys sweep. **Only their backs are vulnerable** (the ✗ painted there): flank them, or knock them round with a thrown cube

**Signature.** **Line** volley, three bolts in a row across the room

**Elements.** Fire burns their pages: a Tribunal near flames loses its marks

**Release.** The three turn to face each other, bow, and their pages fall together, blank

**Touching moment.** It was never three voices. It was one, repeating itself until it sounded like everyone
