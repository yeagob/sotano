# Pretence: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/PRETENCE.md](../../../built/projections/PRETENCE.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Zone IV · The Furnace (floors 7–8).

**Line · pattern · need.** "I'm fine." · the mask · to be accepted

**Path · size · husk · glyph.** Emotion · medium · pale (mask) over dark · `◠` (a smile)

**Behaviour and signature.** A **mimic**: disguises itself as something good: a pickup, a pedestal's glow, an creature's silhouette. Within 2 blocks it opens (0.5 s, the smile splits) and bites. Its mask is hard; its back is hollow

**Release · touching moment.** The mask comes off and lies face up on the floor; behind it, a small honest face, briefly · It smiled so no one would ask; released, it doesn't have to

**Mechanic nod.** Isaac's mimics
