# Undertow: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/UNDERTOW.md](../../../built/projections/UNDERTOW.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Zone II · The Flooded Rooms (floors 3–4).

**Line · pattern · need.** "It's too much." · being overwhelmed · to be held

**Path · size · husk · glyph.** Emotion · medium · dark · `≈`

**Behaviour and signature.** A **swimmer** that lives in deep water. It rises as a **wave** and crosses the room as a wall of water (a Sweep): get above it (a gallery, a placed block, a jump) or be carried. Out of water it is slow and small

**Release · touching moment.** The wave lies down flat and still · It pulled you under because it was drowning too

**Mechanic nod.** Noita's liquid creatures
