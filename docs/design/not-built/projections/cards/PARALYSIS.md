# Paralysis: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/PARALYSIS.md](../../../built/projections/PARALYSIS.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Lineage: Doubt → Second-Guessing → Paralysis (Mind).

Form on this card: III · Paralysis.

**Line.** "Don't move."

**Floors.** 6–9

**Size · husk · glyph.** Tall · dark · `⊥` (a hook driven into the ground)

**Health · cost.** 50 · 3

**Behaviour.** Doesn't walk. Roots itself where it rises and casts a **tether** (a Pull): a slow cold thread that drags you toward it while you stand in its line of sight

**Signature.** **Pull**: motes stream toward it for 0.6 s, then the thread fastens. Break it by stepping behind a block (line of sight) or by hitting the sack

**Elements.** Water loosens its root: in water it can be pushed

**Release.** The hook pulls out of the ground; it takes one step, its first

**Touching moment.** It was never holding you: it was holding the ground, because the ground was the only thing that never moved
