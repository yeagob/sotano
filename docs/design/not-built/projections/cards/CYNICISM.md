# Cynicism: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/CYNICISM.md](../../../built/projections/CYNICISM.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Zone V · The Frozen Bottom (floor 9).

**Line · pattern · need.** "What's the point?" · giving up before trying · to not be disappointed

**Path · size · husk · glyph.** Mind · medium · dark · `⊘`

**Behaviour and signature.** Projects a **Field that drains intentions**: within its ring your intentions lose their light and drop dim at half range. Stay out of the ring and hit it from range, or carry the fight to it with thrown cubes (matter isn't affected)

**Release · touching moment.** Its ring shrinks to a dot and goes out · It decided nothing mattered before anything could; released, one thing did
