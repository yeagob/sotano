# Gloom: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/GLOOM.md](../../../built/projections/GLOOM.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Zone II · The Flooded Rooms (floors 3–4).

**Line · pattern · need.** "Everything is grey." · a heavy mood · to be left alone

**Path · size · husk · glyph.** Emotion · medium flyer · dark · `☁`

**Behaviour and signature.** A cloud that hovers under the ceiling (spawn kind "ceiling") and **rains** cold drops in a wide circle (many small Lobs): you must keep moving. Can't come down; reach it with arcs, from galleries, or by building up

**Release · touching moment.** The rain thins and stops; a patch of floor dries · It wasn't trying to drown anyone; it was carrying more water than a cloud should

**Mechanic nod.** The third circle's cold rain
