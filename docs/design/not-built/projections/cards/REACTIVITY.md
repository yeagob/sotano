# Reactivity: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/REACTIVITY.md](../../../built/projections/REACTIVITY.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Zone III · The Ledger Halls (floors 5–6).

**Line · pattern · need.** "How dare—!" · reacting in anger · to be respected

**Path · size · husk · glyph.** Body · medium · dark · `➤`

**Behaviour and signature.** A **charger**: when you line up with it on an axis, it scrapes the floor (0.7 s, a dashed line drawn ahead of it) and **charges across the room**, smashing soft blocks in its way. It staggers 1.5 s when it hits something hard: a placed block, stone, a wall. That's the window

**Release · touching moment.** It stops short, a block from you, and lowers its head · It was trying to protect something; it never stopped long enough to see you weren't the threat

**Mechanic nod.** Isaac's chargers
