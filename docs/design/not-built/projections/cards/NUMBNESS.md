# Numbness: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/NUMBNESS.md](../../../built/projections/NUMBNESS.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Zone V · The Frozen Bottom (floor 9).

**Line · pattern · need.** "I don't feel anything." · shutting feeling off · to not hurt

**Path · size · husk · glyph.** Emotion · tall · pale (rime) · `❄`

**Behaviour and signature.** Slow. **Freezes** what it touches: water turns to ice (walkable), loose cubes freeze in place, and its Field slows your casting. Your intentions **thaw** what it froze

**Release · touching moment.** The rime melts off it, drop by drop; underneath it is warm-coloured for one frame, then gone · It froze everything so nothing could hurt; the thaw hurts, and it is also the first thing it has felt
