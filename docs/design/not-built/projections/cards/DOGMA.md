# Dogma: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/DOGMA.md](../../../built/projections/DOGMA.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Zone III · The Ledger Halls (floors 5–6).

**Line · pattern · need.** "It has always been this way." · a belief set in stone · certainty

**Path · size · husk · glyph.** Mind · tall · pale · `▣` (a tablet)

**Behaviour and signature.** A monolith that rises at the room's heart and **sets one rule** inside its ring (a Field, its glyph on its face): *no jumping*, *no casting*, *no carrying*, or *no standing still*. Breaking the rule hurts. **Each 25 % of its health it changes the rule**, so you adapt

**Release · touching moment.** Its tablet cracks down the middle; the glyph is gone · It held one rule because one rule was simpler than a world; under it, the stone is blank

**Mechanic nod.** — (a rule-changer, our own)
