# Craving: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/CRAVING.md](../../../built/projections/CRAVING.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Zone I · The Cellar of Habits (floors 1–2).

**Line · pattern · need.** "Just one more." · wanting that never fills · to feel full

**Path · size · husk · glyph.** Body · medium · dark · `∪` (an open cup)

**Behaviour and signature.** Circles you at a distance, and its open body **pulls** (a wind) everything loose toward it: cubes, bombs, pickups, you. When something reaches it, it swallows it (pickups come back on release). Then it lunges

**Release · touching moment.** It closes, full for the first time, and gives back what it swallowed · It wanted to be full; it only needed to stop pulling

**Mechanic nod.** Noita's suction, the circle of winds
