# Alarm: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/ALARM.md](../../../built/projections/ALARM.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Lineage: Panic → Alarm → Dread (Will).

Form on this card: II · Alarm.

**Line.** "Everyone, something is wrong!"

**Floors.** 3–6

**Size · husk · glyph.** Small · dark · `✴` (more rays)

**Health · cost.** 14 · 2

**Behaviour.** Rushes like Panic, but its swell **sets off every Panic and Alarm within 4 blocks** after a 0.4 s delay: a chain, but a readable one (each swell is its own telegraph)

**Signature.** Burst, chained

**Elements.** Same; one in water calms the chain

**Release.** Its rays fold in, one by one

**Touching moment.** It warns everyone because nobody warned it
