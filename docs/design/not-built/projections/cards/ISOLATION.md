# Isolation: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/ISOLATION.md](../../../built/projections/ISOLATION.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Zone V · The Frozen Bottom (floor 9).

**Line · pattern · need.** "Leave me alone." · withdrawing · to be safe from people

**Path · size · husk · glyph.** Emotion · medium · dark · `□` with a dot inside

**Behaviour and signature.** Raises **walls of ice** across the room that split it into cells, alone with you in one. It doesn't attack much; the cells are the danger (with other projections in them). Break the ice to open them

**Release · touching moment.** Its last wall melts and it stands in the open · It built walls to be safe; it was the loneliest thing down here
