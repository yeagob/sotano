# Shortcut: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/SHORTCUT.md](../../../built/projections/SHORTCUT.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Zone IV · The Furnace (floors 7–8).

**Line · pattern · need.** "There's an easier way." · cutting corners · to not have to try

**Path · size · husk · glyph.** Will · small · dark · `↗`

**Behaviour and signature.** Appears **next to locked doors and vaults**, offering a way in (it opens a crack in the wall). Walk through and the crack closes behind you: you're in a small sealed pocket with a wave. Hit it before it opens the crack and it flees

**Release · touching moment.** It points, honestly this time, at the real door · It wasn't lying about wanting to help; it just didn't know the long way was the way

**Mechanic nod.** Noita's trick rooms
