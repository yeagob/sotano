# Rumination: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/RUMINATION.md](../../../built/projections/RUMINATION.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Zone II · The Flooded Rooms (floors 3–4).

**Line · pattern · need.** "Again. Again. Again." · the thought that loops · to finally solve it

**Path · size · husk · glyph.** Mind · long (a worm of 6 segments) · dark · `∞`

**Behaviour and signature.** A **burrower** that tunnels through the rock around the room on a **fixed loop** (you see cracks ripple across the walls and hear it travel in 3D). Bursts out across the room (a Line), bites, dives back in. Its loop is the same every time: learn it, and wait with a bomb

**Release · touching moment.** It surfaces and lies across the floor, a closed ring that opens · It went round and round because it thought one more lap would solve it

**Mechanic nod.** Noita's burrowing worms
