# Noise: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/NOISE.md](../../../built/projections/NOISE.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Lineage: Distraction → Noise (Mind).

Form on this card: II · Noise.

**Line.** "Ooh! And that! And that!"

**Floors.** 4–9

**Size · husk · glyph.** A swarm of 5–8 tiny ones · dark · `⁘`

**Health · cost.** 4 each · 3

**Behaviour.** A swarm that is **drawn to your intentions**: it flies into them and swallows them (each tiny one eats one intention, then is released). It flocks around lit lamps and cleared-room light

**Signature.** **Intention-eating**; it makes you choose when to cast

**Elements.** A flame draws the whole swarm away for 3 s

**Release.** They settle one by one on the nearest ledge and go quiet, a row of small cold eyes closing

**Touching moment.** Every one of them was a small wish to feel something; quiet, they were just tired
