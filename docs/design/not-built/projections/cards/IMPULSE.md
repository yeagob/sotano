# Impulse: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/IMPULSE.md](../../../built/projections/IMPULSE.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Zone I · The Cellar of Habits (floors 1–2).

**Line · pattern · need.** "Now!" · acting before thinking · to not miss out

**Path · size · husk · glyph.** Body · small · dark · `↯`

**Behaviour and signature.** A **leaper**: crouches 0.6 s and leaps across the room to where you *will* be (it leads the target); a cold ring marks the landing. Its landing cracks soft floors

**Release · touching moment.** It lands softly, once, and waits · It was always afraid of being too late; released, it isn't in a hurry

**Mechanic nod.** Isaac's leapers
