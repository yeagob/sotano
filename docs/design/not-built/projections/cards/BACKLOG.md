# Backlog: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/BACKLOG.md](../../../built/projections/BACKLOG.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Lineage: Procrastination → Backlog (Will).

Form on this card: II–III · Backlog.

**Line.** "I'll get to all of it. Later."

**Floors.** 5–9

**Size · husk · glyph.** Tall · dark · `☰` with a leaning top (a stack about to topple)

**Health · cost.** 60 · 4

**Behaviour.** Doesn't chase. **Grows a crate every 8 s** while untouched (up to 6). Throws its top crate across the room in a Lob that lands as a loose crate

**Signature.** **Lob** (crates) + growth

**Elements.** Fire on its stack makes it collapse (all crates fall loose)

**Release.** The stack sits down, crate by crate, into a neat pile you can carry from

**Touching moment.** Everything it put off was waiting for one first step; the pile was always smaller than it looked

**Mechanic nod.** Noita's material-spawning enemies
