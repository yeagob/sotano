# Second-Guessing: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/SECOND-GUESSING.md](../../../built/projections/SECOND-GUESSING.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Lineage: Doubt → Second-Guessing → Paralysis (Mind).

Form on this card: II · Second-Guessing.

**Line.** "Wait, are you *really* sure?"

**Floors.** 3–7

**Size · husk · glyph.** Medium · dark · `??` (two hooks)

**Health · cost.** 26 · 2

**Behaviour.** Walks, and at each pause **drops a stone** from its sack: a loose cube that blocks the path (rubble you can use). Changes direction after every pause

**Signature.** Touch + obstacles

**Elements.** Same; stones dropped in water sink and raise the floor (bridges)

**Release.** Its sack splits; it lets the stones fall and stands up

**Touching moment.** It leaves the stones behind so you won't walk on; released, it lets go of them
