# Comparison: the card

Status: proposal. The card is the design. The built side of this projection is in [built/projections/COMPARISON.md](../../../built/projections/COMPARISON.md), and each card field that is not in the code stays here.

Lineage: Comparison → The Hall of Mirrors (Emotion).

Form on this card: II · Comparison.

**Line.** "They're ahead of you."

**Floors.** 3–6

**Size · husk · glyph.** Tall · dark (obsidian) · `▯` (a slab)

**Health · cost.** 30 · 3

**Behaviour.** Always a little taller than you. It **copies your intentions back**: what you cast, it casts at you, mirrored and cold (your practices' shape, never their warmth)

**Signature.** **Bolt** (your own shot, mirrored)

**Elements.** Water fogs it: in steam it can't copy

**Release.** It shrinks to exactly your height and stands beside you, then dissolves

**Touching moment.** It wanted to know where you stand; released, it stands next to you, not ahead, not behind

**Mechanic nod.** Isaac's mirroring enemies; enemy-owned hooks (ENTITIES M2)
