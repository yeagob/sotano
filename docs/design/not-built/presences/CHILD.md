# The Child

Status: **decided** (the owner approved the Elder and the Child as presences on 2026-10-27; names and lines are still open).

A dweller of play, and of permission to make something badly. It waits in the Cellar of Habits, in a room of loose cubes and unfinished things. Its shape is a loose cluster of cubes that moves like a small animal. It is always joyful; it never cries (the art guide's red line).

## What the design asks

- **Build.** It asks the player to build something with it while it watches: place three blocks.
- **Lesson.** The build becomes a Lesson from the player's Body or Emotion path. Making transforms the maker.
- **Request.** In the idle window, it asks the player to draw something, anything, never judged (see [Requests](REQUESTS.md)).
- **Agreement.** Three loose cubes of different materials, to build a small tower (see [Agreements](AGREEMENTS.md)).
- **Covenant.** It gives no piece of the Covenant (the design's four pieces come from other Presences).

## Open questions

- **The path.** A build could be from either the Body or the Emotion path. Decide the rule for which one.
- **Names and lines.** Open for the owner's ear.
- **Joy.** The red line is that the Child is never crying. A build that fails must still feel like play. Check the line text when it exists.
