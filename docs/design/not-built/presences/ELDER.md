# The Elder

Status: **partly built**. The Elder waits on one seeded floor from floor 1 and gives a lesson of the path chosen on a choice card ([built/presences/ELDER.md](../../built/presences/ELDER.md)). The parts below are not built.

A dweller of what was taught, and what the player chose to keep of it. In the design it waits in a quiet vault of the Ledger Halls.

## What is not built

- **The Ledger Halls and the vault.** The design puts it in a vault with no projections. The build puts it in a quiet room of a seeded floor.
- **The Reading.** The design gives a Reading of the chosen belief: what it may have meant, and what it cost. The build gives two fixed lines.
- **Agreement.** A page of ledger stone and the essence of a rule (see [Agreements](AGREEMENTS.md)).
- **Covenant.** It gives the Page, one of the four pieces (see [Covenant](COVENANT.md)).

## Open questions

- **The vault rule.** Vaults hold a Boundary in the build. Which rule holds for the Elder's room?
- **Names and lines.** Proposed; the owner's ear is open (design section 13).
- **Lesson weights.** Three in four quality one, one in four quality two (a proposal, pending owner ruling).
