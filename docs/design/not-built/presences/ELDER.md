# The Elder

Status: **decided** (the owner approved the Elder and the Child as presences on 2026-10-27; names and lines are still open).

A dweller of what was taught, and what the player chose to keep of it. It waits in a quiet vault of the Ledger Halls, with old tablets and no projections. Its shape is a tall light of ledger stone with no face until the player comes close.

## What the design asks

- **Question.** It asks what the player was taught, and which of it still holds.
- **Choice.** The player answers by choosing one of their paths.
- **Reading.** It gives a Reading of that belief: what it may have meant, and what it cost.
- **Lesson.** It gives a Lesson from the path the player chose.
- **Honesty.** It never tells the player what is true.
- **Agreement.** A page of ledger stone and the essence of a rule (see [Agreements](AGREEMENTS.md)).
- **Covenant.** It gives the Page, one of the four pieces (see [Covenant](COVENANT.md)).

## Open questions

- **The vault.** The design puts it in a vault with no projections. The build puts a Boundary in every vault. Which rule holds?
- **Names and lines.** Open for the owner's ear (design section 13).
- **Paths.** The choice is among the player's paths (Body, Mind, Emotion, Will). The Lesson and the Reading depend on the path system, which is built only as the item tags.
