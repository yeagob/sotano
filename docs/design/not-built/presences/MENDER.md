# The Mender

Status: **proposal**.

A dweller of repair, the kintsugi of the depths. It waits in the vaults of Zones III and IV. It repairs what was broken, and what was broken is one of the player's released projections.

## What the design asks

- **Integration.** The Mender integrates one projection the player released on this floor. It comes back as an ally, with gold seams, for the floor (see [polarity](../../built/encounters/POLARITY.md) for allies).
- **Agreement.** Three shards of different essences, to mend a vessel (floors 5 to 8; see [Agreements](AGREEMENTS.md)).
- **Gift.** The tier 3 gift Mender's Thread (see [Gifts](GIFTS.md)).
- **Covenant.** It gives the Thread, one of the four pieces (see [Covenant](COVENANT.md)).

## Open questions

- **Released is dead.** A released projection dissolves in the build. The Mender needs the projection to come back. Does it come back from its parts, or is the integration of a converted projection?
- **Vault.** Vaults hold a Boundary in the build. The Mender's vault and the Boundary's sanctuary need one rule.
- **Gold.** The gold seams mark what was mended (the design's kintsugi). Keep the same mark for an ally.
