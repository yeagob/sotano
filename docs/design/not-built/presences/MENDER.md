# The Mender

Status: **partly built**. The Mender is in the code: it comes to the first vault the player opens on its floor (3 to 5), and asks for one part in hand for a gift. The built part is in [built/presences/MENDER.md](../../built/presences/MENDER.md).

A dweller of repair, the kintsugi of the depths. In the design it waits in the vaults of Zones III and IV. It repairs what was broken, and what was broken is one of the player's released projections.

## What the design asks

- **Integration.** The Mender integrates one projection the player released on this floor. It comes back as an ally, with gold seams, for the floor (see [polarity](../../built/encounters/POLARITY.md) for allies). Not built: a released projection dissolves for good today, so there is nothing to bring back.
- **Agreement.** Three shards of different essences, to mend a vessel (floors 5 to 8; see [Agreements](AGREEMENTS.md)). Not built. The built request is one part in hand.
- **Gift.** The tier 3 gift Mender's Thread (see [Gifts](GIFTS.md)). Not built as an item: the built gift is a practice of quality three or a special heart.
- **Covenant.** It gives the Thread, one of the four pieces (see [Covenant](COVENANT.md)). Not built.

## Open questions

- **Released is dead.** A released projection dissolves in the build. The Mender needs the projection to come back. Does it come back from its parts, or is the integration of a converted projection? Until a ruling, the Mender takes a part instead (proposed, pending owner ruling).
- **Vault rule.** Built as a default (proposed, pending owner ruling): the Mender comes to the first vault the player opens on floors 3 to 5, once the vault's Boundary has left. The design named Zones III and IV, but vaults end at floor 5 today, so a levels ruling is needed before a vault on a deeper floor.
- **Gold.** The gold seams mark what was mended (the design's kintsugi). Keep the same mark for an ally. Art need: the gold seams on the body, a shape of its own (not drawn; listed for the art agent).
- **Weights and lines.** The gift's weights (three in four a practice of quality three) and the words are proposed, pending owner ruling.
