# The Mender

Status: **partly built**. The Mender is in the code: it comes to the first vault the player opens on its floor (3 to 5), asks for two parts of different kinds, gives a gift and its Thread, and brings back one released projection as an ally. The built part is in [built/presences/MENDER.md](../../built/presences/MENDER.md).

A dweller of repair, the kintsugi of the depths. In the design it waits in the vaults of Zones III and IV. It repairs what was broken, and what was broken is one of the player's released projections.

## What the design asks

- **Integration.** Built (owner, #228, D9; see the built document): the last projection released on the Mender's floor comes back as an ally, once, with the gold seam. Still open: "for the floor" (the built ally stays as any converted ally does; it is not removed when the player goes down).
- **Agreement.** Three shards of different essences, to mend a vessel (floors 5 to 8; see [Agreements](AGREEMENTS.md)). Built as a multi-part card of two parts of different kinds on floors 3 to 5 (owner, #228, D11). Three shards wait for a Mender on floors 5 to 8 (D1: vaults end at floor 5).
- **Gift.** The tier 3 gift Mender's Thread (see [Gifts](GIFTS.md)). Built as an item (owner, #228, D10), beside the practice of quality three or special heart.
- **Covenant.** It gives the Thread, one of the four pieces (see [Covenant](COVENANT.md)). Built.

## Open questions

- **Integration.** Ruled by the owner (#228, D9): built.
- **Released is dead.** Settled by the build of D9: a release stays final everywhere but at the Mender, which brings back the last projection released on its own floor (not one rebuilt from its parts, and not a converted one, which is already an ally).
- **Vault rule.** Decided by agent (README D1): vaults stay on floors 1 to 5 and the Mender on 3 to 5; the levels are not changed. Built as a default (proposed, pending owner ruling): the Mender comes to the first vault the player opens on floors 3 to 5, once the vault's Boundary has left. The design named Zones III and IV, but vaults end at floor 5 today, so a levels ruling is needed before a vault on a deeper floor.
- **Gold.** The gold seams mark what was mended (the design's kintsugi). The mended ally's mark is built (DECISIONS.md §14.4: a gold glow and a slow gold mote). Still owed: the gold seams on the Mender's own body, a shape of its own (DECISIONS.md §14.2).
- **Weights and lines.** The gift's weights (three in four a practice of quality three) and the words are proposed, pending owner ruling.
