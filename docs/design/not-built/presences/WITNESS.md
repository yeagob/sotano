# The Witness

Status: **partly built**. The Witness is in the code on the ninth floor, with a yes-or-no gift; the built part is in [built/presences/WITNESS.md](../../built/presences/WITNESS.md).

A dweller of seeing without judging. In the design it waits in the silence below the ninth floor, the last conversation before the loop back to heaven. It gives no reward but the conversation itself: a Reading of the run.

## What the design asks

- **Reading.** The Witness reads the player's run: their hexagram (a lore element to check against the lore library), the patterns they released, and the ones that held them. Not built.
- **Agreement.** It asks for one part of each pattern the player released. This is the last agreement of the run (see [Agreements](AGREEMENTS.md)). Not built.
- **Covenant.** It gives the Seal, one of the four pieces of the Covenant (see [Covenant](COVENANT.md)). Not built: the built gift is a tier 2 practice or a special heart.
- **Shape.** Abstract, never a human figure. Not built (it uses the Listener's model).

## Open questions

- **The Silence.** The place below floor 9 is not built. The Witness stands in a quiet room of floor 9 until it is (the loop to heaven is a milestone, L4). Proposed, pending owner ruling; a ruling on the place is needed before the Silence is a floor.
- **The hexagram.** A reading by hexagram needs a source check before any text is written (see the [lore library](../../../lore/README.md)).
- **Parts.** A part of each pattern is only possible if the player has released that pattern. A run that released none needs a line of its own.
- **Gift.** The built weights (three practices of quality two to one special heart) and the Seal's place in the Covenant are proposed, pending owner ruling.
