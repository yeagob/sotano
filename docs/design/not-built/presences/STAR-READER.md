# The Star-reader

Status: **partly built**. The Star-reader is in the code, in a quiet room of one seeded Tree floor (1 to 5), with a Take or Decline gift. The built part is in [built/presences/STAR-READER.md](../../built/presences/STAR-READER.md).

A dweller of seeing the whole. In the design it lives on the Leaves at the top of the Tree, and it reads the player's practices as constellations. It shows the teleport doors.

## What the design asks

- **Reading.** It reads the player's practices as constellations. Not built: the built reading is one line in words, not a view of the held practices.
- **Doors.** It shows the teleport doors on the star map. Not built: the star map and the doors do not exist yet.
- **Place.** The Leaves at the top of the giant tree. Not built: the Leaves are a story of the tree, not a room, and an enemy needs a room. The build places it on a Tree floor instead (proposed, pending owner ruling).

## Open questions

- **The Tree.** The Leaves are not built as a place a presence can stand (milestone L3 of the Tree, not built).
- **The map.** The star map does not exist yet, and neither do the teleport doors.
- **Constellations.** The shape of a constellation is a view of the items the player holds. Decide whether it is a map or a picture. Art need: an abstract shape of stars for the body and for the view (not drawn; listed for the art agent).
- **Gift.** The built weights (two in four a practice of quality one, one in four quality two, one in four a special heart) and the floors 1 to 5 are proposed, pending owner ruling.
