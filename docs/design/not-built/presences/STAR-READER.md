# The Star-reader

Status: **proposal**.

A dweller of seeing the whole. It lives on the Leaves at the top of the Tree, and it reads the player's practices as constellations. It shows the teleport doors.

## What the design asks

- **Reading.** It reads the player's practices as constellations.
- **Doors.** It shows the teleport doors on the star map.

## Open questions

- **The Tree.** The Leaves are not built (milestone L3 of the Tree, not built).
- **The map.** The star map does not exist yet, and neither do the teleport doors.
- **Constellations.** The shape of a constellation is a view of the items the player holds. Decide whether it is a map or a picture.
