# The Trickster

Status: **decided** (the owner approved the Trickster's deals on 2026-10-27; the deals' costs are still to be set).

A messenger of the trickster archetype: the shortcut that teaches. It can be met from floor 3, in a dead-end room. It offers a deal with a plain cost, and it says the cost plainly. The player may decline; then it laughs and is gone.

## What the design asks

- **Deal.** A trade with a plain cost: a heart container for a quality-three practice, or the player's bombs for keys.
- **Decline.** The player may refuse. Refusing costs nothing.
- **Agreement.** A trade: a heart for a practice (see [Agreements](AGREEMENTS.md)).
- **Gift.** The tier 2 gift Trickster's Coin (see [Gifts](GIFTS.md)).

## Open questions

- **Heart containers.** The build's hearts and their containers are not described here; check that a heart container exists before the deal names one.
- **Quality three.** The practice quality scale exists in the build. Confirm the price for each quality.
- **Honesty.** The cost is said plainly and never hidden. Keep it that way in every line.
