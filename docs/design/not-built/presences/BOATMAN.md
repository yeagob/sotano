# The Boatman

Status: **partly built**. The Boatman is in the code, by the drowned nave of Zone II, with a toll of one block and a ferry; the built part is in [built/presences/BOATMAN.md](../../built/presences/BOATMAN.md).

A dweller of letting go to move on. It lives in Zone II, by the deepest water. It ferries the player across deep water to a pocket that is otherwise unreachable, if the player leaves one thing on the shore: a block, a bomb or a key.

## What the design asks

- **Ferry.** Across deep water, to a pocket that cannot be reached otherwise. Built as a carry to the island of the drowned nave (a ferry, not a swim); the island is reachable on foot by stepping stones, so the pocket of its own is not built.
- **Toll.** One thing left on the shore: a block, a bomb or a key. Built: a block only. A bomb and a key are counts, not items in hand, and their toll is not built.
- **Agreement.** A block of deep stone, left on the shore, from floors 2 to 4 (see [Agreements](AGREEMENTS.md)). Not built.
- **Gift.** A tier 2 gift. Built: three in four a practice of quality two, one in four one special heart (proposed, pending owner ruling).

## Open questions

- **Two ferries.** The Ferryback creature also carries the player across water in the design (see [Water and ferry](../creatures/WATER-AND-FERRY.md)). The Boatman carries in the build; the Ferryback does not.
- **Toll.** The floor's boss has a toll too (see [Fight flow](../../built/bosses/FIGHT-FLOW.md#8-the-toll)). Two tolls must read as different.
- **Pocket.** Decided by agent (README D12): the nave's island stays the pocket, the template is not changed. A pocket that the nave's island cannot give. A levels ruling is needed before a room of its own is drawn (the levels skill must be asked).
- **Placement.** A run meets the Boatman only when floors 3 and 4 hold a drowned nave (pending a ruling on a fixed Zone II room).
