# The Boatman

Status: **proposal**.

A dweller of letting go to move on. It lives in Zone II, by the deepest water. It ferries the player across deep water to a pocket that is otherwise unreachable, if the player leaves one thing on the shore: a block, a bomb or a key.

## What the design asks

- **Ferry.** Across deep water, to a pocket that cannot be reached otherwise.
- **Toll.** One thing left on the shore: a block, a bomb or a key.
- **Agreement.** A block of deep stone, left on the shore, from floors 2 to 4 (see [Agreements](AGREEMENTS.md)).
- **Gift.** A tier 2 gift. The design lists the Boatman among the givers of the tier 2 gifts (see [Gifts](GIFTS.md)).

## Open questions

- **Two ferries.** The Ferryback creature also carries the player across water in the design (see [Water and ferry](../creatures/WATER-AND-FERRY.md)). Decide whether the creature or the Boatman carries.
- **Toll.** The floor's boss has a toll too (see [Fight flow](../../built/bosses/FIGHT-FLOW.md#8-the-toll)). Two tolls must read as different.
- **Pocket.** The pocket must exist. The room templates decide where; the levels skill must be asked.
