# The Gardener

Status: **decided** (defined in the code, not placed). The owner ruled on 2026-10-29 that the tutorial floor holds no presence, so the Gardener waits for an agreement that asks for a real-world action.

A dweller of care, patience and cultivation. In the design, the Gardener is the tutorial's voice on floor 0: it teaches movement, intentions, blocks and doors in its rooms. In later runs it remembers how far the player went, and says so kindly.

## What is built

The Gardener is defined: an immortal, positive-only presence, a bone-coloured figure with its arms held out and a few living tones on its hands. Its two lines are localised: "Walk slowly. The roots remember where you have been." and "Nothing here has to be finished today." Nothing places it, so it never appears.

## What the design asks

- **Teaching.** On floor 0, the Gardener shows movement, intentions, blocks and doors in its rooms.
- **Memory.** In later runs, it greets the player by how far they went, if the memory system exists (see [Memory](MEMORY.md)).
- **Agreement.** An early agreement asks for a root and a tuft of moss, to plant something where the player was (see [Agreements](AGREEMENTS.md)).

## Open questions

- **Placement.** The design (2026-10-27) places it on floor 0; the owner (2026-10-29) ruled that the tutorial floor holds none. Which room, and on which floor, does it wait in now?
- **Teaching without a presence.** The tutorial teaches the same things through its own rooms. Keep the teaching there, and give the Gardener only the memory?
