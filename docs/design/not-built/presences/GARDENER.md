# The Gardener

Status: **partly built**. The Gardener is placed on one seeded floor from floor 1, in a quiet room, and asks for a flesh part ([built/presences/GARDENER.md](../../built/presences/GARDENER.md)). The parts below are not built.

A dweller of care, patience and cultivation. In the design it is the tutorial's voice; the owner ruled on 2026-10-29 that the tutorial floor holds no presence, so the teaching stays in the tutorial's own rooms.

## What is not built

- **Teaching.** The Gardener shows movement, intentions, blocks and doors. The tutorial teaches them in its own rooms; no Gardener teaches there.
- **Memory.** In later runs it greets the player by how far they went (see [Memory](MEMORY.md)).
- **Agreement.** An early agreement asks for a root and a tuft of moss, to plant something where the player was (see [Agreements](AGREEMENTS.md)). The build asks for a flesh part instead, with no return (proposed, pending owner ruling).

## Open questions

- **Gift.** The build gives nothing for a flesh part. Does the Gardener give a seed or a memory back? Proposed: nothing, pending owner ruling.
- **Teaching without a presence.** Keep the teaching in the tutorial, and give the Gardener only the memory?
