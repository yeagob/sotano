# The Cartographer

Status: **partly built**. The Cartographer waits on one seeded floor from floor 1 and marks the nearest vault or boss in words, on a choice card ([built/presences/CARTOGRAPHER.md](../../built/presences/CARTOGRAPHER.md)). The parts below are not built.

A dweller of knowing where you stand. In the design it lives in the central emanation of each Tree floor, and its marks go on a star map.

## What is not built

- **The Tree.** Its floors (L1 to L5) are not built; the Cartographer waits in a quiet room of a seeded floor.
- **The map.** Built: its mark goes on the star map, as four brass corners round the marked room ([STAR-MAP](../../built/levels/giant-tree/STAR-MAP.md)). The words on the card stay.
- **The hidden pocket.** The design lets the player mark a hidden pocket too. The build marks a vault or a boss only; the pocket is not built.
- **Agreement.** One ore from every zone the player crossed, to chart the floor (see [Agreements](AGREEMENTS.md)). The build asks for one ore, of the zone it waits in, and charts the floor with it (README D13g); the ore of every zone crossed needs a card of up to five objects (D11 caps a card at three) and a count of zones crossed.

## Decided by agent (pending owner ruling)

- **Materials.** Owner ruling D13 (2026-11-19): the ores of the zones are built. The Cartographer charts the floor for the ore of its zone (README D13g).
- **The star map.** Built after the owner's ruling (README D15). A chart for the ore marks both rooms on it.
