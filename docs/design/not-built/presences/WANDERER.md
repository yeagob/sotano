# The Wanderer

Status: **proposal**.

A messenger of lessons on the way. It can be met anywhere from floor 1, in a corridor, once or twice in a run. It says one line of wisdom tied to a practice the player holds or a projection they have just released, and it walks on through a wall.

## What the design asks

- **Appearance.** Once or twice per run, in a corridor from floor 1.
- **Line.** One line of wisdom, tied to a practice held or a projection just released.
- **Exit.** It walks on through a wall and is gone.
- **Agreement.** The mind of the first pattern the player met, to learn what it wanted (see [Agreements](AGREEMENTS.md)).
- **Gift.** A tier 1 gift (see [Gifts](GIFTS.md)).

## Open questions

- **Wisdom.** Every line is a paraphrase of a credited source in the lore library, never a quote from a real person.
- **Through a wall.** The wall is passed without a ghost-like effect. Check that the Wanderer is never in a wall the player must break.
- **Frequency.** Once or twice per run is a rule; the spawn, the corridor and the seed are not built.
