# Mine cart and rails

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A mine cart carries what the player cannot. Load it, kick it, and it rolls along the rails and dumps at the end, or into a projection. Momentum carries what the player cannot lift.

## Design

- **What it does:** rolls along rails after a kick, then dumps its load at the end of the rails.
- **Verb:** load cubes into the cart; kick it.
- **Meaning:** momentum. Some things are carried by a method, not force.
- **Vertical combat:** a cart dropped onto a projection is a way to fight from a height ([VERTICALITY](VERTICALITY.md)).
- **Tier:** T2.
