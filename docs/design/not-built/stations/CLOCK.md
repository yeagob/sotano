# Clock

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A clock swings a pendulum, and things on its platform move only on the beat. Riding the pendulum is the Kybalion's rhythm ([CONCEPTS](../../../lore/CONCEPTS.md)).

## Design

- **What it does:** things on the clock's platform move only on the beat of the pendulum.
- **Verb:** step onto the platform and time the moves.
- **Meaning:** rhythm, the pendulum's swing.
- **Tier:** T1.
