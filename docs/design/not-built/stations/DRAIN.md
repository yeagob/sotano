# Drain

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A drain takes water and sends it to the room below. What the player releases goes somewhere.

## Design

- **What it does:** swallows water and sends it to the room below. A flood from above fills the room under the player.
- **Verb:** water enters the drain. The player does not feed it by hand.
- **Meaning:** what you release goes somewhere, and it may reach the room below.
- **Vertical use:** a chain reaction, in the style of Noita: water through a drain floods a room beneath.
- **Tier:** T3. It crosses a room boundary.

## Open questions

1. Water crossing into the room below breaks the rule that no room reaches another floor when the room below is on another floor. Does a drain stay inside one floor?
2. If it stays inside one floor, does it still cross the rooms' walls? Which rule applies (see [VERTICALITY](VERTICALITY.md))?
