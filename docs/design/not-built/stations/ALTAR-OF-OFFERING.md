# Altar of offering

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

An altar asks for something and gives something back. It asks for three crates, one husk or a meal, and it gives a practice, a key or a heart. The shapes it asks with are carved on it; there is no text. Giving up something to receive.

## Design

- **What it does:** takes an offering and gives a practice, a key or a heart.
- **Verb:** place the offering on the altar. The carved shapes show what it wants.
- **Meaning:** giving up something to receive.
- **Tier:** T2.

## Open questions

1. A practice from an altar is a gift. [POTIONS §4.2](../practices/potions/README.md) says gifts are inventory items; the same rule must hold here, or the altar and the potions disagree.
2. Keys belong to the chest system ([CHESTS](../../built/chests/CHESTS.md)). Does the altar follow its key cost table?
3. The carved shapes must pass the art gate ([DECISIONS](../../../art/DECISIONS.md)).
