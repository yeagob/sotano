# Smokehouse

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A smokehouse preserves. Husks or fish hang over smoke for a few rooms and come out as food that never spoils: preparing for later.

## Design

- **What it does:** hangs husks or fish over smoke for a few rooms. The result is a preserved food that never spoils.
- **Recipe (placeholder):** a smoked fish gives +1 heart and never spoils.
- **Verb:** hang the cube on a hook in the smoke, then leave the room. The count is in rooms, not seconds.
- **Meaning:** preparing for later, the slow way.
- **Tier:** T2.
