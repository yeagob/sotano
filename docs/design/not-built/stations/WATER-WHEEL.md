# Water wheel

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A water wheel turns while water flows past it and powers whatever touches it. Emotion becomes energy.

## Design

- **What it does:** turns while water flows. It powers what is touching it: a bellows, a lift.
- **Verb:** none. It is a machine: the player sets the flow and the wheel does the rest.
- **Meaning:** emotion as energy.
- **Vertical use:** it drives the counterweight lift ([BALANCE-LIFT](BALANCE-LIFT.md)).
- **The mill room:** a water wheel turning a balance lift is the mill in [THEMED-ROOMS](THEMED-ROOMS.md).
- **Tier:** T3, because power passing from one block to another is a system.
