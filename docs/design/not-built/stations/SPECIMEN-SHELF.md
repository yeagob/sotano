# Specimen shelf

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A specimen shelf holds jars of husks, labelled with ruled lines. The player keeps what they want to study at a distance. Breaking a jar releases its light, or, more dangerously, the projection itself.

## Design

- **What it does:** holds husks in jars. Breaking a jar releases the husk's light.
- **Verb:** break the jar with a hit or a throw.
- **Meaning:** what you keep at a distance to study.
- **Tier:** T1.

## Open questions

1. Can a jar release the projection itself? That would spawn an enemy from a station, which needs a rule about who sends it and where it lands.
2. The labels are ruled lines, not text. Is that enough for the player to tell husks apart?
