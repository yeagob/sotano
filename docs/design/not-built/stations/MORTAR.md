# Mortar

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A mortar breaks down to understand. A cube kicked into it is ground to a finer matter.

## Design

- **What it does:** grinds a cube to sand, salt or powder.
- **Verb:** kick the cube into the mortar.
- **Meaning:** breaking down to understand.
- **Recipe idea:** coal and salt together give gunpowder (an open idea, see below).
- **Tier:** T1.
