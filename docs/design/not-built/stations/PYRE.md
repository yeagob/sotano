# Pyre

Status: idea. The burning barrel of [ELEMENTS](../elements/BARREL.md), made into a place. Index: [stations](README.md).

A pyre is where what the player lets go is burned. It is the letting-go station of the fire family.

## Design

- **What it does:** anything thrown in is gone. Husks and crates flare up and leave ash.
- **Verb:** throw or place a cube in. It burns at once; the ash is left on the floor of the pyre.
- **Meaning:** letting go, burning the letter. The fire takes what the player has finished with.
- **Updraft:** its hot air column lifts light things (ash, paper, a jar of air). With a practice that raises a lift stat, the column can lift the player too: an updraft ladder. Without the practice it lifts only light things.
- **Tier:** T2 (the leaving of ash is a timed output).
- **Burning barrel:** the barrel in [ELEMENTS](../elements/BARREL.md) is the same fire without a place of its own; the two must agree on what burns.
