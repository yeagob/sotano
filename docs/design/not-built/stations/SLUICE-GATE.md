# Sluice gate

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A sluice gate lets a contained pool flow along a channel when it is hit, and holds it back when hit again. It is a feeling let out on purpose.

## Design

- **What it does:** hit it to open or close. Opening releases a contained pool along the channel.
- **Verb:** hit it. Each hit toggles the gate.
- **Meaning:** letting a feeling flow on purpose.
- **Vertical use:** flood a vertical shaft to swim up it (a water elevator), or drain it to walk down.
- **Water:** wading and swimming are listed as built in [docs/design/README.md](../../README.md) (not checked here); a gate depends on that.
- **Tier:** T1.
