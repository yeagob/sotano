# Well with a bucket

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A well is reaching into depth. The player lowers a bucket and it comes back with water, a deep-water cube, or something lost.

## Design

- **What it does:** the use key lowers the bucket. It comes back with water or deep water, or with something lost.
- **Verb:** use the well; the bucket goes down, and the result comes up after a moment.
- **Meaning:** reaching into depth.
- **Vertical use:** a rope down that the player can also climb.
- **Tier:** T2.

## Open questions

1. What is "something lost"? A cube from another floor, a memory, a practice? It must not be a punishment without a way back.
2. The use key is not fixed (see [README](README.md), rule 3).
3. Does the bucket's rope count as a special block for climbing ([SPECIAL-BLOCKS](SPECIAL-BLOCKS.md))?
