# The stocks

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md). The family rules for instruments of self-punishment are in [README](README.md), rule 7.

The stocks stand for shame, being put on display. As a tool they are a trap: a projection lured into them is held for a few seconds. Patterns can be caught; they are not tortured.

## Design

- **What it does:** a projection lured into the stocks is held for a few seconds.
- **Verb:** lure a projection next to the stocks. Nothing is done to the projection beyond the hold.
- **Dismantled:** wood.
- **Tier:** not set in STATIONS.

## Open questions

1. Is a held projection "caught" and tested for the owner's respect rule, since the image is a person in shame? The owner decides whether the image belongs.
2. How is a projection lured: by a bell, by an intention, by the player walking?
3. What happens to a held projection when the hold ends: it returns to the fight, or it is released?
