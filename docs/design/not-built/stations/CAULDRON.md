# Cauldron

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A cauldron is for things that need mixing. Water and ingredients become a broth: slow care, not a quick bite.

## Design

- **What it does:** water and two ingredients give a broth. The broth heals over time, or gives a short state.
- **Recipes (placeholders):** root broth, from water, a root and a mushroom, heals one heart over ten seconds. Warm broth makes fire hurt less. Steady broth gives less knock-back. Sap tea, from a fruit and resin, gives a breath boost for the next dive.
- **Verb:** place the water and the ingredients into it, then wait.
- **Meaning:** slow care, the things that need time and mixing.
- **Tier:** T2.
