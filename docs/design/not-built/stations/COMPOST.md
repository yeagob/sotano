# Compost (station)

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A compost heap turns what defeated the player into ground for something new: husks, ash and dirt become rich soil that makes planters grow faster. Growing from what defeated you.

**Name conflict:** the Compost in [BESTIARY §8.8](../encounters/COMPOST.md) is a farming room that yields parts, a different mechanic. This station uses the same word. The owner must rename one of them before either is built; see [OPEN-QUESTIONS](OPEN-QUESTIONS.md).

## Design

- **What it does:** husks, ash and dirt give rich soil. Rich soil makes planters grow twice as fast.
- **Verb:** place the cubes in the heap; collect the soil.
- **Meaning:** growing from what defeated you.
- **Vertical use:** faster vines ([PLANTER](PLANTER.md)).
- **Tier:** T2.
