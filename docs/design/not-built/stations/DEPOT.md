# Depot

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A depot is a marked area on the floor, with a frame. Cubes left on it are kept across the run and never shatter or unload. What the player sets aside on the way down is what they need on the way up.

## Design

- **What it does:** cubes left on the depot are kept for the run. They never shatter and never unload.
- **Verb:** place cubes on the depot's marked area.
- **Meaning:** planning. What you set aside is what you will need.
- **Vertical use:** stock up on the way down, carry material up ([VERTICALITY](VERTICALITY.md)).
- **Tier:** T2 on its own floor; across floors it is T3.

## Open questions

1. Does a depot keep cubes only on its own floor, or does the run keep them when the player comes back up? The second needs the floor-crossing rule.
2. Is there a limit on how many cubes a depot keeps?
3. Does the depot hold a hazardous cube (lava, a bomb)?
