# The press

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md). The family rules for instruments of self-punishment are in [README](README.md), rule 7.

The press is pressure that makes you hard. It is always empty and it is used as a tool: two soft cubes go in and one harder cube comes out. Dense steps for a climb.

## Design

- **What it does:** two soft cubes give one harder cube (dirt and dirt give stone; stone and stone give deepstone).
- **Verb:** place two cubes in, then press.
- **Dismantled:** an iron plate.
- **Vertical use:** dense steps for climbing. [VERTICALITY](VERTICALITY.md) also lists breaking the press's plate floor as a way down.
- **Tier:** T2.
