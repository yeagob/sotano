# Kiln

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A kiln is slow. Soft earth goes in and a light, brittle tile comes out after time. Patience shapes the soft.

## Design

- **What it does:** dirt or mud becomes a tile: a light, brittle block that stacks fast. A clay jar is the other output.
- **Verb:** place the soft cube in and wait; the kiln shows its progress on the block's glow.
- **Meaning:** patience. The soft becomes something that holds a shape.
- **Vertical use:** tiles are cheap, light scaffolding, a fast way up a short shaft. They are brittle, so they break on a hard hit.
- **Tier:** T2 (a timer and an output).
