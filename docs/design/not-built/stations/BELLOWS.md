# Bellows

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

Bellows are breath given to fire. Kicked or hit, they send a gust that feeds the flame and pushes whatever is light.

## Design

- **What it does:** a gust that feeds fire (a faster smelt, a bigger flame) and pushes loose cubes and light projections.
- **Verb:** kick or hit it. It answers with the gust, and the nearby fire grows for a moment.
- **Meaning:** breath fuels the fire. Effort adds to what is already burning.
- **Vertical use:** pointed up, the bellows give a short launch, like a weak bounce pad ([SPECIAL-BLOCKS](SPECIAL-BLOCKS.md)).
- **Tier:** T1.

## Open questions

1. Does the gust push the player, or only cubes and projections?
2. Is a kick on the bellows a positive or a negative intention, or no intention at all?
3. How far does the gust reach, and does it count as a damage source for a projection?
