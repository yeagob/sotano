# Athanor

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

The athanor is the alchemist's slow furnace. It takes one cube and works on it for a whole floor: the player drops the cube in on the way down, and collects the transformed cube on the way up.

## Design

- **What it does:** one cube in, a transmuted cube out after a floor of work. Stone becomes gold-seamed stone, a husk becomes a shard of light, iron becomes something rare.
- **Verb:** place the cube in on the way down; collect it on the way up.
- **Meaning:** the Great Work takes time. The descent prepares the return.
- **Vertical use:** it rewards planning the climb back. It needs the player to come back up through the floor where it is.
- **Where:** only on deep Tree-of-Life floors ([LEVELS](../../built/levels/floors/TREE-OF-LIFE.md)).
- **Tier:** T3. It works across floors, so it needs a system that remembers a cube between floor visits.

## Open questions
