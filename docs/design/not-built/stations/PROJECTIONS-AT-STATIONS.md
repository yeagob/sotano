# Projections at stations

Status: idea. How each projection behaves near a station. The projections themselves are designed in [BESTIARY](README.md); this document only says what they do with stations. Index: [stations](README.md).

Each station changes a fight. Panic bursts next to a furnace and scatters coals; Doubt hesitates at a scale; the Inner Critic takes the lectern. Projections are patterns, not people, so their behaviour mirrors the pattern.

## Table

| Projection | With stations |
|---|---|
| Doubt | Stops at scales and hoppers to "reconsider"; will not step on [pressure plates](PRESSURE-PLATE.md); its husk is heavy, good for the press and the balance lift ([HUSK](HUSK.md)) |
| Inner Critic | Takes the lectern of the [field notes](FIELD-NOTES.md) and lobs judgements from it; breaking the lectern under it knocks it down; at home among the instruments of self-punishment |
| Panic | Bursting near a furnace or pyre scatters burning coals; near the sluice it can blow the gate open; its husk is warm and cooks itself on the hearth faster |
| Procrastination (next) | Lives in the larder; every piece it splits into hides in a jar |
| Distraction (next) | Drawn to bells, mirrors and lenses; rings bells at random |
| Burnout (element) | Lights every furnace and stove it passes; leaves them burning without fuel until they crack |
| Overwhelm (element) | Opens sluices and fills basins |

## Open questions

1. Procrastination, Distraction, Burnout and Overwhelm are marked "next" or "element" in the brainstorm. Their pattern entries belong to the bestiary; the station behaviour waits for them.
2. Can a projection open a sluice or light a furnace? That is a rule for every projection that touches a station, and it must be the same for all.
3. Doubt avoiding pressure plates is a safe spot for the player. Is that intended, or does it make the plate too easy?
