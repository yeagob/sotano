# Ice box

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

An ice box keeps ice and food cold. Ice melts outside it. It is preserving, and it is cooling down.

## Design

- **What it does:** keeps ice and food cold. Ice melts when it is taken out of the box.
- **Recipe (placeholder):** ice and a fruit give a cold fruit. Eating it puts out the fire on the player.
- **Verb:** place the cube inside; take it out when needed.
- **Meaning:** cooling down, keeping what must not spoil.
- **Where:** cold rooms and floor 9 ([CARRYABLES](CARRYABLES.md) lists ice).
- **Tier:** T2.

## Open questions
