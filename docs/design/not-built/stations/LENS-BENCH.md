# Lens and mirror bench

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A lens and mirror bench focuses and bends the player's intention. A glass lens makes a hotter beam; a mirror turns it by ninety degrees.

## Design

- **What it does:** a lens focuses an intention into a hotter beam; a mirror bends it by ninety degrees.
- **Verb:** place the lens or mirror in the beam's path; the bench holds it.
- **Meaning:** focus and reflection, seeing what the intention does when it is aimed.
- **Tier:** T1.
