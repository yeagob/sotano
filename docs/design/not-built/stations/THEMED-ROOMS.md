# Themed rooms

Status: idea. Each room is one line with its verb. Rooms must pass [sotano-levels](../../../../.claude/skills/sotano-levels/SKILL.md) before they are built. Index: [stations](README.md).

These are the rooms that the stations would furnish. Each room is named by the station or the theme it is built around, with the floors the brainstorm suggested.

## Rooms

| Room | Floor | The idea | The verb |
|---|---|---|---|
| kitchen | 0, 3 | A hearth, a long table, a larder, mushroom beds | Cook the husks of the last fight |
| forge | 4, 5 | A forge, an anvil and bellows by a lava channel | Make spikes and climb to the gallery |
| laboratory | 3, 8 | An experiment table, an alembic, shelves of jars | Combine two cubes and see; break a jar and something gets out |
| pressroom | 7 | The rack and the press, empty, on a stage | Use them, then dismantle them: the room warms |
| granary | 3, 4 | Hoppers on a gallery, chutes down to the floor | Fill the quota to raise the bridge |
| mill | 2 | A water wheel turning a balance lift | Open the sluice and ride the lift |
| orchard | 0, L1 | Planters under a tall vault | Plant a bomb, grow a vine, reach the hidden ledge |
| waiting-room | 1 | Rows of chairs, a clock without hands, one closed door | Ring the bell to open it (it calls the projections too) |
| vault-of-weights | 4 | Scales and boulders on rails | Balance the scales; roll a boulder onto a projection |
| cold-store | 9 | An ice box, a frozen shard, a dead furnace | Light the furnace, thaw the shard |
| observatory | L3 | A telescope, an orrery, a star-chart table | Turn the orrery: the teleport door changes star |

## Open questions

1. The laboratory floors conflict. This list puts the laboratory on floors 3 and 8; [POTIONS](../practices/potions/README.md) puts one laboratory on each zone floor without the Compost (2, 4, 6 and 8). See [OPEN-QUESTIONS](OPEN-QUESTIONS.md).
2. Floors L1 and L3 are layers, not the thirteen floors. Their status depends on the layers in [LEVELS](../../built/levels/README.md).
3. The pressroom is on floor 7 (violence). The instruments in it are always empty ([RACK](RACK.md), [PRESS](PRESS.md)).
