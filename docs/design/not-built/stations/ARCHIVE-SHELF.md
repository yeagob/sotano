# Archive shelf

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

An archive shelf is shared storage. The player puts a cube in and takes it out on any later floor's archive. What you learn stays with you.

## Design

- **What it does:** a cube put in is kept and can be taken out on any later floor that has an archive.
- **Verb:** place the cube on the shelf; take it on another floor.
- **Meaning:** what you learn stays with you.
- **Tier:** T3. It crosses floors.
