# Scales

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

Scales weigh two cubes. Balance is polarity: two things that are equal hold each other, and a balanced pair opens a way.

## Design

- **What it does:** a balanced pair (two cubes on the two pans) opens a cabinet or a door.
- **Verb:** place one cube on each pan; the pans show which side is heavier.
- **Meaning:** polarity and balance, as in the Kybalion ([CONCEPTS](../../../lore/CONCEPTS.md)).
- **Tier:** T1.
