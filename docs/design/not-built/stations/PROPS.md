# Props

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

Props are decoration with meaning. They do not transform cubes and they are all breakable into shards, or they react to an intention. They cost T0 or T1 and are grouped here by the place they belong to.

## Rules

- Every prop answers the player's intention ([README](README.md), rule 4). A prop that never reacts is a fixed point with a stated reason.
- Props are placed through the existing decor slots of the room templates ([sotano-levels](../../../../.claude/skills/sotano-levels/SKILL.md)).
- Props follow the art gate ([DECISIONS](../../../art/DECISIONS.md)).

## Meaning

The depths mirror the surface, as the cosmology says ([COSMOLOGY](../../../lore/COSMOLOGY.md)): the depths' cold kitchen mirrors a warm kitchen above, and the library of unread books mirrors the study. Stations could echo at the surface or at the tree when that layer exists.

## Open questions

1. "Toys in the oldest rooms (childhood, gently)": the owner decides how far this goes. Respect and tone are in [STORY](README.md).
2. Circle 4's stacks of coins: does a coin imply currency, and the owner has ruled out a shop ([DESIGN](README.md) "no coins, no shop")? The coins are blocks, not money, and the text must say so.
3. Are the trunk and branch props part of the tree floors, which are not built?
