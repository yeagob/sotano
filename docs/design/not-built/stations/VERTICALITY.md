# Verticality

Status: idea. How the stations help the player go up and down. Index: [stations](README.md).

The world is built from floors of 16 blocks, rooms are up to 8 high with galleries, and climbing back is a planned puzzle ([DESIGN](README.md), the climbing problem). The stations give the player new ways up and down, and each one asks the player to plan.

## Ways up and down

| Need | Ideas | What it asks of the player |
|---|---|---|
| Reach a gallery in the room | [BELLOWS](BELLOWS.md) launch, [BALANCE-LIFT](BALANCE-LIFT.md), [CRANE](CRANE.md), spikes from the [FORGE](FORGE.md), tiles from the [KILN](KILN.md), the rack's planks as ladders | Gather and transform material first |
| Climb out of a pit or a shaft | A vine from a [PLANTER](PLANTER.md) bomb; a water elevator from a [SLUICE-GATE](SLUICE-GATE.md) into a shaft; a well rope from [WELL](WELL.md); a [PYRE](PYRE.md) updraft with a practice | Choose between a bomb now and a ladder later |
| Carry material up | [DEPOT](DEPOT.md), [DUMBWAITER](DUMBWAITER.md), [ARCHIVE-SHELF](ARCHIVE-SHELF.md), [MINE-CART](MINE-CART.md) on ramps | Stock up on the way down |
| Send material down | [CHUTE](CHUTE.md), [DRAIN](DRAIN.md), dumbwaiter | Prepare the next floor before you reach it |
| Get down faster | Sluice drains (ride the water down); breaking the press's plate floor ([PRESS](PRESS.md)); the pit in floor 5 | Open your own way |
| Use the slow way | The [ATHANOR](ATHANOR.md) (one floor of work), [SMOKEHOUSE](SMOKEHOUSE.md), [COMPOST](COMPOST.md): things that pay off on the return | Trust the process |
| Vertical combat | Fight from galleries with a [BELL](BELL.md) as bait below; drop a [MINE-CART](MINE-CART.md) on a projection; flood a lower level from above | Use height as a vantage point |

## Floor-crossing ideas

The chute, the drain, the dumbwaiter and the archive shelf break today's guarantee that rooms never touch the floor above or below ([sotano-levels](../../../../.claude/skills/sotano-levels/SKILL.md)). If the owner wants them, they need a three-party review: a reserved one-by-one column like the boss shaft, its own test, and a rule for what happens when the player digs into it.

Each document that crosses floors says so in its status line.
