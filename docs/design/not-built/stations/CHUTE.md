# Chute

Status: idea. Needs a three-party review before any build, because it crosses floors. Index: [stations](README.md).

A chute is a hole in the floor with a lip. Cubes thrown into it fall to a hopper in the room below. It sends things ahead: the player does not go with them.

## Design

- **What it does:** a cube thrown in falls to a hopper in the room below.
- **Verb:** throw the cube into the chute's lip.
- **Meaning:** sending ahead, preparing the next floor before you reach it.
- **Tier:** T3. It crosses into the room below.

## Open questions

2. Does the chute send to the room directly below only, or to any room of the floor below?
