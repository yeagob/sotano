# Bell

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A bell calls attention. Ringing it calls every projection in the room to it, as bait, or wakes a sleeping door.

## Design

- **What it does:** every projection in the room comes to the bell. A bell can also wake a door that sleeps.
- **Verb:** hit the bell.
- **Meaning:** calling attention. Bait is a choice, and it can open a way.
- **Vertical use:** fight from a gallery with the bell as bait below.
- **Tier:** T1.
