# Experiment table

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

The experiment table is trying things out. The player places two cubes on it and strikes it with an intention; they combine by a hidden reaction table. Unknown results are a surprise, and failures smoke and leave ash.

## Design

- **What it does:** two cubes combine by a hidden reaction table. Examples: iron and water make rust; glass and light make a lens; husk and salt make an essence.
- **Verb:** place two cubes, then strike the table with an intention.
- **Failure:** a failed combination smokes and leaves ash.
- **Meaning:** trying things out. The player learns by doing.
- **Tier:** T2.
- **Discoveries:** a reaction the player finds is drawn in [FIELD-NOTES](FIELD-NOTES.md).
