# Beehive

Status: idea (marked "maybe" in the brainstorm). Index: [stations](README.md).

A beehive gives honey, for the kitchen. The bees chase whoever breaks it, projections included. Community: do not break what sustains others.

## Design

- **What it does:** gives honey to the kitchen. Breaking the hive sends bees after the breaker and any projection nearby.
- **Verb:** harvest honey by hitting the hive carefully, or leave it.
- **Meaning:** community. Breaking what sustains others has a cost.
- **Tier:** T2.

## Open questions

1. Are the bees creatures (the peaceful beings of [BESTIARY](README.md)) or a hazard of the hive? If creatures, they belong to the creatures folder.
2. Do bees damage the player? If so, the damage is a hazard; if not, the bees are a warning.
3. Owner decides whether the beehive belongs at all.
