# Balance lift

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A balance lift is two platforms on a rope. Load cubes onto one and the other rises, with the player on it. One goes down so the other goes up: polarity.

## Design

- **What it does:** two platforms on a rope. Cubes loaded on one platform lower it and raise the other.
- **Verb:** load cubes on the lower platform; step onto the upper one.
- **Meaning:** polarity. One goes down so the other goes up ([CONCEPTS](../../../lore/CONCEPTS.md)).
- **Vertical use:** a lift that the player powers with material, not with a button.
- **Power:** the counterweight can be driven by the water wheel ([WATER-WHEEL](WATER-WHEEL.md)) in the mill room.
- **Tier:** T2.
