# Root tap

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A root tap is the earth sustaining the player. Hit a thick root and sap drips into a jar. The resin glues cubes together into a ledge.

## Design

- **What it does:** a hit on a thick root drips sap into a jar. Resin glues two cubes into a ledge.
- **Verb:** hit the root; place two cubes, one on the other, with resin.
- **Meaning:** the earth sustains you.
- **Vertical use:** a glued ledge is a way up without a bomb.
- **Where:** floor 0, the roots ([PROPS](PROPS.md)).
- **Tier:** T1.
