# Mushroom bed and herb rack

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A mushroom bed, or a herb rack in a drier room, grows ingredients over time. The earth gives if you tend it.

## Design

- **What it does:** grows ingredients in damp rooms, over time. The bed gives a mushroom; the rack gives herbs.
- **Verb:** plant or place a cube on the bed, return later and collect what grew.
- **Meaning:** the earth gives if you tend it. Patience, again, but kinder than the kiln.
- **Where:** damp rooms, mostly floor 0 (the roots) and the mud circle ([PROPS](PROPS.md)).
- **Tier:** T2 (a timer and a grown output).
