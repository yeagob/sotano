# Forge and anvil

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A forge makes tools out of raw matter. Hot iron on the anvil, struck with an intention, becomes a shaped cube: a hook, a spike or a plate. Will hammers raw talent into shape.

## Design

- **What it does:** hot iron on the anvil, plus a hit of intention, gives a tool cube: a hook, a spike or a plate.
- **Verb:** place the iron on the anvil and hit the anvil with an intention. The strike decides the shape.
- **Meaning:** will and craft. The player chooses what the raw material becomes.
- **Vertical use:** a spike placed in a wall is a foothold; a hook hangs a rope (see [VERTICALITY](VERTICALITY.md)).
- **Rooms:** the forge room is on floors 4 and 5 ([THEMED-ROOMS](THEMED-ROOMS.md)), beside a lava channel.
- **Tier:** T2.

## Open questions

1. Which polarity shapes the iron: positive only, or does a negative hit break it? Positive intentions fit the rule of making, but the owner decides.
2. Does a hot iron cube stay hot in the hand, and does carrying it hurt?
3. The practice Craftsmanship proposes two tools from one hit ([PRACTICES-USING-STATIONS](PRACTICES-USING-STATIONS.md)); does that need a second output to be defined here first?
