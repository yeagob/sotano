# The rack

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md). The family rules for instruments of self-punishment are in [README](README.md), rule 7.

The rack is an instrument of self-punishment, reframed. It stands for stretching yourself thin: "never enough". It is always empty: no figure, no blood, only dust, worn straps and ruled lines scratched into the wood.

## Design

- **What it does:** a cube put on the rack and turned splits into two of the tier below. One stone becomes two dirt. More material, less strength.
- **Verb:** place a cube on the rack and turn it.
- **Dismantled:** planks, which make a ladder. Dismantling is a reward: the room heals a little.
- **Tier:** not set in STATIONS. Likely T2, because a turn is a timed action on a cube.
