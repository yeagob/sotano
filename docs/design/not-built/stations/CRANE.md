# Crane and winch

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A crane lifts the heavy by method, not force. The player hooks a cube, or themself, and cranks it up to a gallery.

## Design

- **What it does:** hooks a cube or the player and raises it to a gallery.
- **Verb:** hook the cube (or stand in the hook); crank.
- **Meaning:** lifting the heavy by method.
- **Vertical use:** the gallery is the one place the room's height is reached without a bounce or a bomb ([VERTICALITY](VERTICALITY.md)).
- **Tier:** T2.
