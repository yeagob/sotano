# Husk

Status: proposal. A key proposal of the stations brainstorm, not built. Index: [stations](README.md).

A husk is the cold shell a released projection sometimes leaves behind: the shell the light was trapped in. It is the pattern's leftover matter, what remains once the player has seen through it. It replaces the meat the brief first asked for: projections are patterns, not animals, and their release is gore-free ([ART-GUIDE §8.6](../../../art/ART-GUIDE.md)).

## Design

- **Where it comes from:** a released projection sometimes leaves one.
- **Look:** cold-side colours, dull, with a hairline crack. Each projection's husk differs: Doubt's is heavy like its sack; Panic's is warm and crackles; the Inner Critic's is a curled page.
- **Carried:** a husk is a carryable cube, picked up and placed like any cube ([CARRYABLES](CARRYABLES.md)).
- **Meaning:** what remains once you have seen through a pattern. The pattern's leftover matter.

## The four uses

| Use | Station | Meaning | Result (placeholder) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cook it | [HEARTH-STOVE](HEARTH-STOVE.md) | Integration; "own your shadow" | Nourishes the player; a husk meal heals more |
| Burn it | [PYRE](PYRE.md) | Letting go | A small immediate gift: luck for the room, a mote of light |
| Compost it | [COMPOST](COMPOST.md) | Growing from it | Rich soil; faster vines |
| Study it | [EXPERIMENT-TABLE](EXPERIMENT-TABLE.md) or [ALEMBIC](ALEMBIC.md) | Understanding | Fills a page of the field notes; a potion or an essence |

Each projection's husk may give different results. What the owner chooses to do with a husk is a choice about what defeated the player.

3. How often does a projection leave a husk? The brainstorm gives no rate.
