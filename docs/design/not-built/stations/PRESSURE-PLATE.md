# Pressure plate

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

A pressure plate holds something in place: a door, a sluice or a lift stays open or up while something heavy stands on it. The heavy thing can be a cube, a projection or the player.

## Design

- **What it does:** holds a door, a sluice or a lift while something heavy is on it.
- **Verb:** stand, or place a cube, on the plate.
- **Meaning:** keeping something in place.
- **Tier:** T1.

## Open questions

1. What counts as "heavy"? A projection on the plate, a cube, or any weight above a line?
2. Doubt will not step on pressure plates ([PROJECTIONS-AT-STATIONS](PROJECTIONS-AT-STATIONS.md)); does that make a plate a safe spot for the player against Doubt?
