# Special blocks

Status: idea. Index: [stations](README.md).

Special blocks are not stations: they hold no cube and transform nothing. Each one changes movement or combat in its place, with one simple reaction (tier T1). They are listed here because the brainstorm mentions them in several places; each one needs its own document before it is built.

## The list

| Block | Reaction | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| Bounce pad | Launches the player or a cube upward on touch | [BELLOWS](BELLOWS.md) compares a pointed bellows to a weak bounce pad |
| Ladder | Lets the player climb on contact | [VERTICALITY](VERTICALITY.md) (climb out of a pit) |
| Mirror | Reflects an intention | [MIRROR](MIRROR.md) |
| Bell | Calls projections to it | [BELL](BELL.md) |
| Conductor | Carries an electric charge (electricity milestone) | [TESLA-COIL](TESLA-COIL.md) |
| Light | Lights the room, a lamp | [LONG-TABLE](LONG-TABLE.md) |
| Pressure plate | Holds something in place | [PRESSURE-PLATE](PRESSURE-PLATE.md) |
| Rope | Lets a cube or the player hang and climb | [WELL](WELL.md), [BALANCE-LIFT](BALANCE-LIFT.md) |

## Rules

- A special block reacts to one thing at a time: a hit, a touch or a use.
- It never reacts on load, like every block of the house ([README](README.md), rule 6).
- A special block that never reacts is a fixed point with a stated reason.
