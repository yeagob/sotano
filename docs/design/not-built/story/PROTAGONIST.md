# The protagonist

Up: [not-built story](README.md)

Status: open question (story question 1: who the player is).

## What is built

The player is a first-person view with no face. The only body the player sees is the hands, in a skin tone and a knitted jumper with a cuff band, with a thumb, as the art pass drew them ([ART](../../built/feel/ART.md)). The protagonist is gender-neutral, and no name for them appears in the game's text.

## What the story says

The protagonist is gender-neutral and faceless (first person) by default. The story's open question asks whether the player is ever shown. The recommendation there is that only the hands show, and that the hands change with the state of being, which is not built ([STORY, open question 1](../../built/story/PREMISE.md)).

The states of being change the player through the paths ([PATHS](../../built/story/PATHS.md)). Showing the change is the one visual the protagonist is meant to have, and no state changes the hands today.

## Other places the player is shown

- The Shadow, the keystone boss of the ninth floor, is the player's own silhouette in the room's negative, with the player's outline. This is design ([BESTIARY](README.md)).
- The Comparison projection copies the player's intentions back, not the player's face. It is a mirror of what you do, not of who you are.

## Open questions

1. Is the player ever shown: only the hands, a reflection, a silhouette, or a face?
2. Do the hands change with the state of being, as the recommendation says, and in what way?
3. Does the protagonist have a name in the game, in the menu or on the death screen?
4. The jumper comes from the house above in the art record, and the levels say there is no house on the surface. Does the jumper stay?
