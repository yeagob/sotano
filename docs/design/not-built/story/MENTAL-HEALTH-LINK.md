# A link to mental-health resources

Up: [not-built story](README.md)

Status: open question (story question 4). The audit records it as design only: nothing is in the game.

## What is built

The game has no link to any mental-health resource. The start page, the pause card, the death screen and the HUD carry no such link. The start page says the game is open source and open to collaboration, and it has a short tagline.

## What the story says

The game uses therapy names for its practices, so the story recommends a short, sober link in the menu, phrased as "if you're struggling", as a kindness toward a player who may need one ([STORY, open question 4](../../built/story/PREMISE.md)). The story leaves the decision to the owner.

## Why it is a real question

- The practices are named after therapies (exposure, cognitive behavioural techniques, radical acceptance). A player who is struggling may meet them in the game.
- The projections are patterns, never people or diagnoses. A link must not turn a pattern into a diagnosis.
- The game is open to every player, in English and Spanish, and a link would have to be right in both languages and in each country it names.

## Options

- **No link.** The game stays a game, and the practices are credited as the sources they come from.
- **A sober line in the menu** only, with a link to a list of resources.
- **A line on the death screen**, where a player who has lost a run may be most in need. This is the most sensitive place, and the least intrusive.
- **A line in the README and the open source page only,** outside the game.

## Open questions

1. Do we link at all? (The recommendation is yes: a short, sober link.)
2. Where: the menu, the death screen, the README, or nowhere in the game?
3. Which resources, and for which countries? An international list, a list per language, or the owner's own list?
4. The wording, in English and in Spanish, reviewed by someone who works in the field.
5. Does the link stay visible on every floor, or only when the player asks?
