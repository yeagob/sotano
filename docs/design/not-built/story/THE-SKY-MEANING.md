# The sky and the meaning of silence

Up: [not-built story](README.md)

Status: open question (left open on purpose: the story says so, and the owner has not decided it).

## What the story says

Above the roots a giant tree rises into the sky, and the depths and the tree are one journey seen from its two ends. The deepest point and the highest one meet, and the silence loops back to heaven. What the sky means as a game layer, and how silence connects with heaven, is left open on purpose ([STORY, open question 2](../../built/story/PREMISE.md)).

The cosmology gives the frame: the tree of light descends through ten emanations and the soul climbs back up along the paths, so below and above are one tree ([COSMOLOGY](../../../lore/COSMOLOGY.md)). The respect rules apply: the tree is a structure taken from a living tradition, its sacred names are never used, and no figure from it is made an enemy.

## The built loop, for context

The loop of the silence and the sky is built, and its detail belongs to the built levels documents. In short: standing still in the silence under the ninth circle carries the player up to heaven, the top of the sky brings them back down, and the giant tree and its night sky are in place. What the sky means is not decided, so the built loop is a mechanism without a stated meaning.

## What is not decided

- **What the sky is.** A layer of the world (the leaves are a star map with teleport doors and views of the cosmos, in the levels' owner direction), a symbol, or a reward.
- **How silence connects with heaven.** The owner's options are a fall, a door, or a shaft of light. Each one has a different meaning.
- **Whether heaven is a place or a state.** A place the player can reach, or the feeling at the top of the tree.
- **Whether the sky has a meaning for the run.** Whether reaching it changes the run, or only the view.

## Constraints from the design

- Silence connects with heaven, cyclically (owner decision, 27 October 2026).
- Heaven and the bottom are connected; how is to be decided ([LEVELS](../levels/ENDING-AND-RETURN.md)).
- Projections are patterns, never people. Whatever the sky means must be carried by the place itself, not by a figure that stands for a person.
