# Cleaning the judgements

Status: **proposal** (design text, bestiary section 7.2).

Inkmites are tiny mites that eat the judgement marks of the Lectern and the Margin off the floor. In a fight, they clean the judgements away.

## What the design asks

- **Eating marks.** An Inkmite finds a judgement mark on the floor and eats it.
- **During fights.** The cleaning is part of the fight, so the player can watch the floor clear.

## What the build has

Inkmites wander. The judgement marks of the Lectern and the Margin are built as the projections' own effects, and nothing removes them.

## Open questions

- **Dependency.** The Inkmite's job depends on the judgement marks, which are part of the projections (see their documents). Build the marks first.
- **Safety.** A creature that removes a hazard changes a fight. Decide whether an Inkmite can be hurt by a mark it is eating.
- **Clear.** Should the Inkmites clean the floor at the room's clear, as well as during the fight?
