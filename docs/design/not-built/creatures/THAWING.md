# Thawing

Status: **proposal** (design text, bestiary section 7.2).

Huddlers gather on the ice of the frozen floor. They stand for warmth shared, and the design gives them two jobs: to thaw the player out of the frost that slows casting, and, when befriended, to follow the player and thaw the Numbness's ice.

## What the design asks

- **Thaw by standing.** Standing among Huddlers thaws the frost's slowing effect on the player.
- **Befriended thaw.** A befriended Huddler follows the player and thaws the ice of the Numbness.

## What the build has

Huddlers wander on the ice and answer only positive intentions. The frost's slow and the Numbness's ice are designed, and no Huddler thaws anything.

## Open questions

- **The frost.** The frost's slow is a status the player carries. Decide whether standing among Huddlers removes it at once or over time.
- **Ice.** The frozen floor's ice awaits an art ruling (the ledger of the art guide).
- **Befriending.** A befriended Huddler needs the befriending rule first (see [Befriending](BEFRIENDING.md)).
