# Befriending

Status: **proposal** (design text, section 7.1 of the bestiary).

Some creatures can be befriended: they follow the player for the floor. The act is gentle and small: standing still near them, placing a block for them, or putting out a fire near them. Befriended creatures then do their own small job: a Delver digs a block ahead now and then, and a Huddler follows and thaws the Numbness's ice.

## What the design asks

- **Acts.** Stand still near the creature; place a block for it; put out a fire near it. Any one of them, per creature.
- **Following.** A befriended creature follows the player for the rest of the floor.
- **Jobs.** Each befriended creature does its own job (see the creature's document).
- **Never a chore.** No act is timed, counted or scored. The creature does not thank the player; it only follows.

## What the build has

Creatures wander, flee or rest. Nothing a player does makes a creature follow.

## Open questions

- **Which act for which creature.** The design lists three acts without assigning them. Assign them when the first befriendable creature is built.
- **Floor boundary.** Does a befriended creature stay with the player across floors, or only for the floor?
- **Codex.** A befriended creature could be the first entry in the codex. Decide with the codex.
