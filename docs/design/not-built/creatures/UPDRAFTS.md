# Updrafts and platforms

Status: **proposal** (design text, bestiary section 7.2).

A Drifter floats in the tall rooms of Zone III, riding updrafts. The design lets the player jump on a Drifter to reach galleries and high pockets: a moving platform.

## What the design asks

- **Updrafts.** A Drifter rides rising air in tall rooms.
- **Platforms.** Jumping on a Drifter lifts the player to a gallery or a high pocket.

## What the build has

A Drifter floats about two blocks up and drifts. The player cannot stand on it or ride it.

## Open questions

- **Rooms.** Which tall rooms have updrafts? The room templates decide; the levels skill must be asked before the first one.
- **Safety.** A platform that moves must never drop the player into a fall they cannot see. Decide the rule before the first ride.
- **Hiding.** A Drifter that is hidden by a seal takes its platform with it. Decide whether a seal should hold a ride.
