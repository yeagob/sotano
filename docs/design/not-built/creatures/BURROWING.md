# Burrowing and digging

Status: **proposal** (design text, bestiary section 7.2).

Two creatures dig. A Rootling burrows toward hidden pockets, and following it shows the player secrets. A Delver makes small tunnels and pops out of them; a befriended Delver digs a block ahead of the player now and then.

## What the design asks

- **Rootling pockets.** The Rootling burrows toward hidden pockets, and following it leads to secrets. On floor 0 it also shows the tutorial's next step.
- **Delver tunnels.** The tunnels show where the rock is soft.
- **Befriended digging.** A befriended Delver digs a block ahead of the player now and then.

## What the build has

Both creatures wander. The rooms have hidden pockets, but nothing leads the player to them.

## Open questions

- **Fair secrets.** A secret the creature leads to must be found by a sign the player can read: a tunnel, a sound, a light. Which sign?
- **Digging verb.** A creature that digs needs the room's blocks to change. Does the Delver break blocks the way bosses do, or only soft ones?
- **Tutorial.** The next-step cue is a teaching tool. It belongs with the tutorial's lessons, not the Rootling alone.
