# Bubbles and the ferry

Status: **proposal** (design text, bestiary section 7.2).

Two water creatures give the player a way through deep water. A Bubbler's bubbles refill the player's breath when swum through. A Ferryback is a slow, broad swimmer that the player stands on to cross deep water.

## What the design asks

- **Bubbles.** Swimming through a Bubbler's bubbles refills breath.
- **Ferrying.** Standing on a Ferryback carries the player across deep water.

## What the build has

Bubbler and Ferryback drift in water, and the player cannot ride or refill anything. The build has no breath to refill.

## Open questions

- **Breath.** Does the game need a breath resource? Decide that first; the bubbles depend on it.
- **Carrying.** Can the player stand on a Ferryback without it hiding (a creature that is hurt by a stand would be a mistake)? The stand must be a gentle act.
- **Overlap.** The Boatman presence is also a ferry (see [the Boatman](../presences/BOATMAN.md)). Decide whether the creature or the presence carries.
