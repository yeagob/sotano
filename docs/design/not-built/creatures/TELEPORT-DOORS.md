# Teleport doors

Status: **proposal** (design text, bestiary section 7.2).

Leaflings and Star-mites live on the Tree. The design has them point out the teleport doors on the star map, and join the player's doors together. The Tree is not built, so neither creature has a job yet.

## What the design asks

- **Pointing.** A Leafling points out the teleport doors on the star map.
- **Joining.** A Star-mite joins the teleport doors the player has found.

## What the build has

Both creatures wander on floors 1 to 5, the level the code calls the tree. There is no star map and no teleport door.

## Open questions

- **The Tree.** The doors are on the giant tree above the surface, a milestone (L5) that is not built. Until it is, the creatures' jobs wait.
- **Name collision.** The build's floors 1 to 5 are called the tree level in the code. The creatures are placed there, not on the Tree above. Rename one of them before the Tree is built.
- **Map.** The star map does not exist in the build.
