# New materials

Three materials the design names and the game does not have yet: sand, cobweb and rune stone.

Status: proposal

Part of [blocks (not built)](README.md).

## The design

- **Sand and cobweb** are tier zero, for decoration on the first floor ([DESIGN.md, section 5](README.md)).
- **Rune stone** is tier three, for special places and seals ([DESIGN.md, section 5](README.md)). The design also says the rune block is something to be discovered; that belongs to [ACTIVE-BLOCKS.md](ACTIVE-BLOCKS.md).

Every material is data: its tier, hit points, armour and flags are set in one place, the materials list, and the built rules then apply to it ([built/blocks/TIERS.md](../../built/blocks/TIERS.md)).

## Open questions

1. **Does sand fall.** The design does not say whether sand should fall like a block without support, or stay where it is. The falling of loose matter is a physics question the owner should answer before sand is built.
2. **What a cobweb does.** The design names it as decoration. Does a cobweb slow the player, hold an intention, or do nothing?
3. **What rune stone is for.** The design says rune stone seals special places. Which places, and what opens a seal, is not decided. The ritual of the deep floors belongs to the levels and story sections.
