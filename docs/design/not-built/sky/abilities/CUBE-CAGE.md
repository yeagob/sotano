# Cube cage

Up: [Abilities](README.md) · [The realm of the sky](../README.md)

The cube cage is instantiated cubes that rise around the player and lock them in. The cage is made by the animal, not by the player, so it is not a block the player built.

Status: proposal. The hindrance is the owner's; the rules and the animal that uses it are proposal.

## What the player sees and feels

- **The telegraph.** Outlines of cubes appear around the player's cell, one by one, with a soft tick for each. The outlines show the cage's shape before the cubes are solid.
- **The closing.** The cubes rise and become solid. The player is inside. The cage's walls are close, and the player feels the room shrink.
- **The break.** A shot at the cage breaks a cube. The player can break out through any gap. The cage then dissolves.

## The rule

1. The outline of the cage shows before the cubes become solid. The player always sees where the cage will close.
2. The cage encloses the player's cell only. It does not reach other cells the player has not stood in.
3. A cage never closes over a fatal drop. If the cage closes over a drop, the drop is shown first.
4. The cage dissolves after a few seconds if the player does not break it. Nothing is permanent (realm law 3.1).
5. The player may break out with any shot. A cage cube is not a player-made block, so a shot breaks it and it does not stay.

## How the player answers it

- **Break out.** Any shot breaks a cube. Breaking out is the answer in every case.
- **A positive shot.** A positive shot breaks a cube faster (proposal; see the abilities table).
- **Wait.** The cage dissolves on its own after a few seconds.

## Which animal uses it

Open. Proposal: the koala, which holds the player the way it holds a branch (see [KOALA](../guardians/KOALA.md)).
