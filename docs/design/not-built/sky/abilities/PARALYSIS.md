# Paralysis

Up: [Abilities](README.md) · [The realm of the sky](../README.md)

Paralysis freezes the player for a short time. The player can see, but cannot move or shoot until it ends.

Status: proposal. The hindrance is the owner's; the rules and the animal that uses it are proposal.

## What the player sees and feels

- **The telegraph.** A ring of light and a sound spread from the animal before the freeze reaches the player. The ring has a visible edge the player can step out of.
- **The freeze.** The player's body stops. A cold, grey tint covers the player's own hands, so the freeze is clear. The player feels stillness, not a stun that shakes the screen.
- **The end.** The tint fades and the player moves again. The end is shown, so the player knows when they are free.

## The rule

1. The freeze happens only inside the ring, and only after the ring has shown its edge.
2. The freeze lasts a short time (a target in the guardian's behaviour), then ends on its own.
3. A paralysed player is never left over a fatal drop, or in a place they cannot leave once the freeze ends. The player always has a route up when it ends (realm law 3.5).
4. A freeze does not stack: a second ring while the player is frozen does nothing new.

## How the player answers it

- **Step out of the ring.** Before the ring reaches the player, moving out of its edge avoids the freeze.
- **Hold a steady aim.** A positive shot, held steady, shortens the freeze (proposal).
- **Wait it out.** A freeze that does not drop the player anywhere is only a pause.

## Which animal uses it

Open. Proposal: the lion, whose roar carries the ring (see [LION](../guardians/LION.md)).

## Open questions

1. Is the freeze timed, or does it last until the player answers with a positive intention?
2. Does a paralysed player take hits from minions while frozen?
3. Is the ring always visible from the player's position, or can it come from behind?
