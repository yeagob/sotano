# Dense ground

Up: [Abilities](README.md) · [The realm of the sky](../README.md)

Dense ground is the floor turning denser, the way water is denser than air, so that walking on it slows the player. It is the owner's example of a hindrance: slowing by changing the floor itself.

Status: proposal. The hindrance is the owner's; the rules and the animal that uses it are proposal.

## What the player sees and feels

- **The telegraph.** The cells the animal chooses ripple, darken and take on a glassy sheen, with a low sound that rises and holds. The change comes after a warning the player can read.
- **The change.** The cells become dense. Their surface looks thick and glassy, and they slow the player's walk. The player feels their feet sink and their steps shorten.
- **The end.** The cells return to ordinary ground after a few seconds. There is no lasting change.

## The rule

1. Dense ground changes only the cells the animal chose, and only after its telegraph.
2. Walking on dense ground slows the player. Jumping out of dense ground is still possible, at its normal height.
3. Dense ground never holds the player in place. It slows; it does not freeze (freezing is [paralysis](PARALYSIS.md)).
4. Dense ground returns to ordinary ground after a few seconds. It is not permanent (realm law 3.1).
5. A player-made block placed in dense ground is not changed by it. It stays as the player built it (realm law 3.2).

## How the player answers it

- **Build over it.** A staircase of blocks above the dense cells takes the player out of the slowing ground.
- **Jump.** A jump from the dense cells clears the slowing, and the player keeps the jump's height.
- **A positive shot.** A positive shot at a dense cell returns it to ordinary ground at once (proposal).

## Which animal uses it

Open. Proposal: the panda, whose weight presses the floor into a denser state around its seat (see [PANDA](../guardians/PANDA.md)).

## Open questions

1. How far does the dense change reach: a single cell, a row, or the floor around the animal?
2. Does a player slowed by dense ground get a faster jump, or the same jump?
3. Does the dense state spread to the next cell over time, or only where the animal chose it?
