# Opening holes

Up: [Abilities](README.md) · [The realm of the sky](../README.md)

Opening holes are holes that open in the floor: the animal takes away one or more cells of the floor, and the player must cross, build over or fall.

Status: proposal. The hindrance is the owner's; the rules and the animal that uses it are proposal.

## What the player sees and feels

- **The telegraph.** The cells to open show their edges first: a crack line in the stone, a dust cue, a deep sound. The crack is visible before the floor gives way.
- **The opening.** The cells sink out of the floor. The edges fall first, then the middle, and the player sees the hole widen.
- **The closing.** The hole closes again after a few seconds. The floor comes back, and the original world's blocks return (realm law 3.1).

## The rule

1. The cracks show before any cell opens. The player always sees where the hole will be.
2. A hole never opens under a player who has no route off it. The cracks show the hole, and a route is always there (realm law 3.5).
3. A hole closes again after a few seconds. It is not permanent.
4. A block the player built over a hole stays as built (realm law 3.2). The player's bridge is theirs to keep.
5. A hole never opens in a player-made block. Player-made blocks do not give way to the animal.

## How the player answers it

- **Jump across.** A jump clears one or two cells, depending on the jump's reach.
- **Build over.** A block placed over the hole stays as the player's bridge (realm law 3.2).
- **Wait on the bank.** A hole closes on its own after a few seconds.
- **A positive shot.** A positive shot closes a hole that has started to open (proposal).

## Which animal uses it

Open. Proposal: the panda, whose weight gives way under its seat (see [PANDA](../guardians/PANDA.md)).
