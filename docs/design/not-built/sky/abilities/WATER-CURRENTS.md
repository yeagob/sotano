# Water currents

Up: [Abilities](README.md) · [The realm of the sky](../README.md)

Water currents are instantiated water: the animal fills a channel with water that pushes and carries the player along its flow.

Status: proposal. The hindrance is the owner's; the rules and the animal that uses it are proposal.

## What the player sees and feels

- **The telegraph.** A dry channel first shows its outline, its flow arrows (faint ripples in the stone), and a rising sound of water coming. The water fills after that.
- **The current.** The water flows in one direction and carries the player along it. The player feels a steady pull, and the surface moves with them.
- **The end.** The water drains or evaporates after a while, and the channel goes back to dry. Nothing is left behind.

## The rule

1. Water appears only in the channels the animal chose, and only after its telegraph.
2. A current has one direction for its whole length, shown by its flow arrows before it fills.
3. A current pushes and carries the player. It does not drown them in a dry channel and does not hold them under a fall.
4. The water drains after a few seconds. Its drain is a visible step, not a sudden cut.
5. A player-made block in a channel is an obstacle to the current. It stays (realm law 3.2), and the water goes around it.

## How the player answers it

- **Build a dam.** A block placed across the channel stops the flow at the dam. The dam stays.
- **Cross on the bank.** A player on a bank above the channel is not carried by it.
- **Jump the current.** A jump across the water clears it. The player keeps the jump's height.
- **A positive shot.** A positive shot turns the current's direction for a moment, or stops it (proposal).

## Which animal uses it

Open. Proposal: the elephant, whose trunk sprays water (see [ELEPHANT](../guardians/ELEPHANT.md)).
