# Knockback

Up: [Abilities](README.md) · [The realm of the sky](../README.md)

Knockback is a kick that sends the player flying in an arc, away from the animal that kicked.

Status: proposal. The hindrance is the owner's; its rules and the animals that use it are proposal.

## What the player sees and feels

- **The telegraph.** The animal's leg, trunk or shoulder lowers and turns toward the player. A dust cue and a short low sound show the direction of the kick before it lands. The kick is never a surprise.
- **The push.** The player is lifted and carried along the arc. The camera leans with the push. The player feels a short, hard shove, not a slow drift.
- **The landing.** The player lands on whatever the arc reaches. A landing that is not a block the player can stand on is always shown in the arc before the kick.

## The rule

1. A kick sends the player in one arc, away from the animal, at a set strength (a target in the guardian's behaviour).
2. The direction is shown before the kick lands. The player always knows where they are going.
3. A kick never sends the player into a fatal fall without the arc having shown it first.
4. A kick does not move the player through a block. It stops at the first solid block in its path.

## How the player answers it

- **Step out of the line.** Move so the arc points at a block the player has built, or at open floor.
- **Build a wall.** A block placed in the arc's line stops the flight at the wall. The wall is a player-made block and stays (realm law 3.2).
- **A positive shot.** A positive shot aimed at the animal's leg before the kick lands turns the kick into a soft push (proposal; see [the abilities table](README.md#2-what-a-positive-intention-does-to-each-hindrance)).

## Which animal uses it

Open. The owner named the hindrance but not the animal. Proposal: the elephant (its trunk, see [ELEPHANT](../guardians/ELEPHANT.md)) and the horse (its rear, see [HORSE](../guardians/HORSE.md)).

## Open questions

1. Does a kick do damage, or only move the player?
2. Is the kick's strength the same on every floor, or does it grow with the sky floor?
3. Does a kick over a ledge always land on a block, or can it send the player off a floor with no route back? (The realm's law 3.5 says a route up is always there.)
4. Which animal uses it: the guardian, the minion, or both?
