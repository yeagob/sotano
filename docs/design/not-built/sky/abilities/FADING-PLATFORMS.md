# Fading platforms

Up: [Abilities](README.md) · [The realm of the sky](../README.md)

Fading platforms are platforms that disappear, or parts of them, after a warning. The owner asked for them with clear feedback that warns first. Their feedback is the rule of this document.

Status: proposal. The owner's rule that platforms always warn first is decided. The rest of this document is proposal.

## What the player sees and feels

- **The telegraph.** The platform's edges turn grey, and it flickers, with a tick sound that speeds up. The warning lasts long enough to read, and the platform's full outline still shows.
- **The fade.** The platform, or the chosen part of it, turns to mist and is gone. The player feels the floor drop out from under them if they are still on it.
- **The return.** The platform reforms after a few seconds, with a soft chime. Nothing is permanent.

## The rule

1. **A warning always comes first.** Every platform that fades, and every part that fades, shows its warning before it goes. This is decided.
2. A fade is either whole (the platform goes) or partial (only chosen parts go). The telegraph shows which.
3. A platform never fades under a player who has no route off it. The warning lets the player move before it goes.
4. A platform comes back after a few seconds. It is not permanent (realm law 3.1).
5. A player-made platform does not fade. It stays until the player or a positive intention takes it away (realm law 3.2).

## How the player answers it

- **Move on early.** The warning gives time to step to the next platform.
- **Build ahead.** A platform built next to a fading one stays (realm law 3.2), and the route is then the player's.
- **A positive shot.** A positive shot holds a fading platform for a moment (proposal).

## Which animal uses it

Open. Proposal: the koala, whose branches let go (see [KOALA](../guardians/KOALA.md)), and the horse, whose hooves kick the platforms (see [HORSE](../guardians/HORSE.md)).
