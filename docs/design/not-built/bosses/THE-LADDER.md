# The Ladder (Tree floor L1)

Status: **idea**. The Tree is not built (milestone L5). This is a direction for the owner to design with the Tree.

The climb up the giant tree is not a fight against the same patterns. It is the subtle patterns that come with growing. As above, so below: each upper floor mirrors a zone below. The trunk mirrors the Roots.

- **Mirror:** the Roots (floor 0).
- **Patterns:** striving, never high enough.
- **Boss (idea):** the Ladder, a climb that keeps adding rungs.

## Open questions

- **The owner's design.** Nothing about the Tree is decided, including its bosses, its patterns and its floors.
- **The rule.** How the Ladder ends (a rung the player must choose to stop at, or a fight with a release) is open.
- **The mirror.** The design's mirror is structural: the trunk's room and the Roots' room share a shape and a meaning. Confirm the mirror before the boss.
