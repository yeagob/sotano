# The Grudge: the design not yet built

Status: **proposal**.

Resentment grown old, something huge under the marsh, to never be hurt again. Only a long back of mud and stone shows above the water.

## What the design asks

- **Phase 1, Waits.** It surfaces where the player is not. Sulks (the marsh's swimmers) rise around it.
- **Phase 2, Erupts.** Bursts of mud cross the arena.
- **Phase 3, Surfaces.** It has to come up to breathe, and its core is in its back, open while it breathes.
- **Release.** A causeway across the marsh.

## What the build has

Rings of mud at seeded points, two bursts per attack in phase 2, a surfacing that moves it onto a point, and a charge in phase 3 (see [built: The Grudge](../../built/bosses/GRUDGE.md)).

## Open questions

- **Sulks.** They depend on swimmers, which are not built (see [movement](../encounters/movement/README.md)).
- **Core.** The core in its back needs the voxel body (see [Voxel bodies](VOXEL-BODIES.md)).
- **Breathing.** Is "surfacing to breathe" a time the player can rely on, and is it telegraphed?
