# The Last Thing (Tree floor L3)

Status: **idea**. The Tree is not built (milestone L5). This is a direction for the owner to design with the Tree.

The leaves mirror the Frozen Bottom. Their patterns are subtle: attachment, even to the light.

- **Mirror:** the Frozen Bottom (floor 9).
- **Patterns:** attachment, even to the light.
- **Boss (idea):** the Last Thing, what the player most wants to keep. It is released by letting go of it.

## Open questions

- **The design.** Nothing about the leaves is decided.
- **Letting go.** The release is an act the player does, not a hit. What the game asks the player to do, and how it reads without words, is open.
- **Mirror.** The Shadow's stillness is also a way of letting go. Decide whether the two should share a verb.
