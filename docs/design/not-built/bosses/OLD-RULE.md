# The Old Rule: the design not yet built

Status: **proposal**.

Dogma grown old: a belief from childhood, certainty. The arena is a tomb-temple of stone tablets, and the arena is the boss.

## What the design asks

- **Phase 1, Commands.** It holds two rules at once, as the Dogma's Fields: named rules, such as no jumping, no casting, no carrying, or no standing still.
- **Phase 2, Enforces.** Tablets fall from the ceiling where a rule is broken.
- **Phase 3, Crumbles.** The walls that carry the rules are breakable. Break the rules, literally.
- **Release.** Its tablets become a bridge out.

## What the build has

Two fields (mute and slow) and a column of force where the player stands, a block that falls on the player at the start of phase 2, and a charge in phase 3 (see [built: The Old Rule](../../built/bosses/OLD-RULE.md)).

## Open questions

- **The rules.** Named rules are text the player reads and a HUD message. They need translations in both languages. Which rules, and how many at once?
- **Tablets.** Falling tablets are a stronger picture than a falling block. Is the block a placeholder?
- **Breakable walls.** Breaking a wall that carries a rule is a new verb. Is it worth the cost in the first version?
