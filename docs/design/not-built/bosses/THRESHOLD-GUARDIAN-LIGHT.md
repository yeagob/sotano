# The Threshold Guardian: the route of light

Status: **decided** (the owner decided that both routes exist, 2026-10-27). The details below are the design's, and the separate hole is a proposal.

The Guardian is the gate at the edge of the great pit on floor 5. It lets the player pass only if they defeat it with positive intentions. Negative intentions turn it hostile. Both routes exist: this document is the route of light, the way through with good intentions.

## What the design asks

- **Phase 1, Bars.** It bars the pit. Its plates hold, and positive intentions soften them and mend the cracks, so no intention is wasted.
- **Phase 2, Tests.** It stops and asks the player to keep an intention positive for five seconds. A ring of light fills while they do. A negative intention resets the ring.
- **Phase 3, Steps aside.** Once mended, it steps out of the pit's way. Its blocks become the arch over the pit, and the player walks through. The arch is the way down.

## What the build has

The built Guardian is the shadow route only: it attacks from the start with bars, volleys and charges (see [built: The Threshold Guardian](../../built/bosses/THRESHOLD-GUARDIAN.md)). A good intention can convert it like any boss, which ends the fight without the Tests phase and without an arch.

## The shadow route's hole (proposal)

Released on the shadow route, the Guardian does not become an arch. The pit stays shut, and the way down is a hole dug beside it, a hidden pocket found by digging. The design marks this as a proposal. The build has one hole, the same as every floor.

## Both routes

Either route gives the boss practice (the second practice of the boss room) and the way down. Only the arch or the hole, and the codex entry, differ.

## Open questions

- **Test length.** Five seconds of an intention kept positive. Is it the right length, and does a negative intention reset the ring only, or the whole test?
- **The hole.** Is the hidden pocket found by digging worth building, given that digging is not a verb yet?
- **Conversion.** Should the build's positive conversion of the Guardian be replaced by the Tests phase, or kept as a shortcut?
- **Codex.** Which codex entry does each route write?
