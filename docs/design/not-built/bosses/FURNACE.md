# The Furnace: the design not yet built

Status: **proposal**.

Burnout grown old, to be enough: a great furnace of obsidian and brick, its door a mouth.

## What the design asks

- **Phase 1, Feeds.** It pulls loose cubes into its mouth and burns hotter, and fire spreads.
- **Phase 2, Blazes.** It throws burning debris across the arena.
- **Phase 3, Exhausted.** It has burned everything; it goes cold and cracks.
- **Release.** A cold hearth with one ember of the player's in it: a light.

## What the build has

A pull on the player, bolts aimed around the player during the blaze, a ring of fire from its own body, and fire on the room's floor (see [built: The Furnace](../../built/bosses/FURNACE.md)).

## Open questions

- **Feeding.** Does the pull draw loose cubes as well as the player, and what do the cubes become in its mouth?
- **Debris.** Burning debris is a lob with fire. The build's bolts are not lobs. Which family does the Furnace use?
- **Cooling.** The arena names the room `cool` in phase 3, and the room does not cool in the build. Decide whether fire leaves the room at exhaustion.
- **The ember.** The reward is a light the player keeps. Is it a practice, a block or a status?
