# The Shadow: the design not yet built

Status: **decided** for the keystone (floor 9 has the Shadow, owner 2026-10-27); **proposal** for its phases and its ending.

Everything you do not want to see in yourself: your own silhouette in the room's negative, with your hands and your jumper.

## What the design asks

- **Phase 1, Mirrors.** It does what the player does, a beat later, with the player's intentions, cold.
- **Phase 2, Reveals.** The arena's ice becomes mirrors, and the Shadow shows the projections the player released this run, as shadows.
- **Phase 3, Stops.** It stops fighting. The player walks to it and stands still for three seconds. Built as the ending.
- **Release.** It shrinks into the player's hands, which keep a thin dark ring at the wrist: the state "Shadow integrated" (art guide §9). The way into silence opens.

## What the build has

It copies the player's movement (not the intentions), summons three basic patterns in phase 2, stops in phase 3 and is defeated by stillness. Its release is the usual dissolve (see [built: The Shadow](../../built/bosses/SHADOW.md)).

## Open questions

- **Intentions.** Does the Shadow mirror intentions a beat later, or movement? The design says intentions; the build's mirror is movement.
- **Memory.** Shadows of the player's releases need the run to remember them, which the build does not store.
- **Integration.** The ring at the wrist is a state; it needs the art ruling and a place in the HUD or the body.
- **Silence.** The way into silence leads to the Silence below floor 9, which is not built. Until it is, the Shadow's defeat opens the usual hole.
