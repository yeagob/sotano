# The Shadow: the design not yet built

Status: **decided** for the keystone (floor 9 has the Shadow, owner 2026-10-27) and for the Mirror (issue #339, built: your health, your practices and bombs, a fight chosen from the moment, the stillness ending); **proposal** for its arena and its release.

Everything you do not want to see in yourself: your own silhouette in the room's negative, with your hands and your jumper.

## What the design asks

- **The Mirror.** It is your equal: your health at entry, every practice you hold, your moves and your bombs, no fixed pattern. Built: see [built: The Shadow](../../built/bosses/SHADOW.md).
- **The arena.** The frozen floor's ice becomes mirrors during the fight. The room itself is built: the biggest a floor holds, the fight started across it, cover all over it ([Arenas](../../built/bosses/ARENAS.md#the-shadows-room-built)). The mirrors are not: they would turn its ice patches (and only those) to a reflecting surface during the fight.
- **The stillness ending.** It stops fighting; the player stands still beside it for three seconds. Built, from a third of its health.
- **Release.** It shrinks into the player's hands, which keep a thin dark ring at the wrist: the state "Shadow integrated" (art guide §9). The way into silence opens.

The earlier proposal to summon, as shadows, the projections the player released this run was dropped by issue #339: a player does not summon projections, and the Mirror fights as a player.

## Open questions

- **Integration.** The ring at the wrist is a state; it needs the art ruling and a place in the HUD or the body.
- **Silence.** The way into silence leads to the Silence below floor 9, which is not built. Until it is, the Shadow's defeat opens the usual hole.
- **The conversion.** One heart of yours is 7 of its life, doubled after the first playtest (`LIFE_SCALE` 2, so 14), and your 3.5 damage is its 4. Confirm the double after more play, or name another factor.
- **The stillness ending from a third.** Built as a health threshold so it cannot end the fight at its start. Should it be open from the start?
- **Harmful practices.** They are copied (the mirror carries your burdens), as the issue decided; to confirm with the owner.
- **A word on entry.** Nothing tells the player that it took their practices; the copy shows in its shot. Add a toast at the start of the fight (both languages)?
- **Cold look.** Its bolts are the attack red of every hurting shot (DECISIONS §7.2), and its bombs are the player's own bomb cubes. Should the mirror carry a cold rim, under the art gate?
- **Its pace.** It fires at the player's own fire rate with no pause (the owner removed the limit after the first playtest, instead of adding dodging). Watch whether a fast build makes the fight unreadable.
- **A bigger room still.** The room is at the format's limit (two cells each way, 8 blocks high). A larger arena (three cells long, 43 blocks, or a 3 × 2 hall) needs the template format and the floor plan to allow three cells, and floor 9's plan to keep that much space free: a level decision for the owner, not taken.
- **The far start for other bosses.** Only the Shadow's room raises its boss at the farthest tile; every other boss still rises on a seeded tile at least 3.5 blocks away. Should other deep bosses get it?
- **Its cover under the owner's eye.** The materials and their spread are checked in numbers (contrast against the ashlar floor) but not yet seen in the game; the stone boulders sit closest to the floor in value (ΔL* 6.5) and may need a darker stone or a different place.
- **Boundaries** is classified (its shots deal nothing) but is not in the catalogue today.
