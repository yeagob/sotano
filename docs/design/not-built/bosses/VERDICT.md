# The Verdict: the design not yet built

Status: **proposal**.

The Tribunal grown old, a long bench of ledger stone with seven lecterns, to be beyond reproach. Its design says more than the build does.

## What the design asks

- **Phase 1, Accuses.** The seven lecterns fire in sequence, left to right, as line volleys across the room.
- **Phase 2, Sentences.** Its ✗ marks cover the floor, leaving paths the player can walk.
- **Phase 3, Recesses.** The bench turns. Its back, painted with a giant ✗, is open to hits.
- **Release.** A blank floor of ledger stone, warm once the player walks on it.

## What the build has

Three bolts from one body every two seconds, a few slow fields in phase 2, and a charge in phase 3 with no turn (see [built: The Verdict](../../built/bosses/VERDICT.md)).

## Open questions

- **The mark.** The ✗ is a projection's signature (the Lectern's). Should the Verdict use the same mark, and does the mark slow or only show?
- **The back.** The design's open back is a weak point. The Tribunal's backs are also vulnerable as projections; keep the two rules consistent.
- **Sequence.** Lecterns in sequence need seven bodies, or one body with seven emitters. Which?
