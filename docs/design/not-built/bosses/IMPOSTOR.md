# The Impostor: the design not yet built

Status: **proposal** (the design table does not mark the Impostor as a proposal; the owner should confirm it as decided).

The fear of being found out, a hollow frame of brass (never gold), smiling too wide, with a small real figure inside.

## What the design asks

- **Phase 1, Performs.** It imitates the bosses the player has met this run, in brass, each one's first phase.
- **Phase 2, Hides.** The frame fills with false figures; the player must find the real one.
- **Phase 3, Exposed.** The frame breaks; the small figure was the only real thing.
- **Release.** The empty frame stands as a doorway.

## What the build has

A charge, two random summons from the three basic projections, and a ring of force from its body (see [built: The Impostor](../../built/bosses/IMPOSTOR.md)).

## Open questions

- **Imitation.** Imitating bosses the player has met needs a record of the run. Is that the profile or the run? It is run state, so it should be kept with the run.
- **The real one.** How does the player tell the real figure? By intention, by the frame's reaction, or only by the break?
- **Brass.** The frame is brass, never gold. In the design, gold seams mark what was mended, so gold on the frame would mean repair. Keep the rule.
