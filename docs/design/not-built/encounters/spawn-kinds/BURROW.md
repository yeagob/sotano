# Burrow spawn

Status: **proposal** (design text, bestiary section 2.2).

A projection bursts out of the rock around the room, from a burrow. Cracks run along the wall like a ripple, then it bursts out.

## What the design asks

- **Rising.** Cracks run along the wall like a ripple, then the projection bursts out.
- **Sound.** A rumble travelling through the walls, moving in three dimensions.
- **Fits.** Rumination, the burrower.

## Open questions

- **Burrower.** The burrow needs the voxel burrower, which is not built (see [Burrower](../movement/BURROWER.md)).
- **Rock.** The rock around a room is part of the floor's structure. Decide which rock a burrow may use.
- **Fairness.** A rumble from the walls is the sound-first case; it must be heard before the burst.
