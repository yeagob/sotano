# Liquid spawn

Status: **proposal** (design text, bestiary section 2.2).

A projection rises from the surface of water or lava.

## What the design asks

- **Rising.** The surface rings outward, and the body rises, dripping.
- **Sound.** A bubbling swell, pitch rising.
- **Fits.** Swimmers (Undertow) from water, and fire-borne projections (Burnout) from lava.

## Open questions

- **Swimmers.** The swimmers' movement is not built (see [Swimmer](../movement/SWIMMER.md)). The liquid spawn is only useful with it, or with a rising walker.
- **Elements.** Which liquids a projection may rise from is a rule of the elements (ELEMENTS). The rule is not written yet.
- **Safety.** A projection that rises out of lava must not be placed next to the player.
