# Ceiling spawn

Status: **proposal** (design text, bestiary section 2.2).

A projection drops from a ceiling or a vault block. Motes drip down a thin cold thread, the body hangs, and then drops.

## What the design asks

- **Rising.** Descending drips, a thread of motes, the body hangs, then a soft thump on landing.
- **Sound.** The drips descend in pitch, which tells the ear the spawn is above.
- **Fits.** Flyers, droppers, and Gloom (a cloud that rains from the ceiling).

## Open questions

- **Ceilings.** Not every room has a ceiling block at a useful height. The templates decide where.
- **Gloom.** Gloom's design depends on this kind. Build the kind before the cloud.
- **Fairness.** A drop from above is the fairness rules' sound-first case, the descending drip.
