# Element source spawn

Status: **proposal** (design text, bestiary section 2.2).

A projection is born from a flame or a brazier. The flame turns cold white for the omen, and the projection comes out of it.

## What the design asks

- **Rising.** The flame turns cold-white for the omen; a breath is drawn through the fire; the projection comes out of it.
- **Sound.** A breath drawn through fire.
- **Fits.** Fire-zone projections, such as Burnout.

## Open questions

- **Sources.** Which flames and braziers are sources: any flame, or only those in a room's brazier? The elements design answers this.
- **Put out.** A source put out by the player may be an event trigger (see [Wave triggers](../WAVE-TRIGGERS.md)). Decide whether the source must burn for the projection to rise.
