# Wall spawn

Status: **proposal** (design text, bestiary section 2.2).

A projection steps out of a solid wall block between head height and the floor, never within three blocks of a door.

## What the design asks

- **Rising.** The wall block bulges and cracks in cold light; the projection steps out of it. The block becomes rubble, a loose cube.
- **Sound.** A stone groan, then a crack. The sound comes from the wall, positioned.
- **Fits.** Wall-mounted projections, leapers, and Avoidance (which hides in a block).

## Open questions

- **Templates.** The wall points need a legend in the room templates (`w`), which is not built.
- **Rubble.** The block becomes rubble the player can use. Decide whether that rubble is a loose cube or a block that the room keeps.
- **Fairness.** A wall spawn is the sound-first case of the fairness rules (see [Fairness rules](../FAIRNESS-RULES.md)).
