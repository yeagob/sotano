# Trial room: the pedestal practice

Status: **decided** (the owner approved trial rooms on 2026-10-27; the timings are the design's, and open).

A trial room has a marked door. Inside, a pedestal holds a practice that appears only after four to six waves, on timers. The player may leave before the first wave opens; once it opens, the doors slam.

## What the design asks

- **Marked door.** The trial room's door is marked, so the player knows what it is before entering.
- **Leave before the first wave.** Until the first wave opens, the player may walk out.
- **Slam.** Once the first wave opens, the doors slam, as in any fight.
- **Waves on timers.** Four to six waves, each on a timer, so stalling does not help.
- **Pedestal.** When the last wave is cleared, the practice appears on the pedestal.

## What the build has

A challenge room (see [built: Challenge rooms](../../built/encounters/CHALLENGE-ROOMS.md)) with three waves, a reward per wave and no pedestal. The waves are not on timers, and the door is not marked.

## Open questions

- **Waves.** Four to six in the design; three in the build. Which?
- **Timers.** The length of each wave's timer, and whether a timer runs even while the player is still in the first wave.
- **Practice.** Its quality and its place in the run's catalogue of practices.
