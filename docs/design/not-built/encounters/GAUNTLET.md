# The gauntlet corridor

Status: **proposal** (design text, bestiary section 2.4, special room types).

A corridor between the emanations of the Tree. There is no seal. Projections rise from the walls as the player passes. Each one is announced, and the player chooses whether to fight or to run.

## What the design asks

- **No seal.** The doors stay as they are.
- **Walls.** Projections rise from the walls as the player walks by, each one announced by an omen.
- **Choice.** The player can fight, or keep moving.

## What the build has

Nothing. The built challenge template is also called `gauntlet`, but it is a one-cell room with three waves (see [built: Challenge rooms](../../built/encounters/CHALLENGE-ROOMS.md)). Rename one of them before this is built.

## Open questions

- **The Tree.** The corridor is between the Tree's emanations, which are not built.
- **Wall spawns.** The corridor needs the wall spawn kind (see [Spawn kinds](spawn-kinds/README.md)).
- **Pace.** A corridor that spawns as you walk must never trap the player between two spawns. Decide a spacing rule.
