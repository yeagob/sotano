# Mounted

Status: **proposal** (design text, bestiary section 3.1).

A mounted being is fixed to a wall, a ceiling or a block, and does not move from it. Its attacks come from there.

## What the design asks

- **Fixed.** It does not move; navigation is none.
- **Pairs.** The Shoulds come in pairs in the walls, facing each other, and fire bolts across the room in a crossfire grid.
- **Freed.** Breaking the wall block around a mounted being frees it: it falls, slow and weak.

## What the build has

Nothing that is mounted. The Shoulds fire from their own position through an emitter. Avoidance hides in a shut state. Neither is fixed to a block, and neither is freed by breaking one.

## Open questions

- **Mounts.** The mount must be a block of the room, which the templates place. Decide which blocks may mount a being.
- **Freeing.** Breaking the mount is a verb. Confirm it does not break the room's wall in a way that opens a door.
- **Crossfire.** A pair of mounted shooters is a level design item, not a single projection. Check with the levels skill.
