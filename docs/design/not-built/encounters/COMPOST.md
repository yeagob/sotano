# The Compost

Status: **proposal** (design text, bestiary section 8.8; the owner asked for a farming room, and the name is a proposal).

Parts and agreements need a steady supply, and the game must not make that supply a grind. The Compost is the room where parts are made, in the open and with limits: what a pattern leaves goes back into the soil, and something grows from it.

## What the design asks

- **Where.** One Compost per zone, in a side emanation of the zone's floors, reachable from the main path and never behind a presence's sanctuary. Five rooms: zone I (floors 1 and 2), II (3 and 4), III (5 and 6), IV (7 and 8), V (9). Each draws only from its zone's lineage.
- **The plot.** A room of six to eight raised beds of the zone's material. Each bed is a spawn point. Two wall faces serve the wall-spawning projections.
- **Water and lava.** A water channel in zone II, lava in zone IV, none in I, III and V.
- **Seal.** It seals like any room, with the seal tone turned down to a soil rustle.
- **One harvest.** A visit is one harvest: six projections in two waves, announced by omens, with the usual fairness rules. Nothing is elite and no boss appears.
- **Both ways pay.** A release leaves parts. A conversion leaves an ally, which gives its small gift once per floor.
- **Refill at the door.** The beds refill when the player leaves and comes back. A visit is always a fresh harvest.
- **Three harvests per Compost per run.** The drop chance falls: 100 percent of the base table in the first, 75 in the second, 50 in the third.
- **Rest.** After the third harvest the beds rest: no spawns, the room stays calm, and each bed shows a closed cold sprout. No words are said.
- **Kept on death.** Parts already in the pack stay; a harvest cut short by death drops nothing new.

## Red lines

No timer on screen, no kill counter, no score, no reward for repetition past the limit, no dialogue in the room, and no part that the rest of the game cannot also give. The Compost is one source among others, never the only one for an agreement.

## Open questions

- **Parts.** Parts are built as pickups from released projections. The Compost is a source for them, so its rolls must use the same table.
- **Art.** The bed and the closed sprout need an art ruling ([the art guide](../../../art/ART-GUIDE.md)).
- **Build order.** The design builds it after the Gardener and once a zone's lineage exists.
