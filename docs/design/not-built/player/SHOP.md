# A shop for what you carry

A shop where the player trades what they carry for other things, so that items and parts have a purpose.

Status: idea

Part of [the player (not built)](README.md).

## The design

The owner has a pending task on this, recorded on 2026-10-28: "items and parts need a purpose, and many have none yet. A shop where they can be traded for other things would give them one." ([DESIGN.md, section 13, question 8](../README.md)).

The design also says, in its section on consumables, "No coins, no shop" ([DESIGN.md, section 4](README.md)). That line and the pending task contradict each other, and this document does not decide between them.

The design also says that only a carried thing can be handed to someone, which is the part of the trade that touches the hand ([built/player/HANDS.md](../../built/player/HANDS.md)).

## Open questions

1. **Shop or no shop.** The section on consumables says no shop, and the pending task asks for one. The owner should choose.
2. **Where the shop is.** A room type, a vendor, a floor, or somewhere else?
3. **What trades for what.** The design does not say. Parts for keys? Practices for blocks? Something else?
4. **Currency.** Do keys and bombs count as currency, as the design asks? Keys and bombs are counters, not carried things ([built/player/HANDS.md](../../built/player/HANDS.md)).
