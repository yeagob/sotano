# Movement abilities beyond walking and jumping

Abilities that change how the player moves: a second jump, flight, and a sprint. The Triple Jump's jumps are built and moved to [built/player/TRIPLE-JUMP.md](../../built/player/TRIPLE-JUMP.md).

Status: mixed. The **Triple Jump's gift** is decided: it is the reward for overcoming the fifth tree deity ([HORSE](../sky/guardians/HORSE.md)), and that gift is not built. Flight and sprint remain ideas.

Part of [the player (not built)](README.md).

## The design

The design gives the player walk and jump, with no sprint at first, and says that some items could grant flight, a double jump, and similar abilities ([DESIGN.md, section 4](README.md)). The built movement is in [built/player/MOVEMENT.md](../../built/player/MOVEMENT.md).

## What exists in design

- **The Triple Jump's gift (Status: decided, not built)**:
  - Granted by an item awarded upon overcoming the fifth god of the tree ([HORSE](../sky/guardians/HORSE.md)). The two air jumps, their heights and the item are built: see [TRIPLE-JUMP.md](../../built/player/TRIPLE-JUMP.md).
  - Purpose: Enables long-distance, high-altitude platforming across tiny stepping stones in [Totality](../levels/TOTALITY.md), ascending into space toward the Sun ([Unity](../levels/UNITY.md)).
- **A second jump in the air (idea)**: Lower-tier double jump prior to the tree.
- **Flight from an item (idea)**.
- **A sprint (idea)**: A faster walk, if the owner wants one.

## Open questions

1. **Item identity**: What is the lore name, appearance, and physical nature of the artifact/item granted by the Horse deity? The working name "Triple Jump" is provisional, and the owner has not named it.
2. **When sprint comes**: The design says no sprint at first. Does the owner mean sprint is out for good, or later?
3. **Flight and the dungeon**: Flight would change how blocks are dug and how the climb is planned ([CLIMBING.md](CLIMBING.md)). Should flight be a way up, or only a way across?
