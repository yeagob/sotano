# A second chance at death

Up: [the player (not built)](README.md)

Status: partly built. The second chance is built as [Waymark](../../built/practices/will/WAYMARK.md): a practice that cancels one death per copy and returns the player at full hearts, at the checkpoint they last knew. Its cost, its restore and its source are decided there. The questions that remain open are in [practices/SECOND-CHANCE.md](../practices/SECOND-CHANCE.md), the one document for this idea.
