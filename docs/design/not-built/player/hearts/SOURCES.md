# Hearts from stations

Stations that could give hearts of each kind, instead of only red health.

Status: idea

Part of [kinds of heart (not built)](README.md).

## The design

Today every kind of heart comes from the hearts the game drops, rolled from the floor's chances ([built/player/hearts](../../../built/player/hearts/README.md)). The kitchen cures only half a heart or one heart; the owner wants it to give these kinds of heart later ([not-built/practices/HEART-KINDS.md](../../practices/HEART-KINDS.md)).

## Open questions

1. Which station gives which kind, and at what cost?
2. Does a station's heart roll its kind like a drop, or does the recipe fix it?
