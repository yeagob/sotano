# A heart that explodes when lost

A kind of heart, not built, with an effect when it is lost: the design's example is a heart that explodes and breaks the blocks near it.

Status: proposal

Part of [kinds of heart (not built)](README.md).

## The design

The design gives three kinds of heart as the initial set ([DESIGN.md, section 4](../README.md)): red hearts, soul hearts lost first, and a third kind with an effect when it is lost. Red hearts and the soul hearts (as the blue heart) are built ([built/player/hearts](../../../built/player/hearts/README.md)). The third kind has its own name and its own art.

## Open questions

1. **Name and art.** The design asks for its own name and art and does not give either. They follow the art and naming rules and need the owner's word first.
2. **The effect on loss.** Is an explosion that breaks nearby blocks the effect the owner wants, or another one?
3. **Does the stone or berserker heart already cover it?** Both are built extra hearts with their own rule; the owner may want this one in addition, or not.
