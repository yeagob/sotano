# Soul hearts and a third heart

Two kinds of heart the design plans beside the red heart the game has today.

Status: proposal

Part of [the player (not built)](README.md).

## The design

The design gives three kinds of heart as the initial set ([DESIGN.md, section 4](README.md)):

- **Red hearts**: recoverable. These are the hearts the game has today ([built/player/HEALTH.md](../../built/player/HEALTH.md)).
- **Soul hearts**: temporary, and lost first, before the red ones.
- **A third kind**: with an effect when it is lost. The design's example is a heart that explodes and breaks the blocks near it. It has its own name and its own art.

## Open questions

1. **Name and art of the third heart.** The design asks for its own name and art and does not give either. The art and the name follow the art and naming rules and need the owner's word first.
2. **The effect on loss.** The design's example is an explosion that breaks nearby blocks. Is that the effect the owner wants, or another one?
3. **Where soul hearts come from.** The design does not say whether soul hearts are a pickup, a reward or a practice's effect.
