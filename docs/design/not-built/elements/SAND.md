# Sand

Up: [not-built elements](README.md) · Status: proposal.

Sand is a loose granular material. Nothing in the build is sand: the design adds it so that water, mud, furnaces and lightning can change it.

## What it would do

- **Falls.** A sand cube with nothing under it falls like a gravity block (Minecraft-style) and becomes a loose cube until it lands.
- **Becomes mud when wet.** Water next to sand turns it to mud over time (see [MUD](MUD.md) and [DIRT-TO-MUD](DIRT-TO-MUD.md)).
- **Smelts to glass.** A furnace turns sand into glass (see [GLASS](GLASS.md) and [FURNACE](FURNACE.md)).
- **Turns to glass by lightning.** A charge through sand makes glass (see [ELECTRICITY](ELECTRICITY.md)).

## What it reacts with

Water (mud), a furnace (glass), electricity (glass).

## Open questions

1. Where does sand come from in the rooms: templates only, or from breaking stone?
2. Is sand a solid cube (the player carries it) or a loose material that spills? The design says it falls as a cube.
3. Does sand fall through water, or sink in it?
