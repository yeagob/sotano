# Grass burning

Up: [not-built elements](README.md) · Status: proposal.

The design table says grass burns away to dirt. The build does not: grass is not flammable, so a fire next to it leaves it as grass.

## What it would do

- **Burns to dirt.** Fire next to grass turns it into dirt over a few seconds, the way a crate burns away.
- **Gives the surface a fire.** On the surface, a grass field could burn back to dirt.

## What it reacts with

Fire (burns it), lava (burns it, if lava reaches grass).

## Open questions

1. Does grass burn in the dungeon, where the only grass is the roots' surface, or only on the surface?
2. Does burnt grass leave dirt at once or after a few seconds?
3. Does a burning grass cell spread fire to the neighbouring grass?
