# Deepstone melting in lava

Up: [not-built elements](README.md) · Status: idea.

The design's materials table says deepstone melts in lava, slowly. The build has deepstone (tier 2, 25 points of hardness, the old fear) and no reaction for it: lava does not change it. The table of reactions has only the two rows of water (see [REACTIONS](../../built/elements/REACTIONS.md)).

## What it would do

- **Melts slowly.** A deepstone cube in lava takes time to melt, then becomes something softer or nothing.
- **Shows the depth.** The deep rock of the lower floors is the rock most likely to melt, so a lava room in the deep changes its own walls.

## What it reacts with

Lava (melts it). Possibly a furnace, which the design gives a deepstone smelt.

## Open questions

1. What does deepstone become: nothing, stone, or a new material?
2. How slow is slow, and does it change the validator's rules for lava beside deepstone?
3. Would melting deepstone open doors or break walls of the rooms that are built from it?
