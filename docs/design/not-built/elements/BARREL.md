# Burning barrel

Up: [not-built elements](README.md) · Status: proposal.

A burning barrel stands for letting go: a block thrown in is gone, like a letter burned. It is also a fire source the player can use on purpose.

## What it would do

- **Always lit.** A template places it, lit. It is a fire source that does not decay.
- **Destroys what is thrown in.** Any loose cube thrown, kicked or dropped into it is removed for good. A flammable cube flares up first.
- **Can be knocked over.** An explosion can tip it, spilling its fire onto the floor.

## What it reacts with

Any loose cube (removes it), flammable cubes (flare), explosions (tip it), and the fire it spills (spreads like any fire, see [FIRE](../../built/elements/FIRE.md)).

## Open questions

1. Does the barrel destroy a solid block that the player breaks and carries, only loose cubes, or every cube it touches?
2. Is there a limit (one cube at a time, a cooldown)?
3. Does the barrel count as a validator fire source, so no flame may sit beside it on the door approaches?
4. The design's "Letting go" meaning is in the story; the name and tone are not decided here.
