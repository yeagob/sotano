# Water: finite volume and breath

Up: [not-built elements](README.md) · Status: proposal, with open questions.

Two questions about water are open. The first is whether water is finite: the design recommends it, the build keeps sources forever. The second is whether losing breath and hearts underwater is too punishing for the metaphor. The build has breath and drowning; the owner has not settled the question.

## Finite water (proposal)

- **Finite.** Water in a flooded pocket drains down into the floors below when the player digs into it. Digging matters, and water does not refill forever.
- **Why.** The design says it is cheaper to simulate and makes digging matter.
- **Build today.** Sources never change: a pool is endless until its walls are broken, and a flow recedes only when its source is cut off. There is no volume to drain.

## Breath and drowning (open)

- **Built.** Eight seconds of breath with the head under water, then half a heart every 1.5 seconds. Breath refills four times faster at the surface. See [WATER](../../built/elements/WATER.md).
- **Question.** Is losing hearts underwater too punishing for the metaphor? The design recommends yes to drowning, but slow, with a clear meter. The build does that. The owner has not confirmed it.
- **Dwellers.** The design named Overwhelm, a projection that lurks in deep water and pulls you down. The build's water dwellers are Sulk and Undertow; Overwhelm is not built (the bestiary decides its shape).

## Open questions

1. Finite or endless water? The recommendation is finite.
2. If finite: does water drain to the floor below, and how much is lost per dig?
3. Is drowning at half a heart every 1.5 seconds right for the metaphor, or should it be slower?
4. Is Overwhelm a water projection for the deep floors, or is Undertow its build?
