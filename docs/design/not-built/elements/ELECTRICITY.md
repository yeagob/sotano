# Electricity

Up: [not-built elements](README.md) · Status: proposal.

Electricity stands for insight and sudden energy: a flash that runs through everything conductive at once. The build has no electricity. The spark enemy draws a line arc and does not conduct; the tags on some items named "electric" do not mean a charge in this sense.

## What it would do

- **Sources.** A future item (an insight intention), an electric projection, or a charged block placed in some rooms.
- **Runs at once.** A charge runs through connected conductive blocks (iron, iron ore, water, wet mud) up to a limit, all in one step.
- **Hurts what it touches.** Anything in the charged cells is stunned and takes damage.
- **Changes what it touches.** It turns sand into glass and lights flammable blocks.
- **Powers.** Later: furnaces, doors and mechanisms.

## What it reacts with

Water (spreads through the whole body of water), iron and iron ore (conductors), mud (weakly), sand (glass), flammable blocks (fire), furnaces (power).

## Open questions

1. Does electricity run through water on the whole floor, or only through the connected body of water in the room? The design says the whole body.
2. Is the stun a state the player can read, and how long does it last?
3. Which projection is electric? The design named Spark (an electric skitterer that charges metal). The build's Spark draws a line arc with no conduction, so the two differ.
4. Does an item called electric (a practice) make a charge, or only a line arc like the projection?
