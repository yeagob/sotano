# Mud

Up: [not-built elements](README.md) · Status: proposal.

Mud stands for feeling stuck: earth and emotion mixed. It slows you, swallows kicks and does not ricochet thrown intentions. Heat dries it back into something solid.

## What it would do

- **Slows.** Walking in mud is about 40 percent slower, a stronger effect than water's wading.
- **Swallows kicks.** A kicked cube in mud loses most of its speed; a thrown intention sinks in and stops instead of bouncing.
- **Dries.** Fire or a furnace next to mud dries it into brick.
- **Is made.** Water on dirt or sand makes mud over time (see [DIRT-TO-MUD](DIRT-TO-MUD.md) and [SAND](SAND.md)).
- **Conducts weakly.** Mud carries an electric charge a little (see [ELECTRICITY](ELECTRICITY.md)).

## What it reacts with

Water (makes mud), fire and lava (dries to brick), a furnace (dries to brick), sand (is made from it with water).

## Open questions

1. Is mud an element like water, with levels and flow, or a solid material that water turns into? The design table lists it as a material; the verbs read like an element.
2. How deep is the slowdown in a room where the player stands in mud for a long time?
3. Does mud sit in the room templates as a fixed floor, or only come from water and dirt in play?
4. Which projection lives in it? The design named Stagnation (mud that slows on touch); its behaviour is in the bestiary, not decided here.
