# Dirt turning to mud when wet

Up: [not-built elements](README.md) · Status: idea.

The design table says dirt becomes mud when water touches it, and that dirt and sand do so over time. Nothing in the build changes dirt when water arrives: dirt stays dirt beside water.

## What it would do

- **A slow change.** Dirt in contact with water becomes mud after a few seconds, so a flood or a dug pool slowly turns the earth around it.
- **A shape of the room.** A pool dug into dirt would turn its walls into mud, which changes how the room is walked.

## What it reacts with

Water (becomes mud). Sand too (see [SAND](SAND.md)). Mud (see [MUD](MUD.md)).

## Open questions

1. Is the change permanent or does mud dry back when the water goes?
2. Does a room template with dirt pockets beside a pool count as a built-in mud room, and does the validator need a new rule for it?
3. Does a player-dug dirt wall that floods become mud at once or over the run?
