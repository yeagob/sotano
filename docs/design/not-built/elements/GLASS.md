# Glass

Up: [not-built elements](README.md) · Status: proposal.

Glass is the refined form of sand: clear, fragile, and made by heat or lightning. The owner's brief names it as a refined matter for furnaces; the design says it shatters rather than holding a cube.

## What it would do

- **Shatters.** Glass breaks when it is hit hard, and it leaves no cube.
- **Is made.** A furnace turns sand into glass; a charge through sand makes it too.

## What it reacts with

Sand (is made from it), a furnace, electricity.

## Open questions

1. Does glass count as a block for the player to carry, or is it only a result that shatters? The design table lists it as a material with no cube.
2. How much force shatters it: the same as a bomb, or any kick?
3. Is glass a tier and hardness like the other blocks, or a special case in the damage rules?
