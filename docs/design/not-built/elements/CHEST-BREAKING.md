# Breaking chests

Up: [not-built elements](README.md) · Status: idea, open.

The old design text says a chest is a block and can be broken, and that breaking one spills everything inside. Another line of the same text says chests are unbreakable blocks, and the build is unbreakable. The owner has not chosen. This document keeps the question open and does not decide it.

## The two readings

- **Breakable.** A chest can be broken by a bomb, a blade, a kick or a beam. Breaking it spills everything it holds, as pickups, and the chest is gone. That would be the one exception to "breaking blocks drops nothing".
- **Unbreakable.** The build: a chest is tier and hardness infinite. Nothing digs, burns or blasts it; only keys, or walking up, open it.

## What would change

- A breakable chest could be opened with a bomb or a blade instead of keys, so the key economy would depend on the answer.
- Chests in the rooms would become targets for the player's damage tools, not only for keys.

## Open questions

1. Breakable or unbreakable? The build says unbreakable; the earlier text says both.
2. If breakable, does breaking a locked chest spill its contents as well as lose the keys?
3. Does a chest ever break without spilling (a mimic)?
