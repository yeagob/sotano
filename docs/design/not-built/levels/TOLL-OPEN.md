# The toll on the fifth floor

Up: [not-built levels](README.md) · Status: open.

The way down from each floor has a toll (see [TOLL](../../built/levels/floors/TOLL.md)). The build asks floors 0 to 4 for things from the hand, keys, bombs and hearts, and floors 5 to 8 for practices. The owner's text puts the first practice on the sixth level, which the build reads as floor 5. A sentence elsewhere in the same text says "on the fifth, one practice", which would move it to floor 4.

## The two readings

- **Build.** Floor 5 (the sixth level) asks one practice. Floor 4 (the fifth level) asks five things and no practice. Practices start on the sixth level.
- **The owner's sentence.** Practices start on the fifth level, so floor 4 asks one practice in place of five things. The text does not say what floors 5 and below would then ask.
- **No practice on the fifth floor.** Floor 5 asks something other than a practice, and nothing is named for it. Neither the text nor the build does this; it is listed only as a possibility.

## What would change

- Under the owner's sentence, the way down from floor 4 asks one practice in place of five things, which changes the economy of the middle floors.
