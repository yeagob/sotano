# The tree's core: what is still open

Up: [not-built levels](README.md) · Status: proposal, open (issue #328).

Issue #328 asks for the giant tree's interior to be redesigned: its spatial structure, its route, its goals and its place in the progression. Issue #317 (the broken trunk stairs) collided with it, because the gaps are the route's obstacles. The two were reconciled and the shared part is built; this document holds only what #328 still leaves open.

## What is built (the shared minimum)

In [TRUNK](../../built/levels/giant-tree/TRUNK.md): the interior is the four trunk stories as they were (roots, rings, galleries, hollows); the route is one climb, each story's spiral stair in order; each story's goal is its broken stair, crossed by building, a gap one step bigger than the story below. It is met in every run on the way to the crown. No room, projection or boss was added. The branches above are their own story ([BRANCHES](../../built/levels/giant-tree/BRANCHES.md)).

## Why this minimum

- The owner asked for big gaps on every trunk story (#317) and for an interior with a route and goals (#328); the gaps give each story a goal without a new space.
- The four stories are what the zone names, the book, the achievements and the completion count refer to (`tree.roots` … `tree.hollows`); keeping them keeps all of that true.
- Nothing the owner did not ask for: no new rooms, no enemies, no bosses (the tree holds no projections yet).

## Open questions for the owner

1. What is "el Núcleo": the whole trunk interior (as built now), or a new space at the heart of the trunk?
2. Does a story ask anything besides its gap (a fight, a puzzle, a reward, a piece of the story), and does it give anything when crossed?
3. Should the climb change with progress (mastery, the path completion of #193): smaller gaps on later runs, or an opened way once the crown has been reached?
4. Should the gaps' sizes stay as built (five to eight steps) after playtesting?
5. Should the tree ever hold projections of its own, and on which story?
