# The statue (east)

The east cardinal walk leads here ([CARDINAL-WALK](CARDINAL-WALK.md)). The destination is the statue of this area's Joel-Clown statue, inspired by Silksong. It is a secret room, so it holds a challenge, fixed unique items and its own achievement, like every secret room ([README](README.md#shared-rules)).

## What is decided

- **The destination.** The statue, from the owner's answer of 2026-11-26.
- **The reference.** Silksong inspires the statue. This is a wink at a game: it is paraphrased, never a name, a character, an asset or an exact line ([SOURCES](../../../../lore/SOURCES.md), `silksong`). The statue must still mean something in the story ([NAMING](../../../built/story/NAMING.md), "Where a wink lives").
- **Not defined here.** The challenge, the items and the achievement. The content is issue #334's, as the export asks.
