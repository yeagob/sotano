# The cardinal walk

Walk straight from the surface north, south, east or west, and keep walking for long enough. At the end of the walk stands an element of the Outer Void, one for each direction. Reaching it opens a portal onto a destination. These four secret rooms are reached this way, and no other way.

## The walk

- **Where it starts.** Any point of the surface: the plain of the [built surface](../../../built/levels/SURFACE.md), or the far plain past its passes. The walk does not start from a fixed place.
- **Where it ends.** At the cardinal element of the Outer Void in that direction ([OUTER-VOID](../extra-zones/OUTER-VOID.md)). The surface has nothing at its cardinal points, and the four doorways of the trunk face the same four sides ([TRUNK](../../../built/levels/giant-tree/TRUNK.md)).
- **What counts.** Reaching the element. The walk's straightness is not checked, so no tolerance is needed. Whether the element can be reached by any route, or only by walking straight, is open.
- **What it needs.** Only the walk. No state of being is needed for the four cardinal rooms. The state-of-being triggers belong to the other rooms ([README](README.md)).

## The four destinations (decided)

| Direction | Destination | Kind | Document |
|---|---|---|---|
| North | An ice zone. Its design is not yet defined | Zone | [NORTH-ICE](../extra-zones/NORTH-ICE.md) |
| East | The statue, the area's Joel-Clown statue (issue #334), inspired by Silksong | Secret room | [EAST-STATUE](EAST-STATUE.md) |
| South | The desert of the worms (issue #354) | Zone | [SOUTH-DESERT](../extra-zones/SOUTH-DESERT.md) |
| West | A temple in the style of Muslim temples: the labyrinth (issue #335, Maze 3D) | Secret room | [WEST-LABYRINTH](WEST-LABYRINTH.md) |

The export lists these four among the secret rooms, so each one carries fixed unique items and its own achievement ([README](README.md#shared-rules)). The north and the south are zones, so their form is still open.

## The portal (open)

The portal is the entrance from the element to its destination. Its design is not defined:

- Its look: a visible object, a door, or a hole in the world (question 4 of issue #331).
- Whether it works one way or both ways.
- Whether the element is the portal itself, or the portal stands beside it.

## Open

1. **The distance.** How far each element stands from the surface. It is shared with the Outer Void's design (issue #330), and it decides how long "long enough" is.
2. **A sign on the walk.** Does the walk show a sign the player can read before the element, such as a line of stones, a sound or a light? A secret must be found by a sign the player can read ([BURROWING](../../creatures/BURROWING.md)).
3. **The mirror in the depth.** Each place in the surface can mirror a place in the depth ([COSMOLOGY](../../../../lore/COSMOLOGY.md) §1). The four cardinal rooms have no mirror decided yet.
