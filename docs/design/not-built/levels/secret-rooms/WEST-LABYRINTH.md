# The labyrinth temple (west)

The west cardinal walk leads here ([CARDINAL-WALK](CARDINAL-WALK.md)). The destination is a temple in the style of Muslim temples. It refers to the sacredness of the west and to the journey to Mecca. It is a metaphorical representation of Mecca, and it is the labyrinth of issue #335 (Maze 3D). In the owner's words, Mecca is a maze.

## What is decided

- **The destination and its meaning.** The temple refers to the sacred west and the journey to Mecca. It is a metaphor, not a copy of a place.
- **The room is the labyrinth.** The temple and the maze are one room. The maze's design is issue #335's.
- **Respect.** The art and the text treat the sacred place with respect ([sotano-lore](../../../../../.claude/skills/sotano-lore/SKILL.md) §3). The reference is recorded as `islamic-pilgrimage` in [SOURCES](../../../../lore/SOURCES.md).
- The room is a secret room, so it holds fixed unique items and its own achievement, like every secret room ([README](README.md#shared-rules)).

## Proposed limits (for the owner's approval)

These limits make the respect concrete. They are a proposal, and the owner decides them.

- No likeness of a real building: not the Kaaba, not a real mosque, not a real city.
- No Qur'anic text, no calligraphy copied from a real source, and no religious figure.
- The architecture follows a generic grammar of temple and labyrinth, not a copy of one place.
- The text speaks of the journey. No rite is performed or mocked, and no one in the temple is an enemy.
- A projection in the labyrinth is a pattern of the journey, never a person or a tradition ([sotano-projections](../../../../../.claude/skills/sotano-projections/SKILL.md)).
