# Snake 3D

Snake 3D is a secret room with a challenge (issue #333). It was among the first candidates for the north, east and west cardinal rooms, and the owner gave those three places to other rooms: the ice zone, the statue and the labyrinth. So Snake 3D has no cardinal place.

## What is known

- **Not cardinal.** It is not on the walk ([CARDINAL-WALK](CARDINAL-WALK.md)).
- **The rules.** Fixed unique items, its own achievement and a challenge with a reward, as every secret room ([README](README.md#shared-rules)).
- **The content.** Its own issue, #333.

## Open

1. **The location.** Where it lies, and on which floor or beside which place.
2. **The way in.** A state of being, an action, or digging. Question 1 of [README](README.md#open-questions) applies.
3. **The name.** "Snake 3D" is a working name, a game genre, and it passes [sotano-lore](../../../../lore/SOURCES.md).
