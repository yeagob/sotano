# Boss Rush

Boss Rush is a secret room that is not one of the four cardinal rooms. The owner's answer of 2026-11-26 leaves its location undefined. The issue's analysis reaches it by digging, as the rooms without a cardinal walk are reached.

## What is known

- **Not cardinal.** It is not on the walk and not one of the four destinations ([CARDINAL-WALK](CARDINAL-WALK.md)).
- **The way in.** Digging, as recorded in the analysis of issue #331. The trigger is not defined.
- **The content.** Its own issue, #332. This document holds only its place among the secret rooms.
- **The rules.** Fixed unique items, its own achievement and a challenge with a reward, as every secret room ([README](README.md#shared-rules)).

## Open

1. **The location.** Where it lies: in the rock, below which floor, or beside which place.
2. **The digging trigger.** What the digging must reach: a depth, a block, or a hollow in the rock. Digging is the way in, so the trigger must be one the player can read ([BURROWING](../../creatures/BURROWING.md)).
3. **The challenge's form.** Whether the boss rush belongs to the challenge rooms already built ([CHALLENGE](../../../built/encounters/CHALLENGE-ROOMS.md)) or is new.
