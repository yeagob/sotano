# Unity: the seventh and final level going up (The Sun)

Up: [not-built levels](README.md) · [Totality](TOTALITY.md) · [The realm of the sky](../sky/README.md) · [ENDGAME](../story/ENDGAME.md)

Status: decided for the setting (occurs in the Sun), its role as the final destination of the upward climb, and granting the solar relic needed to confront the Spiked Entity at the bottom of the world. Proposal for the space layout, visual manifestation, and final interaction.

## 1. What Unity is

Unity is the seventh and ultimate level of the upward journey, reached at the apex of [Totality](TOTALITY.md). It takes place entirely within and upon **the Sun**.

If the bottom of the basement is the deep, dark Silence of the unconscious ([COSMOLOGY](../../../lore/COSMOLOGY.md)), Unity in the Sun is its celestial counterpart: absolute illumination, undivided radiance, and the completion of the cycle.

## 2. Setting and atmosphere

- **The Solar Core**: A realm of boundless golden and white light. The blackness of space and the physical matter of the Earth have vanished.
- **Form and geometry**: Physical architecture gives way to pure geometric light, solar flares that form stable walking paths, and concentric rings of illumination.
- **Soundscape**: Deep, warm, resonant harmonic hums replacing all previous mechanical and environmental audio. Silence rendered as pure resonance.

## 3. Experience and reward (Status: decided)

- **No conventional enemies or hindrances**: In accordance with the sky laws, there is no violence or negative intention here. The journey of struggle ends at the gateway of the Sun.
- **The Culmination**: The player stands at the center of the world's origin and end.
- **The Solar Relic Reward**: Conquering Unity bestows a celestial relic/artifact of pure solar power. This item is the only force capable of piercing the invulnerability of the **Spiked Entity** waiting at the bottom of the depths ([ENDGAME](../story/ENDGAME.md)).
- **The Return**: Armed with the solar relic, the player can return to the abyss to face the spiked guardian and complete the ultimate reconciliation between the heavens and the depths.

3. **Dialogue / Presences**: Does any deity or guardian appear in Unity, or is the player completely alone in oneness?
