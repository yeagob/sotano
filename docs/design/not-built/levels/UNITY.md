Up: [not-built levels](README.md) · the tree's missing story, to be defined.

Status: idea, to be defined by the owner. Nothing about this story is decided, built or specified yet.

## What is known

- The giant tree is meant to have two stories that are not defined yet: unity and unity. The built tree has seven stories (see [the giant tree](../../built/levels/giant-tree/README.md)).
- This story is the other of the two. Its place in the tree, its meaning and what the player does in it are open.

## What has to be decided

- Where it sits in the tree: above the built stories, between them, or in the sky (see [the upper maps](FLOOR-KINDS.md) and [the constellations](TREE-CONSTELLATIONS.md)).
- Whether it is a walkable story like the built ones, or a room map.
- What the player meets there, and whether it relates to the five guardians of the sky (see [guardians](../sky/guardians/README.md)).
- Its name and its meaning. Names follow the naming rules in [story](../story/README.md) once the owner has defined it.
