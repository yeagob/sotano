# The Outer Void

Past the far plain there is no ground yet ([SURFACE](../../../built/levels/SURFACE.md)). The Outer Void is what lies past that edge: the endless terrain of issue #330. This document keeps what the secret rooms need of it. The rest is issue #330's.

## What the secret rooms need

- **A unique element in each direction.** One cardinal element stands north, one south, one east and one west, each at a distance from the surface. The export places them in issue #330, and the cardinal walk ends at them ([CARDINAL-WALK](../secret-rooms/CARDINAL-WALK.md)).
- **One portal each.** Each element opens its portal onto one destination: north the ice zone ([NORTH-ICE](NORTH-ICE.md)), south the desert ([SOUTH-DESERT](SOUTH-DESERT.md)), east the statue ([EAST-STATUE](../secret-rooms/EAST-STATUE.md)) and west the labyrinth ([WEST-LABYRINTH](../secret-rooms/WEST-LABYRINTH.md)).
- **Unique.** Each element is the only one of its kind, and its direction is its identity.

## What the build has beside it

- The grass of the surface goes out to 304 blocks. The ring of mountains rises at about 488 blocks. Past the far plain there is no ground ([SURFACE](../../../built/levels/SURFACE.md)).
- The surface is closed to digging beyond the old ground ([WORLD-STORAGE](../WORLD-STORAGE.md), decision 8).

## Open

1. **The distance.** How far each element stands from the surface. It sets how long the walk is, and it is shared with the walk's design ([CARDINAL-WALK](../secret-rooms/CARDINAL-WALK.md)).
2. **The look of the void.** Its ground, its sky and its fog.
3. **One world or several.** Whether the zones lie inside it (see [README](README.md#open)).
4. **The mirror in the depth.** Whether the void mirrors a place below ([COSMOLOGY](../../../../lore/COSMOLOGY.md) §1).
5. **The name.** "Outer Void" passes [sotano-lore](../../../../lore/SOURCES.md). The totality document already names "the Void of space" ([TOTALITY](../TOTALITY.md)), so the two names must be told apart before either is used in the game.
