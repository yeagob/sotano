# The ice zone (north)

The north cardinal walk ends at its element in the Outer Void, and the portal there opens onto an ice zone ([CARDINAL-WALK](../secret-rooms/CARDINAL-WALK.md)). The owner set the destination. Its design is not defined yet.

## What is known

- **The destination.** An ice zone, from the owner's answer of 2026-11-26.
- **The rules of a secret room.** The export lists the four cardinal destinations among the secret rooms. So the ice zone carries fixed unique items and its own achievement ([secret rooms, shared rules](../secret-rooms/README.md#shared-rules)).
- **The ice of the frozen ninth floor.** The built ninth floor is frozen: every pool is ice on its surface, and the ice is solid, breakable and slippery ([CIRCLES](../../../built/levels/floors/CIRCLES.md), the frozen ninth).

## Open

1. **The whole design.** Its shape, its challenge, its items and its achievement. None is defined.
2. **The ice.** Whether the zone uses the same ice as the ninth floor, or its own.
3. **The place.** Whether it lies inside the Outer Void or beyond it ([README](README.md#open)).
