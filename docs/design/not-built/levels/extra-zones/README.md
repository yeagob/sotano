# Extra zones

The built surface ends its ground at the far plain: past the passes, the plain behind the mountains has no ground yet ([SURFACE](../../../built/levels/SURFACE.md)). The extra zones are the places beyond that edge, which the cardinal walk and its portals reach. Nothing here is built, and the surface stays closed to digging beyond the old ground ([WORLD-STORAGE](../WORLD-STORAGE.md), decision 8).

## Shared rules

- **Reached by a walk or a portal.** A zone is reached by the cardinal walk and its portal ([CARDINAL-WALK](../secret-rooms/CARDINAL-WALK.md)). It is not a floor of the dungeon.
- **Secret rooms in form.** The four cardinal destinations carry fixed unique items and an achievement, as every secret room does ([secret rooms, shared rules](../secret-rooms/README.md#shared-rules)).
- **The world's grammar.** A zone uses the blocks and the element verbs of the built world ([sotano-levels](../../../../../.claude/skills/sotano-levels/SKILL.md)). Its art passes [sotano-art](../../../../../.claude/skills/sotano-art/SKILL.md).
- **Names.** Zone names pass [sotano-lore](../../../../lore/SOURCES.md) before they reach the game.
