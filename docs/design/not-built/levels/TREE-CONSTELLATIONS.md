# Constellations: the leaves or the heaven zone

Up: [not-built levels](README.md) · Status: proposal, open question.

The design puts the constellations in the leaves: a star map where the stars are rooms, teleport doors link them, and the views of the cosmos open around them. The build puts the constellations in the heaven zone, above the canopy, as a chain of star pads. The two readings are both in the design; the owner has not chosen where they belong.

## The two readings

- **Leaves (design).** The leaves are the third upper floor, a star map of constellations. Each star is a room, the teleport doors link the stars, and the views of the cosmos are in the room. The canopy (the leaves zone) is where the stars are.
- **Heaven (build).** The constellations are three groups of star pads, rising above the canopy in the heaven zone, chained into one ring. Stepping on a pad carries you to the next. The canopy has no stars.

## What would change

- If the stars move into the leaves: the canopy becomes a map of rooms, and the heaven zone keeps only the night and the top of the sky.
- If the stars stay in heaven: the leaves stay a walkable crown, and the design's "teleport doors" become the pads.
- If both: the canopy holds the constellations' rooms, and heaven holds the top of the sky, the cosmos view.

## Open questions

1. Where do the constellations belong: in the leaves (the design), in heaven (the build), or both?
2. Are the star pads teleport doors (the build, one chain) or doors between rooms (the design)?
3. Does a constellation hold rooms with projections, or is it only a view of the cosmos?
4. Does the chain of pads keep one ring, or split into one ring per constellation?
