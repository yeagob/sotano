# The climb from the roots to the surface

Up: [not-built levels](README.md) · Status: decided (owner, 2026-10-27), not built.

There is no house on the surface. The player starts in the roots, below the surface, and must climb to it "however you can": by building blocks up through the dirt, by the tree itself, or by any other means the level allows. The owner decided this; the build has no way up.

## What the build does

- The roots are floor 0, where the run starts. Above them lies dirt, then the grass at the surface.
- The roots' rooms join each other by doors, and no door or stair leads up. A player who wants to rise must dig or build through the dirt above the roots.
- The surface has no house, no stair and no shaft to the roots.

## What the owner decided

- No house stands on the surface.
- The player climbs from the roots, however they can: blocks, the tree itself, or another way.

## Open questions

1. What is the first climb? A stair of blocks through the dirt, a shaft the player digs, or a path made of the tree's roots?
2. Does the climb need any practice or key, or is it free like the roots' own rooms?
3. Is the climb's first stretch part of the tutorial (a lesson on the walls), or left to the player to discover?
4. Does the surface have anything to do besides the tree and the sky?
5. Revisited floors on the way up: stay empty, or repopulate with tougher enemies? (Recommendation: partial repopulation; it adds tension to the climb.)

## Revisited floors (proposal)

Status: proposal, not built. The design asks for floor streaming: only the current floor and its neighbours are kept loaded. What the player broke is remembered: if you come back up, your holes are still there. In the build each floor replaces the last ([SCOPE-AND-ROADMAP](../SCOPE-AND-ROADMAP.md), item 6).

The climb back up passes through the floors already visited, so these rules decide what the climb meets. The open question is number 5 above.
