# The world beyond the map

Status: proposal, with two superseded parts marked below. This is the layer that is not on any map: hidden pockets in the rock, side biomes, and the knowledge that opens them. The official dungeon is the visible part; around it there is more.

## Hidden pockets in the rock (proposal)

Small caves with an item or an altar, with no hint on any map. You find them by digging, or because an item or a sound hints at them. The item that would show them is a proposal: the mole's eye, which sees the outline of hollows and rooms through rock ([EARLY-SKETCHES](../practices/EARLY-SKETCHES.md)). The Rootlings are the creatures that dig toward them ([BURROWING](../creatures/BURROWING.md)).

## Sideways: other biomes (proposal)

Dig far enough horizontally and the rock changes material, and **other biomes** appear: a crystal cave, a flooded zone, a sunken city. Higher risk, higher reward. The scenery of the caves is the cover-art proposal in [CAVERN-SCENERY](CAVERN-SCENERY.md).

## Persistent knowledge, not persistent power (proposal)

Between runs you don't get stronger; you learn. You unlock new items in the pools (as in Isaac) and discover recipes, like the alchemy of Noita: for example, placing a certain block on a certain altar while holding a certain item. Some recipes change with the run seed, so they can't simply be copied from a wiki. The design's versions: [MEMORY](../presences/MEMORY.md) (what dwellers remember across runs, knowledge only) and [RECIPE-MODEL](../practices/potions/RECIPE-MODEL.md).

## Superseded: below floor 4

The first brief put a sealed layer of tier-3 material below floor 4, to be opened by a combination of items or a ritual. The design now has ten floors and the silence below them ([SILENCE](../../built/levels/floors/SILENCE.md)). The sealed layer is not in the design.

## Superseded: a house on the surface

The first brief put a house in a clearing on the surface, where the protagonist comes from, with doors and cellars that only make sense once you know what is below. The owner decided on 2026-10-27 that there is no house on the surface: the player climbs from the roots however they can ([ROUTE-FROM-ROOTS](ROUTE-FROM-ROOTS.md)).

## The surface and the next layer (open)

Reaching the surface ends the "normal" run and opens the game's next layer. What that layer is stays deliberately open. The sky above the surface is the realm of the sky ([not-built/sky](../sky/README.md)); the built surface is in [built/levels](../../built/levels/README.md). The owner's open question is what ends a run and where the way from silence to heaven is: see [ENDING-AND-RETURN](ENDING-AND-RETURN.md).

## Secret rooms (proposal)

A secret room has no door. It is reached by breaking a wall, or bombing it, as in Isaac, but here it is also reached **by digging from anywhere**. Breaking a room's wall to get in or out is expensive for the room's own walls: see [ROOM-WALLS](../blocks/ROOM-WALLS.md). Secret rooms are not built: the built room types are in [built/levels/rooms](../../built/levels/rooms/README.md).

## Special doors (proposal)

Besides the doors that are built (open, sealed while you fight, locked by a key, and blown apart by a bomb: [built/levels/rooms/DOORS](../../built/levels/rooms/DOORS.md)), the design has one special door per floor that asks for something strange. Reinforced doors are in [ROOM-WALLS](../blocks/ROOM-WALLS.md).
