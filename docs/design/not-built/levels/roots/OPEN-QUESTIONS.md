# Open questions for the owner

Up: [the Roots: what is still open](README.md) · Status: open. Only what the owner's decisions on issue #147 (and the corrections of 2026-11-12) do not settle. Each question has the default that is built, so no work waits on it. Questions 19 to 25 concern the easy layout, built in C3 and D1.

Answered by the owner on 2026-11-12 and removed: level 0 gives exactly one key or one bomb; "five times lower" is the chance a crate gives anything; a converted boss has its own achievement, separate from destroying it; step lines speak in stars (`qualityStars`).

1. **Lesson wall or "south for plates".** Taken literally, plates on every south wall would put the lesson behind a forward walker in six rooms. *Default:* each room's lesson hangs on the wall a forward walker faces; plates go on the other wall ([PANELS §7](../../../built/levels/roots/PANELS.md#7-which-wall-holds-the-lesson)).
2. **After the last page.** The issue says wrap to page 1; the owner said "never beyond the current page". *Default:* the current page stops at the last page and moves on when the owner adds pages; browsing still reaches every page ([PAGES §1](../../../built/levels/roots/PAGES.md#1-the-current-page)).
3. **The page stone.** "One block in the start room" does not say which, nor whether it shows the current or the shown page. *Default:* the middle block of the start room's north wall at eye height, showing the shown page's number on book pages ([PAGES §4](../../../built/levels/roots/PAGES.md#4-the-page-stone)).
4. **Which run endings count.** *Default:* death, R, "New run" and a new seed; not the sandbox, not closing the tab; at most once per run.
5. **Panels hidden by their room.** In `roots-step` and `roots-seal` part of each panel is behind the room's own walls today. *Default:* raise the step-room panels 1.1 blocks and shrink the seal-room panels to fit their strips; keep the dig-room north panel as a secret behind its wall ([PANELS §6](../../../built/levels/roots/PANELS.md#6-where-each-panel-hangs)). This holds in the teaching layout (page 1) only; the easy layout hangs every panel at the standard mount ([EASY-LAYOUT §5](../../../built/levels/roots/EASY-LAYOUT.md#5-where-the-panels-hang-in-the-easy-layout)).
6. **The notice.** *Default:* a blocking card (the presence card with an "Achievement" kicker), shown at the next calm moment, rather than a toast that vanishes ([ACHIEVEMENTS §4](../../../built/progression/ACHIEVEMENTS.md#4-the-notice)).
7. **What counts as a completed meditation.** *Default:* the Sanctuary's `complete` outcome from a Boundary (the Roots or a vault); the quick reward and the debug console do not count.
8. **When mastery applies.** *Default:* from the next run; the notice says "Next time you wake in the Roots".
9. **A player-facing reset.** *Default:* none in #147; a console command forgets the page and achievements and keeps the staircase ([SAVE](../../../built/progression/SAVE.md)).
10. **Seeds change once on floor 0.** The Roots' new loot and forks change floor 0 for every existing seed code (`GEN_VERSION` + 1); floors 1 to 9 replay exactly as before, because `pedestalSlots` no longer needs to change ([REWARDS §6](../../../built/levels/roots/REWARDS.md#6-rules-the-table-keeps)). *Default:* accept.
11. **"Knowledge, not power".** The older meta-progression idea says runs make you wiser, not stronger. Mastery makes the Roots richer. *Default:* the owner's decision wins; the idea is read as "no stat or practice is kept", which still holds.
12. **The quality of the extra practices.** The owner fixed when they appear (levels 10 and 25), not their tier. *Default:* ★ to ★★★, the Roots' gentle band; the altar reaches ★★★★ from level 90.
13. **The Shadow's stillness ending.** *Default:* it counts as turning the Shadow to your side (`boss.shadow.converted`), not as destroying it ([ACHIEVEMENTS §1](../../../built/progression/ACHIEVEMENTS.md#1-the-40-that-ship-with-147)).
14. **Issue #179's count.** With two achievements per boss and four for pages of the book, 40 ship now and 60 remain, not 78. *Default:* the issue is updated to 60.
15. **The names and the three book pages.** Proposals in both languages, except the owner's "Lucky Number" and "Nice" ([ACHIEVEMENTS](../../../built/progression/ACHIEVEMENTS.md), [HIDDEN-BOOK](HIDDEN-BOOK.md)).
16. **Quoting a modern book at length.** Page 7 quotes about seventy words of *La locura lo cura* (1995), a work still under copyright. *Default:* show it as a quotation with full attribution (author, work, publisher, year), its source in `docs/lore/SOURCES.md`; the owner confirms the length is acceptable, or asks the publisher.
17. **Where the end of a page is.** *Default:* the treasure room's north panel (`roots-gift` → `north`), the plate wall of the last room before the forward ring closes; on book pages it replaces page 1's Sanctuary panel while the quote is shown ([BOOK-DATA §2b](../../../built/levels/roots/BOOK-DATA.md#2b-quotations)).
18. **Page achievements beyond the book.** Pages 54, 69 and 100 cannot be earned until the book has that many pages, so level 40 is out of reach today. *Default:* keep them in the catalogue; they count once the pages exist.
