# Open questions for the owner

Up: [the Roots as an evolving tutorial](README.md) · Status: open. Only what the owner's decisions on issue #147 do not settle. Each question has the default the design uses, so no work waits on it.

1. **Lesson wall or "south for plates".** Taken literally, plates on every south wall would put the lesson behind a forward walker in six rooms. *Default:* each room's lesson hangs on the wall a forward walker faces; plates go on the other wall ([PANELS §7](PANELS.md#7-which-wall-holds-the-lesson)).
2. **After the last page.** The issue says wrap to page 1; the owner said "never beyond the current page". *Default:* the current page stops at the last page and moves on when the owner adds pages; browsing still reaches every page ([PAGES §1](PAGES.md#1-the-current-page)).
3. **The page stone.** "One block in the start room" does not say which, nor whether it shows the current or the shown page. *Default:* the middle block of the start room's north wall at eye height, showing the shown page's number on book pages ([PAGES §4](PAGES.md#4-the-page-stone)).
4. **Which run endings count.** *Default:* death, R, "New run" and a new seed; not the sandbox, not closing the tab; at most once per run.
5. **Panels hidden by their room.** In `roots-step` and `roots-seal` part of each panel is behind the room's own walls today. *Default:* raise the step-room panels 1.1 blocks and shrink the seal-room panels to fit their strips; keep the dig-room north panel as a secret behind its wall ([PANELS §6](PANELS.md#6-where-each-panel-hangs)).
6. **Level 0: "at most one bomb or one key".** *Default:* exactly one, key or bomb, chosen by the seed.
7. **"Crates whose chance to hold an item is five times lower".** Crates never hold practices. *Default:* the chance a crate gives anything at all is five times lower (0.02 to 0.06).
8. **The notice.** *Default:* a blocking card (the presence card with an "Achievement" kicker), shown at the next calm moment, rather than a toast that vanishes ([ACHIEVEMENTS §4](../../progression/ACHIEVEMENTS.md#4-the-notice)).
9. **What counts as a completed meditation.** *Default:* the Sanctuary's `complete` outcome from a Boundary (the Roots or a vault); the quick reward and the debug console do not count.
10. **When mastery applies.** *Default:* from the next run; the notice says "Next time you wake in the Roots".
11. **A player-facing reset.** *Default:* none in #147; a console command forgets the page and achievements and keeps the staircase.
12. **Seeds change once.** Taking the Roots' two pedestal slots out of the run-start reservation keeps floors 1 to 9 identical at every mastery level, but changes them once for every existing seed code (`GEN_VERSION` + 1). *Default:* accept the one-time change.
13. **"Knowledge, not power".** The older meta-progression idea says runs make you wiser, not stronger. Mastery makes the Roots richer. *Default:* the owner's decision wins; the idea is read as "no stat or practice is kept", which still holds.
14. **A converted boss.** *Default:* it counts as overcome, as `bossDown` already treats it.
15. **Quality numbers in the step lines.** Players do not see practice quality anywhere else. *Default:* the lines say "quality up to 2 of 4"; the owner may prefer words.
16. **The 22 names and the three book pages.** Proposals in both languages ([ACHIEVEMENTS](../../progression/ACHIEVEMENTS.md), [HIDDEN-BOOK](HIDDEN-BOOK.md)).
