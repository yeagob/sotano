# Cosmology: as above, so below

Status: **lore and direction**, not built. The owner's brief (translated): *the surface and the depth represent the Hermetic idea "as above, so below"; at some deep, far point they should be connected, in the endgame.*

## 1. The shape of the world

The dungeon goes **down** into the basement of the mind: ten floors from the roots to the frozen bottom, then silence. Above the surface there is no house (owner, 2026-10-27): a giant tree rises into the sky (§4b, [LEVELS.md](../design/not-built/levels/ENDING-AND-RETURN.md)). *§1's table below predates that decision; the mirror now runs between the depth and the tree.*

The Hermetic principle of correspondence ("that which is below is like that which is above", *The Emerald Tablet*) says these are not two places but one, seen from two sides. So the world is built as a **mirror**:

| Depth (inner) | Surface (outer) |
|---|---|
| Floor 1: the cellar of habits | The ground floor: daily life |
| Floor 2: the rooms of feeling | The bedrooms: intimacy, family |
| Floor 3: the old structures (beliefs, family systems) | The study and the library: what you were taught |
| Floor 4: the oldest pain and the strongest patterns | The attic: what's stored and forgotten |
| Below bedrock: the source | Above the roof: the sky |

Each floor of the depth has a counterpart above, and what you do in one should echo in the other (a room you clear below opens a window above; a practice found below changes something in the house). The details are for the surface layer of the game; the principle guides every place we add.

## 2. The seven principles as systems

The seven Hermetic principles (as summarised in *The Kybalion*, 1908) map onto systems the game already has or plans:

| Principle | Meaning | System |
|---|---|---|
| **Mentalism** | "The all is mind" | Intentions shape the world: everything is breakable by will |
| **Correspondence** | As above, so below | Depth and surface mirror each other; the endgame connects them |
| **Vibration** | Everything moves | Electricity, sound, the pulse of projections ([ELEMENTS](../design/not-built/elements/ELECTRICITY.md)) |
| **Polarity** | Opposites are degrees of one thing | Fire and water; lava and water making obsidian; harm and healing in the same item |
| **Rhythm** | Everything flows out and in | Descent and return; runs that begin again, carrying knowledge |
| **Cause and effect** | Every cause has its effect | Events, synergies, chain reactions |
| **Gender** (generation) | Creating needs two complementary forces | Breaking and building: destroying blocks and placing them to make something new |

## 3. The endgame connection

At the deepest, farthest point, the depth and the surface meet. Direction, to be designed with the owner:

- **Below the bedrock** is sealed (tier ∞). The only way through is a practice or ritual built from what you learned on the way (for example, an *Emerald Tablet* practice, or a combination of practices from all four paths).
- Breaking through the bottom **does not lead further down: it opens onto the sky above the house**. You fall up, or fall through and land on the roof. The deepest point *is* the highest one.
- This is also *The Alchemist*'s ending: the treasure was where you started. The house you came from is changed by what you found below.
- The loop is the point: integration is not escaping the depth but bringing it to the surface, and seeing the surface with what the depth taught you.

## 4. Other maps of the same world

Three traditions describe the same shape from other angles. This is direction, not built; each mapping waits for the owner before it becomes content.

### The Tree of Life (Kabbalah)

The Tree describes light descending through ten emanations, from the crown (Keter) to the kingdom (Malkuth, the physical world), and the soul climbing back up along 22 paths. That is a run: down through the depth, back up to the surface and the sky. Below and above are one tree, which agrees with correspondence (§1).

**Owner's decision:** the Tree is the **literal map of the generated world**. The generator lays places out as emanations and connects them by paths; the exact mapping (which emanation is which floor or place, how the 22 paths become connections) is to be designed with the three-party review before it is built.

Isaac Luria's teaching of the **shattered vessels** fits the core loop closely: light too strong for its vessels broke them, and sparks fell and were hidden in husks. **Repair** (tikkun) is gathering them and rebuilding, and it mends the world, not only the person. In The Roots the blocks that intentions break are the vessels, the material you carry is the light they held, and building with it is repair. **Owner's decision:** in the game these pieces are called **shards** (a nod to the shards of the Cosmere, each carrying an intent, see [SOURCES](SOURCES.md)). This gives the game's ambition a name: a game that transforms the world.

Respect rule (from the [`sotano-lore`](../../.claude/skills/sotano-lore/SKILL.md) skill): Kabbalah is a living Jewish tradition. We use its structure (tree, emanations, vessels, sparks, repair), never the sacred names of God, and never turn its figures into projections.

### The Book of Changes (I Ching)

The I Ching reads any moment as one of 64 hexagrams of six lines, built from eight trigrams. Lines that change turn one hexagram into another: nothing stays still.

- **The run as a moment.** **Owner's decision:** the run seed is shown and read as a hexagram: same seed, same reading. Changing lines could hint at how the run turns.
- **The eight forces as elements** ([ELEMENTS](../design/not-built/elements/ELECTRICITY.md)):

| Trigram | Force | Element |
|---|---|---|
| Heaven | Creative, strong | The sky above the surface |
| Earth | Receptive, yielding | Blocks, the ground you build on |
| Thunder | Arousing | Electricity |
| Water (the abyss) | Danger, depth | Water |
| Mountain | Keeping still | Stone, bedrock |
| Wind / wood | Gentle, penetrating | Wood, growing blocks |
| Fire | Clinging, clarity | Fire, lava |
| Lake | Joy, openness | Mud, marsh |

### The Kybalion, further

Beyond the seven principles (§2), two practical teachings suggest mechanics: **mental transmutation** (change a state by moving it along its own pole, fear toward courage, rather than fighting it) and **neutralising the pendulum** (rise above the swing of rhythm instead of being carried by it). The first suits practices that turn a state into its opposite; the second suits rooms or projections with a rhythm that rewards timing over reaction.

## 4b. The thirteen floors (owner, 2026-10-27)

The owner set the world's shape: ten floors down and a giant tree of three floors up. Floors 0–5 are laid out as the Tree of Life's ten emanations (bigger, more themed and more complex as you descend); 5–9 are random maps; the tree above holds the trunk (a vertical map), the branches (a 3D map) and the leaves (a star map of constellations with teleport doors and views of the cosmos). Down is a descent through the layers of the unconscious to the bottom of Dante's inferno, and then silence; up is heaven. Owner decisions the same day: floor 0 is the earth and the roots (peaceful creatures, no house above: you climb from the roots); floors 1–9 are Dante's nine circles in order, the ninth frozen; floor 5 ends in a great pit to the next layer; silence connects back to heaven, cyclically (this replaces §3's "the bottom opens onto the sky above the house": there is no house, and the loop is silence ↔ heaven). Design: [LEVELS.md](../design/not-built/levels/ENDING-AND-RETURN.md). Respect rule for Dante: his structure and images, never his figures as projections, no punishment of people.
